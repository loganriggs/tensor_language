# A functional atlas of a bilinear transformer: a category engine, an induction circuit, and the attention that needs an MLP

*Draft, 2026-07-29. Companion interactive figures: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f27aeab4-438f-465a-9a33-aba8272b43ee*

## Abstract

We give a component-level functional map of a 546M-parameter softmax-free bilinear transformer
(bilin18) and test its generality across four bilinear-MLP models spanning three attention families
and two depths. Three results. (1) **Compositional decomposition:** every attention layer 2–17 can
be reconstructed from a growing symbol basis built only of preceding layers, beating both a
per-token memorization table and a random-basis null at all sixteen layers, with cost that *declines*
with depth; the bilinear MLP decomposes too but far more lossily. (2) **A functional atlas resolves
the model into three families:** a battery of next-token tasks collapses — five "category" tasks
(subword continuation, punctuation, capitalization, digit, function-word) share one near-identical,
90–96%-MLP-driven machinery in the early stack MLP 0–3 that *literally builds a linearly-decodable
next-token-category code* (probe accuracy 0.53→0.61 across MLP 0–3, causally collapsing to 0.51 when
those layers are ablated); induction is a distinct attention mechanism; layout/newline is a small,
MLP-suppressed head circuit. (3) **Generality and mechanism:** the category-engine / induction-attention
dichotomy is architecture-general, but bilin18's shared MLP-1 hub and its newline outlier are specific
to its two-branch attention (a fourth, 18-layer softmax model dissociates like the shallow ones). The
mechanism: previous-token information is universal (built by layer-0 attention in every model), but
bilin18's two-branch product pattern needs MLP 1 to make *both* branches co-fire at the copy source —
ablating MLP 1 collapses its induction-match rate by 85% and inverts the task, while softmax models
compute the match without MLP 1.

## 1. Model and method

bilin18 = `gpt2-bilinear-sqrd-attn-18l-9h-1152embd`: 18 layers, 9 heads, d_model 1152, no softmax.
Attention pattern is the unnormalized product of two bilinear forms, (q1·k1)(q2·k2)/d², causal; the
MLP is bilinear, Down(L·x ⊙ R·x). Base cross-entropy on held-out FineWeb is 3.076 nats. All
interventions are mean-ablations (replace a component's output by its dataset mean); all audits are
held out.

## 2. Compositional decomposition of every attention layer (§2 scripts: qk_l217_symbolgen, qk_l26_mlp, qk_l26_causal)

Each attention layer L's query/key input is regenerated by a ridge map from a **symbol basis of
preceding layers only**: 96 embedding principal components, the layer-0 named archetype activations,
and a per-head 16-dimensional PCA of each attention layer 1..L−1. Replacing the layer's pattern with
the symbol-generated one and re-running the whole model, the cost (ΔCE vs 3.076) beats both baselines
at all sixteen layers and declines with depth: layer 2 +0.0176, layer 5 +0.0298, layer 12 +0.0027,
layer 15 +0.0022, layer 17 +0.0097 (table +0.04–0.32, random +0.02–0.05 throughout). Deeper patterns
are *more* expressible in named terms — "sharpening is grounding."

The bilinear MLP decomposes from the same symbols but at roughly six times the attention cost with a
modest named-vs-random margin: it reads residual content (prior MLP writes, token carriage) the
attention-symbol basis cannot express. The causal stack — replacing all of layers 2–6's patterns at
once, each reading the already-substituted stream below — holds at +0.231 versus a memorization
baseline of +1.136 (five times better). Symbol-driving induction specifically retains only 64% of the
task; a previous-token embedding plus rank-64 basis recovers 81%, leaving ~19% irreducibly non-linear
(the exact match-and-copy is not a low-rank readout of preceding attention).

## 3. A functional atlas: three families (§3 scripts: qk_circuit_atlas, qk_induction_minimal, qk_subword/punct/newline_circuit, qk_circuit_null)

Backward-elimination on a task-specific cross-entropy-reduction metric yields minimal sufficient
circuits (≈90% retention) that vastly exceed same-size random-subset nulls (induction 90.7% vs 3.6%
random). Widening to a seven-task battery and scoring each of the 180 components' knockout importance
per task, the tasks **collapse into three families**:

- **Category prediction** — subword, punctuation, capitalization, digit, function-word. Component-
  importance profiles are near-identical (pairwise correlation 0.98–0.999), 90–96% MLP-driven,
  concentrated in the early stack MLP 0–3. One shared "predict the next token's category" machinery.
- **Induction** — 28% head mass in bilin18, a match-and-copy attention fabric on top of MLP 1; the
  nominal induction head h5.5 is individually dispensable (97% survives its removal).
- **Layout / newline** — the outlier (importance correlation 0.36–0.51 with everything). Its minimal
  circuit is twelve heads and the category MLP stack *interferes* with it (removing MLPs improves
  newline). Task magnitude is small (0.199 nats).

This corrects a coarser three-circuit reading: subword and punctuation are not separate circuits but
one MLP family whose minimal-circuit heads are near-passengers (importance mass 96% and 91% MLP).

## 4. The category engine is literal (§4 script: qk_category_engine)

A linear probe reading the next token's category (six-way) from the residual climbs from 0.527 at the
embedding to 0.611 right after MLP 0–3, then to 0.679 by mid-network (majority baseline 0.437).
Causally, mean-ablating MLP 0–3 collapses the blk-4 probe back to 0.510 — the raw-embedding level. The
early MLP stack *builds* the category code the five category tasks read.

## 5. The MLP-1 hub is a multiplexer, not a shared computation (§5 scripts: qk_mlp1_hub, qk_mlp1_pc0)

MLP 1 is the dominant knockout in every family (it inverts induction; +8.5 on subword; +3.1 on
punctuation). But its output is high-rank (top-8 directions = 25% of variance), each task reads a
distinct 16–32-dimensional slice (important-direction overlap 0.14–0.23), and the single shared
direction, PC 0, is a content-versus-structure axis (high for lexical content and mid-word fragments,
strongly negative for punctuation and clause boundaries). MLP 1 is a dense enrichment bus that
different circuits tap differently.

## 6. Generality and the two-branch mechanism (§6 scripts: qk_atlas_bilin12/bilinsm12/swiglu18, qk_prevtoken_source, qk_mlp1_role)

The atlas was run on four bilinear-MLP transformers: two-branch unnormalized (bilin18), single-branch
normalized squared (bilin12), and softmax (bilinsm12 at 12 layers, swiglu18 at 18). **Architecture-
general:** the early MLP stack builds the category code, and induction is a separate attention
mechanism — in the three non-two-branch models induction is 87–100% head mass and *anti*-correlates
with the category family. **bilin18-specific:** the MLP-1 shared hub and the newline outlier; the
fourth model (swiglu18, 18-layer softmax) shows induction at 100% head mass, so these are a property
of bilin18's two-branch attention, not its depth.

Mechanism. Previous-token information (what induction matches on) is universal: a linear probe reads
token[j−1] from the residual, built by layer-0 attention in every model (ablating attention-0
collapses it 0.27→0.04; MLP 0 does not build it — it dilutes it). What MLP 1 does is feed the
two-branch *match*: ablating it collapses bilin18's induction-match rate — the fraction of second-copy
queries whose top-attended key is the correct copy source — by 85% (0.0355→0.0054) and inverts the
task, whereas in both softmax models the match is untouched and induction improves. The two-branch
product needs MLP 1 to make both branches co-fire at the copy source; a single softmax bilinear form
reads the match directly. In the softmax models MLP 1's category content mildly interferes with
induction, mirroring the newline-interference effect.

## 7. Honest limitations

- Audits use held-out FineWeb at 128–512 tokens; the model is competent to ~512.
- The symbol decomposition is faithful for *average* next-token behaviour, not mechanism-exact for
  sharp token-matching (the ~19% induction residual).
- The minimal-circuit retention metric degrades when a task's magnitude is tiny (newline: 0.199 nats),
  where pruning can overshoot 100%.
- bilin18 differs from the 12-layer models in *both* attention family and depth; the 18-layer softmax
  control (swiglu18) is what attributes its anomalies to attention rather than depth.
- swiglu18 carries a swiglu MLP rather than a strictly bilinear one; the atlas is MLP-internals-agnostic
  (it measures MLP-vs-head importance), so this does not affect the head/MLP conclusions, but its
  category-code internals were not probed.

## 8. Take-away

Across four architectures, a bilinear transformer divides next-token labour on an MLP-versus-attention
axis: the early MLP stack is a category-prediction engine writing a decodable next-token-category code;
induction is an attention copy mechanism. The one model that couples them — routing induction through
an MLP hub — does so because its two-branch product attention needs the MLP to align both branches at
the copy source. The coupling is an attention-design artifact, not a general feature of the
computation.
