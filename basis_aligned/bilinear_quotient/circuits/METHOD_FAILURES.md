# Method-failure ledger

This ledger records inference rules that have already failed. Applying a failed rule to a new tensor
is a replication or stress test unless the proposal adds a task-conditioned measurement capable of
changing the conclusion.

## `method.unsupervised_energy_or_variance_as_circuit_basis`

Aliases: activation energy, output variance, principal-component basis, top singular directions,
energy recovery, magnitude ranking, low-rank discovery.

### Established evidence

- Section 617: output principal components captured a compact subspace but did not align with
  functional token classes; supervision found a different rotation inside that space.
- Section 836: apparent recovery by a class-and-position subspace was matched by a shuffled-label
  subspace of the same rank, showing that the headline was mostly capacity rather than meaning.
- Section 1056: a bottom band containing only 0.8% of activation variance remained more causally
  important than a matched random band.
- Sections 2822–2826: the same warning replicated for recent removal-effect tensors; an in-sample
  energy fit did not transport causal answer effect, while a task-defined answer-minus-competitor
  direction captured much more causal effect per unit energy.

### Allowed uses

Variance, energy, reconstruction error, and rank may measure storage, provide a capacity-matched
control, or engineer a circuit already identified by task and intervention evidence.

### Disallowed inference

High energy, explained variance, or low reconstruction error alone does not establish that a
direction is semantic, causal, reusable, necessary, sufficient, or selectively manipulable. A new
discovery claim needs a task-conditioned causal endpoint, a matched-capacity null, held-out testing,
and a statement of which circuit computation the direction predicts.
