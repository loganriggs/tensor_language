# Novel capitalization and register

## CURRENT tier: 2

A late committee is a surgical pooled-capitalization removal handle, but copied names
dominate its strongest effect and no novel-only upstream mechanism is known.

## Behavior and tensor program

Endpoint: capital-initial targets without an earlier identical antecedent, excluding
registered sentence/newline starts.  Controls are copied capitalized, sentence-initial
capitalized, and lowercase frequency matches.

Fixed tensor form: shared late-crew QK/OV HOSVD or paired-product basis.  Router: a
pre-outcome native-state selector must separate register service from antecedent-copy;
the semantic `novel` label may score but may not route the live program.  Extraction
retains only the novel residual; removal leaves copied-entity service live.

## Evidence

- [`capitalized_committee12.py`](../../capitalized_committee12.py): pooled all-12 damage
  `0.6031` target/`0.0403` elsewhere.
- [`capitalized_removal_greedy.py`](../../capitalized_removal_greedy.py): no small

- [`capitalized_committee_grain.py`](../../capitalized_committee_grain.py): QK/OV split.
- [`circuit_capcopy_results.json`](../../circuit_capcopy_results.json): copied/novel
  separation that prevents pooled promotion.

## Terminal gates

OOD holds out proper nouns, acronyms, title case, sentence starts, and BPE forms.  The
novel target lower bound must remain positive after copied targets are excluded;
default extraction/collateral gates apply.

Shared-owner caveat: pooled capitalization is largely copied-entity service and cannot
promote this entry.
