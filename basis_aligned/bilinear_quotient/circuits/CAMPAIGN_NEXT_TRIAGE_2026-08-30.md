# Post-induction/successor campaign triage — 2026-08-30

Status: outcome-blind planning note.  No circuit-specific terminal receipt exists for
the candidates below.  The cited bilinear-quotient scripts are discovery evidence;
their rows, adaptive rankings, hooks, and result files are not terminal authority.

## Decision rule and ranking

The ranking favors the shortest path to a source-closed, zero-native-call fixed tensor
that can be extracted and removed on fresh document-disjoint roles.  A tokenizer or
parser may define score masks, but may not route an intervention.  TopK selection,
nearest-match lookup, decoded labels, and target masks are forbidden in an executable
arm.  Native continuous QK scores, bilinear MLP gates, fixed head projectors, RoPE, and
fixed causal masks are allowed and must be priced.

| Rank | circuit | current tier | chance of a quick Tier-4/5-quality terminal result | principal risk |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | matched bracket closure | 4 | highest | exact algebra is dense; nested assay is underpowered |
| 2 | newline boundary | 4 | medium-high for certification, low for new compression | five-head owner; known compression is not behavior-specific |
| 3 | article choice | 3 | medium | rank-16 write is promising, but the trigger null failed and the front chain is not composed |

“Quick” here means a promotive extraction/removal/OOD certificate or a decisive
registered negative.  Dense identity replay can certify an executable mechanism but
does not by itself establish simplicity or move a stored-removal ledger.

## 1. Matched bracket closure

### Why first

[`bracket_match_results.json`](../bracket_match_results.json) reports L13H8 deletion
`+0.8254` nat on 84 closer targets versus `+0.00376` global; deleting the true match
edge costs `+0.6890`, while nearest-incompatible/random/previous controls cost
`+0.0136/+0.0035/+0.0742`.  The physical writer expansion in
[`bracket_pointer_pairs_results.json`](../bracket_pointer_pairs_results.json) replays
input and score to `1.42e-7/4.67e-7` relative error.  This is the strongest single-head,
selective endpoint remaining.

The negative evidence is useful rather than disqualifying: the top ten of 625 writer
pairs carry only `0.1425` of match-cell absolute mass and differ from the distractor
top ten by only one pair.  [`bracket_query_rank_results.json`](../bracket_query_rank_results.json)
needs rank 64 to kill 80% of the effect.  Therefore the next program must use the full
fixed head factors; it must not resurrect a failed TopK claim.  The 12+12 synthetic
cells in [`bracket_nested_results.json`](../bracket_nested_results.json) failed their
context sanity gate and are not OOD evidence.

### Cheapest concrete experiment

Run a four-arm, fresh-role `L13H8` terminal:

1. native model;
2. stored exact L13 attention with all nine heads reconstructed;
3. the same stored program with the constant H8 output slice zeroed;
4. the same stored program with H8's QK/OV factors replaced by a frozen equal-price
   spectral derangement.

The bracket stack is used only to score compatible closer, incompatible closer,
no-opener, quote, and global cells.  It never chooses a key or gates the program.  Use
natural SELECT and one-shot OOD roles split by delimiter type, depth, distance, and
code/prose; add a newly validated synthetic nested panel, but do not reuse the failed
12+12 outcome.  Promotion requires exact extraction, positive document-bootstrap
removal and specificity lower bounds, global upper bound `0.01` nat, every delimiter
subtype retaining sign, and the fixed derangement failing.

Reusable circuit code: target/control construction from
[`bracket_match.py`](../bracket_match.py), prospective nesting templates from
[`bracket_nested.py`](../bracket_nested.py), factor identities from
[`bracket_pointer_pairs.py`](../bracket_pointer_pairs.py), and rank/random calibration
only from [`bracket_query_rank.py`](../bracket_query_rank.py).  Do not reuse their row
streams or `kill_match` parser as an executable intervention.

## 2. Newline and structural boundary

### Why second

The owner is already stable and well powered.  [`newline_crew_screen_results.json`](../newline_crew_screen_results.json)
and [`newline_crews_results.json`](../newline_crews_results.json) identify the fixed
crew `{L7H2,L8H2,L10H2,L11H0,L12H6}` with `+0.6166` target damage and `+0.0049`
elsewhere over 7,397 newline positions.  The score expansion is numerically exact
(`1.29e-7` input, `5.14e-7` score).

Its simplification route is already falsified: in
[`newline_head_rebuild_results.json`](../newline_head_rebuild_results.json), 100/625
pairs retain only `0.692` newline behavior and 200 retain `0.918` on newline versus
`0.933` on controls.  Thus TopK writer pairs are both insufficient at the registered
small budget and nonspecific.  Exact full factors are still a valid fixed tensor and
make a terminal certificate relatively likely, but new Tier-5 compression is not.

### Cheapest concrete experiment

First run a source-closed one-head canary for L12H6, then the frozen five-head terminal
if the head-mask and zero-native-call ledgers pass.  Arms are native, exact stored
reconstruction, fixed global deletion of the named head(s), and equal-price head-label
derangement.  No newline mask may route execution, and no writer TopK is admitted.

Score newline CE against position-jitter, count-matched nonnewline, other punctuation,
capitalization, quote/bracket, and global cells.  OOD must hold out whole structure
domains (prose, code, lists/tables), line-length bands, and document domains, not merely
random positions from the same rows.  The five-head extraction must meet the campaign
recovery bound; removal must retain positive target-minus-each-control lower bounds and
global damage at most `0.01` nat.

Reusable circuit code: exact crew and masks from
[`newline_crew_screen.py`](../newline_crew_screen.py) and
[`newline_crews.py`](../newline_crews.py); full 625-pair identity and replacement
known answers from [`newline_head_pairs.py`](../newline_head_pairs.py) and
[`newline_head_rebuild.py`](../newline_head_rebuild.py).  The latter's adaptive ranking
is excluded from the candidate.

## 3. Article choice

### Why third

This is the best compact Tier-3 candidate.  [`article_choice_verify_results.json`](../article_choice_verify_results.json)
has full AUC `0.8700`; front attention and MLP ablation reduce it by `0.1671` and
`0.1330`, compared with `0.0071` for a random subspace.  The front-chain trace and
[`article_circuit_depth_results.json`](../article_circuit_depth_results.json) identify
an attention-0-to-MLP0 route.  [`article_write_rank_results.json`](../article_write_rank_results.json)
shows a FIT-derived rank-16 MLP0 write projection leaves only `0.0024` margin shift,
versus `0.01543` for rank zero and about `0.012`–`0.013` for equal-rank random
subspaces.

The blocker is real: [`article_trigger_trace_results.json`](../article_trigger_trace_results.json)
has `null_ok=false`, and no result composes exact L0/L1 attention, continuous MLP0
gates, and the rank-16 write.  A decoded article or vowel label cannot fill that gap.

### Cheapest concrete experiment

Before a full terminal, freeze the rank-16 MLP0 output projector on FIT documents and
run one disjoint SELECT sufficiency/falsifier with four fixed arms: native MLP0,
rank-16 keep plus frozen mean, rank-16 removal, and three frozen equal-rank random
projectors.  All arms execute the native bilinear gate as an owned fixed contraction;
the article mask is score-only.  Require the rank-16 program to beat every random arm
simultaneously on article-choice margin recovery and article-versus-other-determiner
specificity.  Failure retires the compact route before an expensive front-chain run.

If it passes, compose it with the owned exact L0/L1 attention bank and run terminal
extraction/removal.  OOD holds out noun bigrams, noun-frequency quartiles,
vowel/consonant onset, sentence-position bands, and domains.  Reuse
[`article_choice_verify.py`](../article_choice_verify.py) for the endpoint,
[`article_circuit_depth.py`](../article_circuit_depth.py) for the frozen front-chain
sites, and [`article_write_rank.py`](../article_write_rank.py) for the projector
construction.  Do not reuse the failed trigger null or its adaptive clusters as
terminal evidence.

## Why the others wait

- **Copied entity:** `circuit_capcopy_results.json` is large but diffuse across 13
  heads, and the source-closed four-head copy attempt has `+0.44869993` target damage
  but collateral margin `-0.01440914`.  The fixed induction matcher is reusable, but a
  name-fragment payload and interaction-resolved owner are still missing.
- **Novel capitalization:** 12-head removal is `+0.6031/+0.0403`, the six-head greedy
  set reaches only `+0.3000`, and copied entities dominate.  No fixed native selector
  separates novel register from copy service.
- **Quote closure:** L13H8 is surgical (`+0.5240/~0.003`), but the decoded quote-state
  direction loses only `0.0049` of the gap causally, and the weights/control tests fail.
  Bracket extraction should establish the shared head machinery first.
- **Numeric formatting:** the only direct screen has 77 targets and `null_ok=false`.
  It should receive a powered behavior screen before any tensor compiler work.

This ordering avoids duplicating induction/successor infrastructure and maximizes the
chance that the next GPU run changes an extraction/removal/OOD ledger rather than
producing another localization-only result.
