# From the 62-leaf census to ten executable circuits

Status: discovery bridge, not a terminal certificate.  This note keeps two uses of
“circuit” separate.  The census has 62 statistically localized *behavioral slices*;
the ten-circuit campaign asks for executable tensor programs with extraction,
selective removal, collateral, and OOD tests.  A slice does not become a high-tier
circuit merely because one component is unusually important on it.

## What the new census establishes

On 256,000 scored positions, mean ablation localized all 62 curated slices to a
best component with member/off-slice absolute-CE concentration between `2.61` and
`12.28` (median `4.08`).  Interchange agreed on the best component for 45/62.
The globally loudest component, MLP1, won zero slices, so this is not simply a
ranking of component scale.  Attention 8 wins 16 slices and attention 16 wins 13:
almost half the census routes through two shared substrates.

This earns localization evidence.  It does **not** yet establish a human-readable
behavior definition, a fixed internal subspace, selective removal, OOD transport,
or a zero-native-call tensor executor.  `circuits/BATTERY.json` and
`circuits/DOSSIER.md` are therefore candidate generators, not substitutes for the
tier rubric.

## The useful crosswalk

1. **Newline/structural boundary is strengthened and split.**  `r.2.0.0`
   localizes to attention 8 at concentration `12.28`; `r.3.0.0` and `r.3.0.1`
   localize heading/list newlines to attention 16 at `11.87` and `7.57`.
   The five-head newline crew should not be treated as one flat owner.  The terminal
   scorer must stratify heading/list, ordinary prose, code, and other punctuation;
   the program should price a shared structural substrate once and distinct early
   and late children separately.
2. **Capitalization/name service has several stages.**  Rare/proper-name-like
   `r.0.3.0` localizes to attention 3; generic-versus-proper-noun `r.23.2.1`
   localizes to attention 8; the weak capitalized-target slice `r.7.1.1` localizes
   to attention 7.  This supports the campaign's separation of copied entities from
   novel capitalization instead of one pooled capitalization circuit.
3. **Punctuation is a shared control family, not yet one circuit.**
   `r.11.1.1`, `r.11.1.2`, and `r.13.2.1` localize punctuation effects to different
   components.  Bracket, quote, and newline experiments must score these leaves as
   collateral and must not infer delimiter semantics from generic punctuation
   damage.
4. **Templated/list/numeric formatting has concrete candidates.**  `r.11.3.1`
   distinguishes templated completions from list/numeric breaks; `r.6.1.1` contains
   URL/list separator behavior; `r.6.3.0` contains hard delimiter behavior in
   travel-guide layouts.  These are better starting masks for the numeric-formatting
   campaign than the old underpowered 77-target screen, but their behavior labels
   and nulls still require fresh validation.
5. **Induction remains the cleanest algebraic program.**  Its equality tensor is
   defined independently of census membership and already has exact zero-native-call
   replay.  The fresh natural/code transaction is the first campaign item capable of
   obtaining extraction, removal, collateral, and OOD evidence together.

## How to use the localizations without leaking labels into execution

The best component or learned subspace is a FIT/SELECT prior.  It may choose which
fixed tensor is materialized before FINAL/OOD, but it may never route an intervention
on a token.  Behavioral parsers and census tags are score-only.  A promoted program
must execute the same contraction on every token, and a removal must be a fixed
tensor edit or fixed component projector.  TopK support, nearest-member lookup, or a
tag-conditioned hook would fail tensor composability and earns no extraction credit.

The ongoing DAS run is the right next falsifier for the hierarchical hypothesis: it
learns rank-1 and rank-4 component subspaces on rows 0--599 and evaluates them on
rows 600--999.  A result is interpretable only if the optimizer-health gate proves
the subspace moved from initialization.  Even a passing DAS result remains discovery
evidence until frozen on new documents and tested for selective causal effects.
