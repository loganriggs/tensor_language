## How simplicity metrics changed the work

Simplicity is being treated as a prediction about a capability, not as one universal
score.

- Stored values earned trust for rank-640 because lower storage coincided with a real
  standalone artifact and predictive/causal retention.
- Local low rank was demoted after its axes failed probe-to-probe repeatability.
- Local mean-squared error was demoted after early-MLP composition failures.
- Lexical classes remain a useful description, but have not beaten equally priced
  continuous alternatives downstream.
- Gauge-independent gate count now defines the MLP1 candidate: harmless factor
  rescaling, sign, or ordering changes cannot change the control or selected program.
- Sparse physical gates will count as useful simplicity only if they predict stable
  held-out downstream response, then finite edits, lower execution cost, and selective
  removability.

The three-hour mathematical reviews caused three concrete changes: they moved the
search from arbitrary latent coordinates to physical actions; separated response-span
reconstruction from reproduction of the all-gates operating point; and made gauge
invariance plus simultaneous inference executable requirements. They also suggest the
predeclared failure branch: study the intrinsic Gram spectrum of MLP1's quadratic forms
and seek a new simultaneous factorization rather than repeatedly searching checkpoint
gate subsets.
