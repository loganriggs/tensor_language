# Corrected subject-number removal and L11H3 mediation V1

Registered after the inherited attractor-indexing bug was found and after the
corrected internal scalar decomposition, but before opening any corrected-panel
behavior logits. This assay repairs the behavioral removal and mediation
evidence on the frozen 128-row authority whose attractor is asserted to be an
atomic opposite-number noun in every row.

For each row, remove the frozen embedding-number coordinate by moving the
normalized subject embedding to decoder score zero at exactly preserved norm.
Compare its agreement-margin damage with 16 frozen random tangent edits at the
same token, norm, and distance. Also measure collateral change in the unrelated
`can`/`will` margin.

At L11H3, restore either the full native head-output difference, its projection
onto the already-frozen rank-one writer axis, or one of 16 matched random output
axes. Full-head and rank-one restorations retain an explicit native base-head
donor port; this assay tests transfer and mediation, not donor-free extraction.

All model runs are divided into the same two 64-row context batches used by the
precision-qualified scalar assay. Instrumentation requires decoder correctness,
removal geometry within `1e-5`, repeated removed-head captures within `1e-5`,
native accuracy at least `.75` in every number/template/regularity cell, exact
prices, and finite values.

Coordinate removal is live only if damage RMS is at least `.5`, at least `.05`
of native-margin RMS, and positive on at least `.75` of every registered cell.
It is selective only if it exceeds every equal-distance random control, is at
least twice their median RMS, and `can`/`will` collateral is at most `.5` of
target damage.

The corrected small mediation path transfers only if full L11H3 restoration has
cosine at least `.75`, positive fraction at least `.70`, and least-squares
damage recovery in `[.05,.20]`. The rank-one restoration must recover
`[.04,.18]` of damage and reproduce the full-head rescue with cosine at least
`.97`, relative L2 at most `.20`, and norm ratio in `[.75,1.25]`. It must also
have lower error than every matched random output axis and at least ten times
their median effect RMS.

Price: 72 full forwards over 4,608 sequences, 16 embedding controls, 16 output
controls, no fitting, gradients, or parameter updates.
