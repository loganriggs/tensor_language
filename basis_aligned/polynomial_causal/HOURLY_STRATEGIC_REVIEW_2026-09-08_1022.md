## Circuit interpretation targets and full objective

A useful decomposition must identify the information read, computation performed, write, and
downstream readers; group causal variables across native module boundaries and split native
modules when necessary; predict held-out and OOD effects; expose an executable circuit or a
precise background interface; support selective removals, swaps, and edits; compose and reuse
shared subcomputations; and remain stably identified across documents, gauges, and restarts or by
operational equivalence. The program-level objective remains a smaller transparent tensor program
that is predictive, composable, selectively manipulable, and cheaper under literal storage,
compute, edge, state, and program price. Rank, reconstruction, variance, or CE alone cannot close
any of these circuit requirements.

  selected-writer effect; the greedy receipt is replayed numerically.

  pooled task margins. No task family or document is selected from an arm outcome.

  measures its 2x2 residual explicitly. Final normalization/readout nonlinearity remains visible.
- **Leakage/post-selection:** only original FIT may select a cumulative prefix. HOLDOUT cannot
  reselect. The factorial has no fitted or selected component.
- **Dead knobs/order:** all module hooks must fire once; R1M1 exact replay and A11/P1 replay are live

- **Precision/noise floor:** replay limits are `1e-5`, substantially below scientific recovery

  are the wrong units. The factorial is the independent boundary test; a response-positive branch
  proceeds to attention-head splitting, while an identity branch changes the object to a residual
  boundary variable.
