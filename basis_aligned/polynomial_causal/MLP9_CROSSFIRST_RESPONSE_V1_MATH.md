## Exact response, including changing normalization

Let z be the native pre-MLP9 residual state, $w\in\mathbb R^{1152}$ the fixed head9 writer, and

$$
\mathcal B(x)=D[(Lx)\odot(Rx)],
\qquad L,R\in\mathbb R^{4608\times1152},\quad D\in\mathbb R^{1152\times4608}.
$$

The MLP adds an output bias, which cancels from a response difference. Define the **native bias-free normalized MLP output** and squared RMS denominators

$$
m_0=\mathcal B\!\left(\frac{z}{\sqrt{\rho_0}}\right),
\qquad
\rho_0=\frac{\|z\|^2}{1152}+\epsilon,
\qquad
\rho_a=\frac{\|z-aw\|^2}{1152}+\epsilon.
$$

The fixed weight map

$$
J_w=D\left[\operatorname{diag}(Rw)L+\operatorname{diag}(Lw)R\right]
\in\mathbb R^{1152\times1152}
$$

gives the exact change in residual-plus-MLP output:

$$
\Delta(a)
=-aw+\left(\frac{\rho_0}{\rho_a}-1\right)m_0
-\frac{a}{\rho_a}J_wz
+\frac{a^2}{2\rho_a}J_ww.
$$

The denominator is also explicit:

$$
\rho_a=\rho_0-\frac{2a\langle z,w\rangle}{1152}
+\frac{a^2\|w\|^2}{1152}.
$$

This retains normalization's full dependence on the edit. It is not a small-amplitude Taylor approximation or a frozen-denominator formula.

For fixed native contextz, every amplitude response lies in the span of four vectors: $w,m_0,J_wz,J_ww$. The two-edit mixed response

$$
\Delta(a+b)-\Delta(a)-\Delta(b)
$$

has its linear writer term cancel, so it lies in the span of the lastthree. The coefficients follow directly from the rational formula; no projection fit or factorization is required. These are per-context response directions, not three identified semantic circuits.
