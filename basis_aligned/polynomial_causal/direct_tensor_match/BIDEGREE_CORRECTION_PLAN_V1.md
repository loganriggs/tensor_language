# Bidegree-typed correction of the CP512 parent (matched capacity, all 16 outputs)

22 September 2026, 17:20 UTC. Claude (Fable). Registered before any fitting. Licensed by the source-geometry census (SOURCE_GEOMETRY_CENSUS_PLAN_V1.md): with the parent's pieces in the correct order, 0.833 of the residual's piece energy over outputs 4–15 sits in pieces with at least one context factor, the pure-state piece of the target carries only 0.090, and the residual's five pieces cancel (their energies sum to 4.6× the residual) — the CP parent, fitted on x = S + C, matches the sum on-distribution with the wrong bidegree typing. The run-1 receipt with the reversed parent order is preserved as SOURCE_GEOMETRY_CENSUS_V1_run1_parent_order_bug.json; the corrected receipt is queued and will be written to SOURCE_GEOMETRY_CENSUS_V1.json before this rung runs.

**Question.** At the capacity of Codex's final hybrid correction (96 quartic atoms, 442,464 added coefficients), do atoms whose four reads are typed as reads of the propagated source state S or of the context transport C repair the residual better than atoms that read x = S + C, under the same objective, loop, seeds and budget?

**Target and panels.** Exactly the hybrid experiment's: target = the stored native quartic labels (SENSITIVE_ROOT_CALIBRATION_V2 panel 0, 6,144 states from 96 documents × 64 positions; RESIDUAL_FRESH_STATES_V1, 16,384 states from 256 fresh documents; ALL_FEATURE_MATCHED_RESPONSES_V1, 2,494 same-token same-position pairs), parent = the frozen CP512 MIXED_CP_FEATURES_SEED1001_V1 evaluated at the stored normalized row, residual = target − parent. All 16 outputs are fitted and reported (the hybrid protected outputs 0–3 and fitted only 4–15).

**Source split.** S and C are captured with the census instrument on both panels (embedding + MLP writes vs attention writes, each propagated through the λ-recurrence to the MLP16 input and divided by that input's rms); S + C must reproduce the stored rows to 1e-4.

**Correction class.** Per output, 6 atoms (16 × 6 = 96, matched to the hybrid's 12 × 8). An atom is a product of four unit-normalized reads; in the TYPED arm an atom of bidegree (p, q) reads S p times and C q times; in the UNTYPED control every read is of x. The number of atoms of each bidegree per output is fixed before fitting from the corrected census: largest-remainder rounding of 6 × that output's residual piece shares (40/31/22/13/04). Coefficients are profiled in closed form (ridge 1e-8) at every step under the objective; the reads are trained by Adam (lr 0.05, 400 updates, two seeds 25001 and 25002, raw random initialization). Objective = mean over the 16 outputs of the native-sensitivity-weighted relative squared error on the fitting split (80 documents); the snapshot is the step with the lowest objective on the held-out 16 calibration documents. Nothing from the fresh panel or the pairs enters fitting or selection.

**Metrics** (as in the hybrid receipt): per-output fresh value error rms(pred − y)/rms(y); small-output value RMS = rms over outputs 4–15; response error on the matched pairs per output and its small-output RMS; cross-start cosine of the two seeds' correction functions on the fresh panel per output.

**Predictions (scored as written; failures preserved).**
1. pred_a_integrity: S + C reproduces the stored rows on both panels to ≤ 1e-4; the exported programs reloaded from disk reproduce every reported fresh error to ≤ 1e-8; the planted controls pass (typed atoms vanish when the source they read is zeroed; profiled coefficients recover a planted combination; allocations sum to 6).
2. pred_b_typed_beats_untyped: for both seeds, the typed arm's fresh small-output value RMS AND response RMS are ≤ 0.85 × the untyped arm's. Prior: unsure.
3. pred_c_beats_hybrid: for both seeds, the typed arm's fresh small-output value RMS ≤ 0.40 and response RMS ≤ 0.42 (Codex's hybrid: 0.466/0.492; its Gaussian-Adam baseline 0.526/0.541; parent alone 0.526/0.622). Prior: unlikely.
4. pred_d_large_outputs_kept: for both seeds, the typed arm's fresh value error on each of outputs 0–3 is ≤ the parent's, and on output 3 ≤ 0.9 × the parent's. Prior: likely / unsure.
5. pred_e_cross_start_stability: the typed arm's cross-start cosine on the fresh panel has mean ≥ 0.9 and minimum ≥ 0.8 over outputs 4–15 (Codex's hybrid: 0.70–0.93 on text, two of twelve above 0.9). Prior: unsure.

**Price.** Forwards: 12 (calibration split) + 32 (fresh split) ≤ 64 registered; model backwards 0; four fits of 442,464 parameters (96 atoms × 4 reads × 1,152 + 96 coefficients) for 400 updates each. Literal program cost per atom: 4 × 1,152 read multiply-adds, 3 products, 1 coefficient multiply, 1 add; reported in the receipt.
