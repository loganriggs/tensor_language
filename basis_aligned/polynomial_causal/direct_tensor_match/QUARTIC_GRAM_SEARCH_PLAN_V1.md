**Signed Gram representatives for quartic graph initialization**

For an orthonormal quadratic monomial vector z(x), search symmetric K with f(x)=z(x)^T K z(x). The linear coefficient map A has a nullspace: changing K in that nullspace preserves the polynomial. Search for low-rank K by alternating exact projection onto A(K)=f and signed rank truncation. This is a heuristic, not a minimum-rank certificate. Root eigenvectors become candidate quadratic features; price their dense leaves separately.

Preregistered control: five scalar structures (radial square, rotated dense square, signed difference of squares, product of quadratics, shared quadratic factor times a sum), dimension5, known root ranks1/1/2/2/2, four starts,1000iterations maximum. Require canonical polynomial replay<1e-12 and best-of-four coefficient error<1e-6 for each family. Report every failure. Coefficient norm is fully symmetric Frobenius via monomial multiplicities. This tests function-equivalent representations, not uniqueness or arbitrary circuit recovery.

Relevant primary literature: [Gram spectrahedra of ternary quartics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10533) studies PSD Gram representations. Our signed matrix search drops PSD because model outputs need not be nonnegative; PSD rank/existence results do not transfer. The affine Gram representation is the applicable construction. Prior radial/CP controls already establish representation dependence; the new action is explicit nullspace search rather than fixing one coefficient representative.
