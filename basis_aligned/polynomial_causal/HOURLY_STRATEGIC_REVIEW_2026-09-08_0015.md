## Circuit interpretation targets and full goal

A circuit must eventually provide: (1) a computational specification of what is read, computed,
and written; (2) cross-boundary grouping and within-module splitting instead of assuming heads or
MLPs are semantic atoms; (3) held-out and OOD causal prediction; (4) an executable circuit or
explicit sufficiency interface; (5) selective removal, interchange, and editing with redundancy
and interactions measured; (6) predictable composition and reuse; and (7) stable identification
across text, constructions, gauges, and fitting restarts.  The full goal remains a smaller
transparent tensor program that is jointly predictive, composable, manipulable, and simpler under
literal storage, compute, edge, state, and program prices.  Low rank and weight alignment remain
diagnostics until causal and selective evidence licenses them.
