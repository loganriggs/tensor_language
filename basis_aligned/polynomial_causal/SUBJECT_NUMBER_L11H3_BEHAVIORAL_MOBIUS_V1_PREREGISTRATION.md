# Subject-number L11H3 three-port behavioral Möbius V1

## Purpose

Resolve the registered `18–19%` behavioral nonadditivity of the three main-edge
graph into explicit sparse interaction edges.

## Frozen design

Use the same corrected authority, embedding-number intervention, pre-L11 site,
and three native ports (`upstream_0_7`, `mlp_8`, `mlp_10`) as the passed
behavioral test. Run all eight port-swap corners through the native L11–L17
suffix and compute the exact seven-term Möbius expansion of the agreement-margin
damage in float64.

The three main effects are mandatory. Of the three pair terms and one triple
term, select the first correction that minimizes discovery residual, then a
second correction that minimizes the remaining discovery residual. Apply the
same unscaled correction edge(s), with no reselection or coefficient fitting,
to context OOD, lexical OOD, and joint OOD.

## Registered gates

- Instrument: finite; native corner-0 replay at most `1e-5`; closed component
  reconstruction at most `1e-10`; gauge correction relative L2 at most `1e-5`;
  absolute and relative Möbius closure at most `1e-10`; exact prices/checkpoint.
- One-correction graph: component-relative L2 at most `0.10`, cosine at least
  `0.99`, and positive aligned recovery on every panel.
- Two-correction graph: the same gates on every panel.

Passing either composition gate gives a sparse behavioral graph at the
briefing's `<10%` target. The experiment inherits, rather than reruns, the
parent's equal-L2 removal controls and rescue test.
