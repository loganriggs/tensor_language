## Circuit interpretation targets

The circuit target remains: (1) an explicit read/operate/write/consumer computation, (2) grouping
across native modules and splitting within them when operationally required, (3) held-out and OOD
prediction, (4) extraction or causal sufficiency, (5) selective removal/swap/edit, (6) predictable
composition and reuse, and (7) stable identification across corpora, gauges, and restarts.

The program-level objective remains a smaller transparent tensor program that is jointly predictive
on fresh/OOD text, composable, selectively manipulable, and literally cheaper in storage, compute,
edges, state, and program price. A valid screen, low rank, low loss, or exact local tensor alone does
not complete that objective.
