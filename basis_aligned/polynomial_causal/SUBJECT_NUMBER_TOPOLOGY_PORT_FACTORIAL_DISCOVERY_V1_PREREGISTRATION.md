# Subject-number topology port-factorial discovery V1

Registered after the valid topology-conditioned `YZ` prototype null. This is
opened-authority discovery over the same 16 two-clause rows; it reads no answer
logits or downstream intervention outcomes and makes no fresh-OOD claim.

At subject positions 5 and 14, capture native and opposite-number roles and use
the validated exact MLP8 input decomposition into six typed ports `E/A/U/W/Y/Z`.
Evaluate all 64 hybrids, always through the recipient-native downstream head
background. The frozen native L11H3 axis, `[1,z,s,zs] beta` law, and independently
effective fixed coefficient `-31.14876154` remain unchanged.

Use two reciprocal leave-one-template-out folds at each site. On a training
template, a subset qualifies if its fixed-coefficient relative L2 error is at most
`.50` and sign agreement at least `.75`. Select among qualifiers by minimum port
count, then minimum error, then the canonical `E/A/U/W/Y/Z` bit mask. If none
qualifies, select the minimum-error subset but mark that fold unqualified. No
subset, family, interaction, or threshold may be chosen after held-out values are
read.

Instrumentation requires exactly two physical forwards over 64 sequences, 2,048
offline head evaluations, native head replay, input closure, and every hybrid
closure at most `5e-5`, plus finite values for all 2,048 cells.

Across the 32 held-out site/row examples, the selected sparse port program must
have fixed-coefficient relative L2 error at most `.50`, sign agreement at least
`.90`, every fold qualified on training data, and use at most three ports in every
fold. As a matched null, compare each selection with every other subset of the
same cardinality; the selected program must beat the median held-out null error by
at least `.10`. If cardinality is zero or six, so no matched alternative exists,
the null gate fails rather than being treated as vacuous.

Independently report a Boolean-cube Möbius decomposition of the generated scalar
coefficient across all ports. This allocation is diagnostic: a small number of
large low-order terms motivates a frozen sparse topology adapter; diffuse or
high-order mass closes this port-subset route on the current boundary.
