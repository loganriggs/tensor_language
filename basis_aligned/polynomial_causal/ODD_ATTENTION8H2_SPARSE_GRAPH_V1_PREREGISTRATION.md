# Prospective inherited-city sparse interaction graph V1

Freeze a three-port Boolean intervention lattice at the head8.2 city-source
write. The ports are donor **routing**, donor **current value**, and donor
**inherited/first-layer value**. Each of the eight corners is propagated through
the already identified head9.8-O response and the native suffix. The complete
lattice is measured on 48 previously authored discovery rows and on 96 natural
FineWeb rows selected outcome-blind from the unused `skip39000` cache.

The unchanged `sparse_interaction_graph.py` computes all seven non-baseline
Möbius terms. A coefficient-free three-step greedy search sees only the authored
rows' target-token effects. The selected masks are then frozen and evaluated on
the natural rows. No masks, coefficients, thresholds, or rows change after
natural-panel scoring. Eight corners over 144 prefixes cost 1,152 body forwards;
there are no fits, gradients, or model updates.

- **A — instrument:** row hashes and pair structure pass; recomputed native
  head8.2 source channels, routing/current/inherited identities, full Möbius
  closure, and the generic graph reconstruction all have relative error at most
  `1e-5`; every non-native corner is live and finite, changes only frozen
  destinations, and exactly 1,152 body forwards execute.
- **B — prospective OOD prediction:** the three discovery-selected unscaled
  atoms predict the full-donor target effect on the untouched natural rows with
  component-relative L2 error at most `.35`, cosine at least `.9`, and no sign
  reversals where the full effect magnitude is at least `1e-5`.
- **C — behavioral composition:** the sum of the three separately measured
  Möbius atoms predicts their joint full-corner effect within `.35` separately
  in both natural cue directions and within `.35` on every one of the six
  endpoints. Errors are relative to this component's full effect.
- **D — reusable sparse graph:** the same frozen mask set has discovery error at
  most `.25`, requires fewer than all eight lattice corners after freezing, and
  each selected atom is live on the natural panel. The report names the three
  ports, selected masks, and minimal required corners.
- **E — target selectivity:** for the frozen graph prediction, each unrelated
  cat/dog, red/blue, Monday/Tuesday, and apple/orange RMS is at most `.5` of the
  target-effect RMS on the natural panel.

This tests prospective graph selection, OOD prediction, and nontrivial joint
composition. Existing extracted executors and pair-centered removal evidence
are not silently upgraded: four-trait certification still requires binding the
selected graph to a standalone package and an equal-norm same-site removal null.
The natural cache is not proven pretraining-disjoint.
