## Top five next actions

### 1. Finish the MLP2 finite CMR experiment

**Why first:** it attacks the largest early-stack interaction at a site without a
duplicative global-gate assay.  It is a small, falsifiable executable: retain 512 of
4,608 MLP2 products, replace omitted products by fit means, and fold those means into
the bias.  Compare a trajectory-complete suffix/Fisher selector with centered local
write mass, uncentered activation/Down mass, invariant weight mass, random, and
deranged controls at identical price.

**Success:** actual finite final-logit CE/KL/nRMSE and top-1 agreement beat every
control under simultaneous document inference; signed small edits agree with the
tangent; an actual-logit margin certificate is nonvacuous; no native MLP2 call remains.
Then cross the unchanged program with C512.

**Cost/falsifiability:** one site and one retained width keep GPU cost bounded.  A
failure prunes K512 native-basis MLP2 compression rather than inviting a budget sweep.

### 2. Execute the fresh 16-subset copy interaction cube

**Why:** the current copy effect is large, robust, and strongly nonadditive.  Only ten
subset arms are missing.  Exact Boolean Möbius coefficients can tell whether one pair
or triple explains most of the effect, while displacement norms and a scaled-full-set
curve distinguish a special interaction from generic large-ablation nonlinearity.

**Success:** nonadditivity replicates and a pair/triple accounts for at least half of
the full copy effect with simultaneous positive specificity.  Otherwise head subsets
are pruned as the coordinate system and the downstream-Fisher basis becomes next.

### 3. Run the MLP3 K512 native-Down behavioral port

**Why:** Family F's refitted decoder failed, but the native-Down K512 diagnostic had
better downstream KL (`0.05772`) despite worse local NRMSE.  This is the strongest
existing one-ninth-width polynomial candidate and directly tests whether preserving
the checkpoint's output semantics matters more than local reconstruction.

**Success:** finite CE/KL and edit behavior beat random and decoder-refit controls on
fresh rows with no hidden native call.  This branch remains separate from MLP2 because
its candidate already exists and its failure mode is different.

### 4. Build the common early-stack composition telescope

Install frozen MLP0, MLP2, and attention candidates on one shared role.  Measure
singletons, pairs, and joint replacements; use Möbius terms to predict unseen
compositions.  This is mandatory before independently successful parts can be called
a whole program.  It ranks fourth because it depends on obtaining a valid MLP2
candidate first.

### 5. Use response-conditioned factors/balanced coordinates if native units fail

Combine MLP1 `Left`, MLP1 `Right`, MLP2 response, and the validated copy latent as
multiple views, or balance an edit-derived controllability covariance against a
suffix-observability covariance.  Require split stability, held-out response
prediction, and finite causal swaps against PCA/HOSVD/Haar controls.  This is the
principled alternate-basis route, but it is more expensive and assumption-heavy than
first falsifying the exact native MLP2 grammar.
