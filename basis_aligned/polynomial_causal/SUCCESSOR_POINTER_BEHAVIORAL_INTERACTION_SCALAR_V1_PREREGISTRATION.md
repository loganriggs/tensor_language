# Successor pointer behavioral interaction scalar V1

The confirmed length-six attention8–12 plus MLP8–12 mediator is strongly nonadditive: the sum of the separate attention and MLP behavioral responses overshoots their joint response in both opened families. The previous low-rank experiment targeted the full 1,152-dimensional residual interaction and required one basis vector per fit row. This experiment instead tests whether the causally relevant interaction compresses at the behavioral interface to one shared suppressive scalar.

FIT is every opened unprefixed length-six group: five month endpoints and three digit endpoints. For the coherent-to-late transplant, let `e_A`, `e_M`, and `e_AM` be the post-softcap backward-margin changes caused by attention8–12, MLP8–12, and their union. Define `x=e_A+e_M`, `y=e_AM`, and fit exactly one through-origin coefficient `beta=(x·y)/(x·x)` across all eight FIT groups. The frozen law predicts `e_AM_hat=beta*(e_A+e_M)`. A suppressive interaction requires `0 < beta < 1`. No family, endpoint, prefix, direction, intercept, residual basis, or construction coefficient is allowed.

HOLDOUT is the outcome-blind prefixed authority generated before model execution: the labels `Sequence:`, `Pattern:`, `Continue:`, and `Items:` crossed with the same eight semantic endpoints and transpositions. Exact token sequences are disjoint from FIT. The coefficient is frozen before any HOLDOUT outcomes are analyzed. Each prefix is run as a separate batch because tokenized prefix lengths differ.

The exact `AM` parent must remain live in every family and prefix: native accuracy at least .75 in every condition; mean coherent-minus-late backward target at least 1.0; exact `AM` projection at least .50 and cosine at least .70 against that target; late-forward and early-backward control RMS at most .50 of target RMS. Self replay must be within `1e-5` logits and the all-module ceiling within `1e-4`.

The frozen scalar passes FIT and HOLDOUT when, overall and in every family (FIT) or prefix-by-family cell (HOLDOUT), prediction versus exact `AM` has cosine at least .90, relative L2 error at most .35, sign agreement at least .80, and predicted/actual norm ratio from .65 to 1.35. Predicted late-forward and early-backward controls must each remain at most .50 of the native backward-target RMS in every evaluated cell. Report exact additive, joint, interaction, target, scalar prediction, and control metrics.

A pass establishes a one-scalar behavioral interaction adapter conditional on the live attention and MLP component responses. It does not compress or generate those component responses, identify a 1,152-dimensional residual subspace, establish length-seven transfer, or close the full circuit. A null rejects this frozen scalar without subgrouping, bar changes, or refitting on HOLDOUT. Price is 55 forwards and 440 sequence evaluations, one analytic fit, zero gradients or updates, and no quantization.
