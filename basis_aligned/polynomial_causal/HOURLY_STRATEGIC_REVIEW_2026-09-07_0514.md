## Circuit targets and controlling goal

A completed circuit must specify what is read, computed, written, and consumed; group components
across native boundaries and split native modules when their response directions serve different
functions; predict held-out and OOD effects; be sufficient or expose a precise live interface;
support selective removal, swapping, and editing; compose shared and task-specific paths; and remain
stable across documents, constructions, controls, gauges, and fits. The controlling goal remains a
smaller literal tensor program that is jointly predictive, composable, manipulable, and cheaper in
storage, compute, state, edges, and intervention price. Neither a fitted axis nor low causal loss is
a completion certificate.

## Route, confounds, and alternatives

The highest-information current route is the opposite-half target-contrast confirmation because it
directly changes within-module splitting, held-out prediction, selectivity, and stable identification.
It dies if either task falls below `.80` behavior projection, worst residual exceeds `.05`, or C
movement exceeds `.10` on the swapped halves.

Confounds audited:

- **Post-selection/leakage:** ranks and sites are frozen; discovery and evaluation halves swap in
  full. The v1 strict-dominance miss is preserved.
- **Baseline and frame mixing:** target and C errors are normalized task-wise; matched C donors have
  identical answers, foils, lengths, and semantic endpoints. Two earlier misaligned instruments are
  preserved invalid rather than padded or truncated.
- **Nonlinear composition:** all selected response projections run jointly through the native
  transformer. Linear PCA is only the within-site response definition.
- **Precision:** absolute float error is interpreted only alongside reference-normalized max error
  and RSE. The extra audit was required by a concrete invalid gate, not a generic rerun.
- **Shared-token difficulty:** target contrast is fit jointly across tasks but every held-out metric
  is task-separated; C cannot select rank or direction.
- **Dead knobs:** no fitted penalty, optimizer, or rank sweep chooses the reported rank 8.

Alternatives now ranked:

1. **Opposite-half target-contrast response basis.** Current route; kill under the bars above.
2. **Weight translation of a cross-fitted response split.** If 1 passes, project attention c-proj
   blocks and MLP Down maps into the identified response bases, then verify exact causal replay and
   expose upstream pattern/value and bilinear-product interfaces. Kill if weight contraction cannot
   reproduce the response intervention scale-relatively.
3. **Predictive-state causal quotient or conditional gate.** If 1 fails, linear feature subspaces are
   sample-specific; fit an operational equivalence quotient over downstream effects or a task-
   conditioned nonlinear gate. Kill if it cannot predict held-out intervention responses better
   than the full module mask while staying C-selective.
4. **More native masks or generic-PC complements.** Rejected: complete evidence now shows generic
   transport remains inside the same high-ranked modules and outside the leading control PCs.
