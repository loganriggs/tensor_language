# Bracket key2 native-relative transport V1

The seventh-construction source factorial attributes `.83088` of full-program
error RMS to the frozen absolute key2 prototype. Payload already preserves
recipient context by adding a frozen donor-minus-recipient type displacement to
the native recipient payload. Test the analogous architecture-native transport
for keys: native recipient key plus the same frozen rank-two donor-minus-
recipient type displacement.

On the opened seventh cascade panel, compare the published absolute donor-free
program with two fixed candidates: relative key2 with absolute key1, then
relative key2 plus relative key1. Both retain the frozen relative payload, live
recipient query, and native suffix. A candidate must improve donor-key2 vector
relative L2 by at least `.05` over the absolute key2 prototype and pass the
existing overall, ordered-pair, exact-parent, and matched-control behavioral
bars. Select in that fixed order.

Only a passing candidate may open the separately generated eighth serial-
pending construction. Require unchanged key transport, program, candidate,
bars, and behavioral transfer there. Overall cosine must be at least `.90`,
relative L2 at most `.40`, sign at least `.90`, and norm ratio `.60`--`1.40`;
every pair requires cosine `.75`, relative L2 `.50`, and sign `.85`; controls
must be at most `.50` of exact target RMS. Exact effects must be positive on
`.90` of every pair, native replay must be within `1e-5`, native capability at
least `.75`, and all frozen table ranks must equal two.

No coefficient, table, rank, delimiter feature, query, subgroup, construction
feature, fit, gradient, update, gain, bar change, donor state in a program arm,
or quantization is allowed. Maximum conditional price is 11 forwards and 1,584
sequences; a FIT null stops at 6 forwards and 864 sequences, with zero fits,
backwards, and updates.
