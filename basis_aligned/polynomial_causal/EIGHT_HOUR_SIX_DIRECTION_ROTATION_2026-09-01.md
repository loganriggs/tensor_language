### `03:02 UTC` — direction `6`

- Full goal restated: find a substantially smaller predictive, composable, manipulable, literally priced
  tensor program; do not mistake a good fit or a compute policy for identified structure.
- Exact object and literal price: (i) a 64-product planted bilinear teacher/student with a known
  block/tree/DAG support family; (ii) every 50,257-token exact position-zero MLP0 input; and (iii) legal
  `S in {4,8,16,32}`, fixed-`K=512` router programs priced as
  `|union|*3456 + S*1152 + S + 1152`, versus native MLP0 `15,926,400` scalars.
- Opposing predictions and frozen bars: independent student support recovery at R2/Jaccard/F1
  `>=.98/.85/.85`; a small router with accuracy `>=.70` and MSE improvement `>=15%`; or a half-price
  router with R2 `>=.80`. The structural-prior follow-up required spectrum-prior R2/Jaccard/F1
  `>=.98/.85/.85` and discrimination against wrong priors.
- Result/receipt: teacher positive control recovered Jaccard/F1 `1.0/1.0`; a dense student fit the same
  function at R2 `0.999940` but recovered Jaccard/F1 `0.281/0.0`. Real router state labels were learnable
  above chance (best `S=4`: `.652` versus `.25`), but even oracle fixed subsets had negative R2; best legal
  R2 was `-0.052` at `S=32`, while unconstrained per-token top-k reached `0.754`. With the correct support-size
  spectrum prior the toy reached R2/Jaccard/F1 `0.999927/.820/.775`, missing the structure bar. A wrong
  pair-only prior reached still higher R2 `0.999999` with F1 `.245`.
- Null, confound, or identifiability limit: the explicit dense-random negative control also scored
  Jaccard/F1 `.281/0.0`, so the scorer did not hallucinate structure. More importantly, held-out function
  fit cannot select among incompatible structural priors; future real-MLP structural claims require an
  external discriminator (priced compression, intervention transfer, or OOD behavior).
- Information gained: raw trained bilinear factors are non-canonical even for a structured teacher; a
  small finite router has genuine label signal but fixed `K=512` subsets do not capture MLP0. The large
  gap to per-token top-k confirms that combinatorial input-dependent support is not a small-state tensor
  network.
- Continue/kill/park decision: **kill** raw weight-support recovery and this small fixed-subset router;
  **park** minimum-description structured factorization until it has a non-R2 discriminator.
- Next direction started: direct cross-layer sharing of the invariant bilinear coefficient tensors, with
