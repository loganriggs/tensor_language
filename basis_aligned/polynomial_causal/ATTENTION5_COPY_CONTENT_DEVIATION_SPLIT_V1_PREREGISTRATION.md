# ATTENTION5_COPY_CONTENT_DEVIATION_SPLIT_V1 preregistration

Broad document-CE head sets and response quotients did not transfer from natural
text to code. Use the pre-existing terminal-copy cell masks as a task-defined
causal endpoint. On the first24 final-natural and code documents, compare the
fixed fit-set attention5 mean against deviations from shared head7,
gross-write-specific head5, variable-correction-specific heads6+3, heads5+7,
heads6+7+3, and all nine. Positive copy cells and frequency/position-matched
negative cells were frozen by the terminal-copy lifecycle before this experiment.

- **A:** cache/parent hashes, mask disjointness, support counts, and matched
  manual/model CE hold.
- **B:** mean-only damage on positive copy cells is at least.05 nat per corpus.
- **C:** heads5+7 recover at least50% of positive-cell mean damage and exceed
  their matched-negative recovery by at least.15 per corpus.
- **D:** heads6+7+3 recover at least50% of matched-negative mean damage and
  exceed their positive-cell recovery by at least.15 per corpus.
- **E:** shared head7 recovers at least20% in both cell types and corpora, and
  all-nine restoration matches native cell CE within.005 nat.

Passing C/D/E would separate an induction/copy correction from broader content
routing at a task-defined endpoint. Any failure preserves the measured cell
effects but rejects this head-group split; no subset or threshold is changed
after outcomes. This is not another rank fit, generic document-CE quotient,
installed surrogate, or complete attention5 circuit.
