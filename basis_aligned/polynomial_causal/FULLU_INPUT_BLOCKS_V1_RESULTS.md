## Executed red-team continuation: remove the fixed-basis restriction

Using the note's notation, $f(P)=tc/[a(t-a)]$. Its Euclidean derivative is

$$
\nabla f(P)=\frac{t}{a(t-a)}[K-2\Phi(P)]
-\frac{ct(t-2a)}{a^2(t-a)^2}K.
$$

The projector-manifold gradient is

$$
\operatorname{grad}f=P\nabla f+\nabla fP-2P\nabla fP.

preserving rank and orthogonality. The new CPU check
