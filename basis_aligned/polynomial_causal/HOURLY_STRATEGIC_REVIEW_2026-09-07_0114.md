## Circuit interpretation targets and full goal

A completed circuit must specify what is read, computed, written, and consumed; group or split
across native heads/modules according to causal function; predict held-out and OOD behavior; support
extraction or a precise background-conditioned interface; permit selective removal, swap, and edit;
compose and reuse shared subcomputations; and remain identified across documents, constructions,
controls, gauges, and restarts. The program-level objective remains a smaller transparent tensor
program that is jointly predictive, composable, manipulable, and cheaper in literal storage,
compute, state, edges, and intervention price. Neither low-rank reconstruction nor low CE alone is
completion.

## Route decision and confounds

The highest-information route remains full response patching guided by the canonical weight modes.
It can turn weight incidence into cross-task causal writer/reader membership, or falsify that use of
weights. Another lambda/noise/KL sweep is lower value: control penalties are demonstrably
family-specific, and complete-family selection already rejects learned movement. The next DAS
advance must change the observed operator—multiple environments, downstream readers, and
full-vocabulary effects—not merely retune the same scalar loss.

Confounds audited:

- Baseline subtraction: candidate patches will include exact base self-patch and full donor-minus-base
  denominators, with signed projections and vector cosines rather than ratios on individual tiny rows.
- Frame mixing: temporal and is/was task rows remain separately reported; pooling is used only for a
  frozen canonical basis.
- Nonlinear composition: complete head/module responses are patched and the network recomputes all
  later layers; combinations will be executed, never inferred by adding singleton scores.
- Shared token difficulty: task/family panels and layer-matched low heads are explicit controls.
- Leakage/post-selection: candidate layers L6/L7/L9 and every one of their 27 heads plus three MLPs
  are frozen from the weight atlas before causal outcomes; no winner-only test.
- Precision/noise: the physical-projector diagnostic is float64, while scientific interventions remain
  the model's native float32 and retain exact replay controls.
- Native boundaries: heads/modules are screened as full response carriers, not assumed semantic
  atoms; a positive distributed result triggers greedy combinations and a negative result demotes the
  weight atlas to incidence only.

Different serious alternatives are (i) direct finite response patching, (ii) causal-response/Hankel
minimal realization, (iii) predictive-state quotients learned across readers, and (iv) tangent/Sobolev
matching of interventions. The finite patch atlas dominates now because it directly tests the missing
causal-membership premise cheaply. Hankel realization already supplied the canonical modes;
predictive-state and tangent objectives become useful only if full patches show that fixed weight
incidence fails under context.
