# Numeric sequence payload-router interaction v1

## Question

At the exact block-8 H3+H7 final-source term, does fresh digit/number-word `+1` continuation use the already identified shared cached numeric payload alone, the final-source score router alone, or their multiplicative joint substitution? Does any successful arm remain selective against capable digit and number-word copy behavior?

## Frozen rows and intervention

Bind all 32 capability-passed nonadjacent `+1` pairs and the 32 digit/word copy pairs from the earlier spaced authority. There are 128 unique endpoints. For every row and both directions, exactly decompose block-8 H3+H7 at the final query and final numeric source. Recompute the native suffix under three arms:

1. `score`: donor final-source score times the recipient's native complete value;
2. `cached`: recipient score times recipient own-value plus donor cached value;
3. `joint`: donor score times recipient own-value plus donor cached value.

All other heads, sources, layers, and terms remain native. The execution costs three forwards and 640 sequence evaluations: native and exact-factor replay over 128 unique endpoints, then one batch of 384 interventions. There are no gains, fits, row filters, ranks, or quantization.

## Frozen bars

- A: every input/capability hash and checkpoint matches; native replay, head-source sum, and cached decomposition relative squared errors are at most `1e-10`; installed-term error is at most `1e-5`; all interventions are nonzero; price is exact.
- B: `cached` transfers the `+1` answer in every construction×representation×direction cell: donorward fraction at least 0.75, donor-answer wins at least 0.50, and positive mean donor-margin effect and donor CE gain.
- C: `joint` passes the same transfer bars and its mean effect is at least 1.10 times the larger of `score` and `cached` in every construction×representation×direction cell. This is the frozen multiplicative-interaction predicate.
- D: at least one arm that passes all target cells also passes every matching copy cell: recipient-answer preservation at least 0.85, absolute mean recipient CE change at most 0.10, median absolute partner-margin change at most 0.25 of that arm's target median, and median intervention norm at least 0.10 of that arm's target median.

A/B/D supports a selective cached relation carrier. A/C/D supports a selective multiplicative router interaction. A score-only arm that clears every target and control cell is reported separately as a selective score router. Any target-passing arm with no selective arm supports a shared final-source factor without relation selectivity. Relation state is outside these factors only if no arm clears every target cell. No partial construction, representation, direction, arm, threshold, row, head, or gain rescue is licensed.
