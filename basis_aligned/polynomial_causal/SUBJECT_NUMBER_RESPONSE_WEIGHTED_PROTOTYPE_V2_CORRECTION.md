was invalid by its own preregistered trust-region instrument: the four candidate
prototype norms were `2.437`, `5.430`, `3.146`, and `4.391` times their fixed
mean-prototype norms, above the declared maximum of `2.0`. The V1 receipt and
runner are preserved as invalid evidence. Its favorable scientific metrics do
not license a claim.

V2 makes one prospective correction before rerunning: increase the response
ridge fraction from `1e-2` to `1.0`. All other choices remain frozen: rank 16,
one response-Jacobian fit per fold/direction, eight deterministic permutation
controls, parent coefficient maps, replay tolerances, scientific gates, and the
`2.0` prototype-norm capability gate. This is shrinkage in the already-declared
causal-response metric, not a fit to behavioral outcomes. There is no ridge
sweep and V2 is the only corrected arm.

The interpretation remains discovery-only on opened constructions. A valid
positive result still requires freezing an all-row prototype and testing fresh
text with causal substitution, removal/null selectivity, and composition.
