On the capability-passed nonadjacent authority, does the exact block-8 H3+H7 final-source cached-value term carry a bidirectional numbered-list successor state while preserving digit and number-word copy behavior?

## Frozen intervention

Use the 48 capability rows without changes. At each endpoint, exactly decompose block-8 attention. For heads 3 and 7 only, retain the recipient's native final-source attention score and own-value term, replace the final-source cached-value term with its paired donor term, and recompute the complete native suffix. Evaluate both base-to-donor and donor-to-base directions. No gain, fit, row selection, vector extraction, threshold search, rank, or quantization is allowed.

The execution costs three forwards and 288 sequence evaluations: a native pass over 96 endpoints, an exact-factor capture/replay pass over those endpoints, and one batched pass over 96 interventions.
