## New evidence

### Geometry is not a causal selector

The six-component replication gives full-direction cosine versus causal cross-effect
Spearman correlations of +0.4212 at a8, +0.4198 at a16, -0.5411 at m16, and
-0.6823 at m14. After removing each component's common direction, the maximum
absolute correlation over all six components is only 0.1773.

Thus similar activation directions can have opposite causal relationships. HOSVD and
cosine remain useful initializers or descriptions, but are falsified as a general
hierarchy-selection criterion.

### M16 DAS is deliberately mixed evidence

The completed 375-second M16 run passed optimizer-health controls for all six rank-one
fits: each moved 0.999–1.000 from initialization and reduced its objective. Learned
directions were less geometrically entangled than registered: mean absolute cosine was
0.3976, versus a preregistered lower bar of 0.50 and closed-form residual 0.5185.
But only **1/6** was causally selective at the 10% margin, versus 2/6 closed-form.

Therefore the strong claim that these are one geometrically identical direction
failed, while the claim that rank-one DAS does not separate the six causal uses
survived. Neither result establishes one mechanism, six mechanisms, or a reusable DAG.
The artifact's fraction_of_full_recovered exceeds 1 for several fits, so it is an
intervention-effect ratio here, not variance explained.

The registered minus-outlier follow-up then completed in 322 seconds. All five fits
again passed health controls. Mean absolute cosine remained low at 0.3896, but **0/5**
directions was causally selective at the 10% margin. Thus the lone 1/6 selective
direction was indeed the selectivity outlier. The best current statement is:
rank-one learned directions at M16 are geometrically distinguishable but do not yield
editable circuit-specific causal children. Calling them either one mechanism or five
mechanisms would go beyond the measurement.

## What is explained and what is not

There are three different levels:

1. **Exact local algebra.** MLP0's folded token-token, token-context, and
   context-context polynomial expansion is essentially exact. Equality fetching can
   also be replayed exactly.
2. **Compression.** A certified whole-program replacement removes 5.35% of stored
   values. Several local rank-512 maps and enriched rank-1/rank-4 directions are useful
   descriptions but are not independently editable programs.
3. **Causal terminal explanation.** Only 10.92% of the strict deletion-CE ledger is
   named, and no action passes extraction, selective removal, collateral, and OOD.

The largest gaps are:

- 89.08% residual causal CE;
- no signed, additive source-by-target-by-document causal coordinate system yet;
- separately promising MLP0/1/2 reductions do not yet compose into one executable
  early-layer program across residual/RMSNorm interfaces;
- equality/induction extraction and OOD prediction work, but an induction-only mask
  counts other equality-copy uses as collateral;
- exact local replay does not guarantee OOD behavior under code, length, vocabulary,
  or structural shifts;
- low-rank factors can have non-gauge null directions, making edits basis-dependent.

### 3. Repair and re-audit bracket closure receipt atomicity

Bracket closure has an exact dense adapter and repaired lifecycle, so its marginal
path to a terminal extraction/removal/OOD result is shorter than new discovery. It
also tests whether L13H8 is a reusable parent shared with quote closure.

The independent outcome-blind re-audit completed after this ranking and returned

late-failure tests proving that terminal-only state is impossible.
