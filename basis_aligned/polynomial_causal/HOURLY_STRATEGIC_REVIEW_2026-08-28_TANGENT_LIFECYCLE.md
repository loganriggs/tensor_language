1. **No measured cross-depth early-MLP state.** We do not know whether MLP0--2 effects
   pass through a stable low-dimensional causal state, or whether the successful local
   codes use incompatible gauges/interfaces.
2. **Semantic quotient remains unearned.** The latest circuit audit's
   `pred_a_profile_discriminates` failed. Token classes or feature clusters cannot be
   called semantic merely because their activations are geometrically close.
3. **Finite edits remain outside the tangent certificate.** A low tangent tail is exact

4. **Rank640 is brittle off the natural manifold.** It passes all 8 natural-prefix

   OOD/capacity gap, not evidence against its natural-row admission.
5. **Consequence harnesses are not yet applied to a shared MLP state.** Extraction,
   selective removal, collateral damage, and OOD transport still cannot validate a
   proposed MLP simplicity definition.
