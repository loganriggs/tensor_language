## Candidate pruning

The tangent realization survives because it measures a causal input/output operator
through the complete residual/RMSNorm/attention path, composes at a cut, has sharp
split/rank/gap falsifiers, and distinguishes a shared linear dictionary from
context-specific coordinates at bounded GPU cost.

A metric-aware physical-subspace comparison is retained but sequenced after the pilot:
mapping coefficient projectors through the direction matrices can strengthen the
gauge claim, but cannot rescue an unstable operator. Nonlinear state identification
and sparse polynomial synthesis are retained only if the linear hypothesis fails or
supplies a small candidate state.

Further token clustering, isolated semantic correlations, local-MSE fits, attention
rank sweeps, and position-wise refinements are pruned because they duplicate completed
work or cannot test composition. Full Hankel/automata synthesis is deferred: continuous
contextual dynamics and RMSNorm violate the cheapest finite-state assumptions, while
the tangent operator is the cheaper local realization test.
