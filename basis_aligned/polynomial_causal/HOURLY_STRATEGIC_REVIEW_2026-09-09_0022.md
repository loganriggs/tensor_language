## Circuit interpretation targets and full goal

A useful circuit decomposition must supply seven things: (1) a computational specification of what
is read, computed, written, and consumed; (2) grouping across native module boundaries and splitting
within them according to operational equivalence; (3) held-out and OOD prediction; (4) extraction or
sufficiency of an executable interface; (5) selective removal, swap, and edit evidence that accounts
for redundancy and interactions; (6) predictable composition and reuse; and (7) stable
identification across corpora, gauges, and fitting restarts. The full goal is still a transparent
tensor program that is jointly predictive, composable, manipulable, and literally simpler under
storage, compute, edge, and state prices. Rank, reconstruction, variance, and CE are supporting
controls or prices, not substitutes for circuit evidence.
