pieces across native boundaries and split mixed heads/MLPs by computation; predict
held-out and OOD effects; expose an executable sufficient interface; support selective

remain stable across corpora, gauges, and fitting restarts. The program-level target
remains a smaller literal tensor program that is jointly predictive, composable,
manipulable, and simpler in storage, compute, state, edges, and description.
