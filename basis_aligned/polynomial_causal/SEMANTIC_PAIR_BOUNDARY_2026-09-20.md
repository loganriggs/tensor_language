# Origin of semantic source-pair interactions

This is a causal boundary screen on the now-opened96 new-construction rows. Previous work shows all three group-B pairs predict native finite intervention effects within1.99%, while frozen two-pair sparsity and native capability gates fail. The question here is where to fold the required interactions.

Let F be block11, h the unmodified pre11 state, d_i the selected source/site edits, and g the entire suffix12–17 plus final normalized, softcapped number readout. All baseline reinjection and first-value contexts are fixed exactly as in the previous intervention.

Define y0=F(h), delta_i=F(h+d_i)-y0, and eta_ij=F(h+d_i+d_j)-y0-delta_i-delta_j. The full output damage is E_ij=g(y0)-g(y0+delta_i+delta_j+eta_ij). Singles are E_i=g(y0)-g(y0+delta_i). Their pair interaction is J_ij=E_ij-E_i-E_j.

Evaluate the counterfactual with eta omitted: E_sum=g(y0)-g(y0+delta_i+delta_j). Then J_suffix=E_sum-E_i-E_j and J_boundary=E_ij-E_sum, with J=J_suffix+J_boundary exactly. The boundary term includes nonlinear downstream transport of eta in the presence of both individual responses. This is an ordered decomposition, not a unique attribution or proof that g is linear. All RMS operations and two-QK attention remain native.

Competing registered predictions: J_suffix predicts J within20% relative error for every material pair/cell, or J_boundary does so. Material means ||J||>=1% of the native combined B-effect norm in that cell. At least one material cell required. Either or both dominance claims may fail. Report absolute pair/B ratios alongside relative interaction errors, so cancellation does not become an explanation by default.

Instrument: native previous effects replay within absolute1e-4 and exact counts24prefix/120block11/184post11-suffix batch calls. Zero fits. This is full native causal work, not a smaller extracted executable. The native agreement failures from the prior panel remain failures.
