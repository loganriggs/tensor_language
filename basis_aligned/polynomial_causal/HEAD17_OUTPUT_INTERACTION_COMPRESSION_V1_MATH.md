Let \(W\in\mathbb R^{1152\times128}\) be the actual head17.2 output map. Let \(O\in\mathbb R^{6\times1152}\) contain the six frozen UK-minus-US unembedding rows used by the regional panel. The MLP17 numerator change under \(z\mapsto z+Wh\) is

$$
O\big[B_{17}(z+Wh)-B_{17}(z)\big]=T(z,h)+S(h,h).
$$

For \(C=OD\),

$$
T_{oia}=\sum_k C_{ok}\big[L_{ki}(RW)_{ka}+R_{ki}(LW)_{ka}\big],
$$

$$
S_{oab}=\frac12\sum_k C_{ok}\big[(LW)_{ka}(RW)_{kb}+(LW)_{kb}(RW)_{ka}\big].
$$

## Scope and strongest alternative explanations

The input \(h\) here is arbitrary. In the actual retained predictor it is generated by the three attention contractions, coupled to the residual background and normalization. This coefficient probe has not yet exploited that producer constraint or its graph. Failure on this broader domain is not failure to compress the retained circuit. Shared one-mode projection optima also do not settle unrestricted LL1 factors or sparse arithmetic topology.

The output tensor uses **contrasts before final normalization and softcap**. Because the two token logits are saturated separately, their raw difference alone is insufficient to reconstruct the final margin: the common logit level also matters. A behavioral compressed implementation must retain both individual token readers (or an equivalent mean-plus-difference interface) and the final norm. Similarly, the MLP input denominator and its changing-baseline correction remain explicit. Calling these coefficients a finished behavioral circuit would omit real computations.

Next: establish the twelve-token or equivalent mean/contrast interface before optimizing richer shared blocks, and fold the actual retained attention producer rather than interpreting arbitrary-port spectra as decisive. Native validation must compare to both the retained uncompressed predictor and the original model at the same boundary.
