## Circuit interpretation targets

A useful decomposition must specify what information is read, what operation combines it, what is written, and which
later computations use it. It must be able to merge pieces across attention heads or MLPs when downstream computation
treats them as one variable, and split a native module when its pieces serve different tasks. It must predict held-out
and shifted inputs, support an executable circuit or explicit interface, permit selective removal, substitution, and
editing without unrelated damage, compose predictably, and remain stable across documents, corpora, fitting restarts,
and plausible gauges—or be defined directly by downstream operational equivalence. Literal storage, compute, edge,
state, and program-description costs matter for eventual adoption, but compression alone is not interpretation.

The full goal remains a smaller transparent tensor program that is predictive on fresh and OOD text, composable when
its replacements are installed together, selectively manipulable, and literally simpler.

## Is this the highest-information route?

Yes. It directly operationalizes the user's proposed interaction-determined basis: “same” means later computation
cannot distinguish two changes under held-out causal tests. It can group different source pairs and different score
implementations, or honestly return no pairwise quotient. Unlike another sparse or low-rank fit, every positive edge
has an immediate physical interchange experiment and every negative result closes a precise equivalence class.

4. **Signed multi-term composition algebra.** If no pair is equivalent, search only preregistered small combinations
