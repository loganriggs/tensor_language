## Circuit interpretation targets

A useful decomposition must eventually:

1. specify what information is read, what operation combines it, what is written, and which later computation uses it;
2. group pieces across native heads/MLPs when they have the same downstream role and split native modules when their
   pieces serve different computations;
3. predict held-out and genuinely shifted text;
4. extract an executable sufficient computation or an explicit computation-plus-background interface;
5. support selective removal, interchange, and editing while active unrelated controls remain intact;
6. compose and reuse shared subcomputations predictably;
7. retain stable identity across documents, fitting restarts, and gauge changes or be defined by downstream operational
   equivalence.

The full goal remains a smaller transparent tensor program that is predictive, composable, manipulable, and literally
simpler in storage, compute, edges, and state. Rank, variance, reconstruction, and CE are prices or controls, not circuit
identity.
