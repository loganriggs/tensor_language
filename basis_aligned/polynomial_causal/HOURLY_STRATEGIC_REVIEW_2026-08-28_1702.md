## What the newest results mean

### Cost simplification is real but not sufficient

The table/map factorization gives an exact algebraic constraint

$$
\operatorname{rank}(W_s) \leq \operatorname{rank}(\text{token table}) + 1.
$$

This corrected the rank-1 program's price to 0.485M stored values. That is a valid
implementation simplification. It is not a faithful explanation: rank 1 loses
1.346--1.393 CE nats relative to rank 64. CE penalizes rank truncation 3.9--4.4 times
more strongly than top-1 does when both are expressed as lost progress toward the live
model.

Nevertheless, the cost--CE and cost--top-1 Pareto sets are the same nine programs on
all three roles. “Pareto” means that no retained program is both cheaper and better
than another retained program. Three of four full-rank partial programs are dominated
on both axes, so structured truncation can improve cost without depending on which
fidelity metric is chosen.

### Deeper partial compilation really approaches the model

For rank-64 programs, extending the exact live suffix from no live layers to layer 13
raises top-1 agreement with the live model from about 22.4% to 64.2% and lowers
$\mathrm{KL}(p_{\rm live}\Vert p_{\rm program})$ from about 3.04 to 1.04 nats.
Agreement with the live model is different from correctness against the next token;
it shows that the program increasingly reproduces the live model's mistakes as well
as its successes.

Across all 12 depth/role cells, the live-to-program KL is within 0.7% of the program's
extra CE relative to live. Algebraically, their expected difference is controlled by
the data distribution, and empirically the equality says there is no additional
model-facing success hidden by the task CE. Improving CE is therefore not optional:
for this family it is almost exactly improving distributional imitation of bilin18.

### The early interface remains the main causal uncertainty

MLP0--2 account for 0.7277 of 0.8727 held-out global ship nats and 1.0776 of 1.176
novel-rare nats in the existing Shapley allocation. Interactions contribute 43--64%
of measured token-cell effects. MLP2 can compensate for MLP0/1 errors, so independent
local fits can look good and fail when combined. A context-free substitution also
caused 74--153x consumer-norm explosions at attention layers 5/6. The polynomial
program retains context and may avoid that failure, but it must demonstrate this in
the observed suffix rather than inherit credit by analogy.
