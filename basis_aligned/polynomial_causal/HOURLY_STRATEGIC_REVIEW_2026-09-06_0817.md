## Circuit interpretation targets and full goal

The circuit standard remains: (1) specify reads, operations, writes, and downstream users; (2) group causally equivalent pieces across native modules and split native modules where functions differ; (3) predict held-out and OOD activations and effects; (4) extract a sufficient executable interface; (5) support selective removals, swaps, and edits; (6) predict composition and reuse; and (7) remain stable across corpus, gauge, and fitting choices. The program-level goal is still a smaller transparent tensor program that is jointly predictive on fresh/OOD text, composable under joint replacement, manipulable under registered counterfactuals, and simpler under literal state/storage/compute/edge price. The aspectual circuit is bounded to one behavior and does not implement raw text to `resid:10`, unrestricted syntax, or a whole-model replacement; the durable goal is not complete.
