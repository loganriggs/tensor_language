## Immediate goal and interpretation targets

The immediate goal is a reusable codebase that can produce hundreds of high-quality, nonduplicated circuit records,
at roughly one basic causal screen or honest null per ten serial minutes. A useful circuit must eventually say what
information is read, what computation is performed, what is written, and what downstream computation uses it. It
must allow components to be grouped across native heads or MLPs and split within them; predict held-out and OOD
behavior; support extraction and selective interventions; compose with other circuits; and remain stable across data
splits and plausible changes of basis. The program-level goal remains a smaller transparent tensor program that is
predictive, composable, manipulable, and simple. Rank or reconstruction alone satisfies none of these circuit goals.
