# Rung500 preregistration — named MLP9 reader calibration for the copy-score relation

**Registered:** 2026-09-02 18:13 UTC

**Parents:** rung459's independently selected MLP9 response, rung499's prospective task-positive/general-circuit-null
result.

**Claim level:** prospective calibration of one named downstream reader. A pass says MLP9 distinguishes the known
score computation from payload and wrong-score controls on current text/actions. It is not a new circuit discovery,
an MLP decomposition, or compression.

## Why this observation and why it is prospective

Rung499 prospectively reproduced the L5H5-score replacement on copy-task CE, but nine general circuit coordinates
could not consistently distinguish that score from L5H5's payload. The registered null route requires changing the
observation set before searching candidates.

Rung459 independently selected the MLP9 write response on different rows before the current census existed. For the
same recipient, its held natural-text response cosine was`.844` for L5H5 score, while its fit response cosines were
`.116` for L5H5 payload and`-.803` for L7H3 score. Rung500 freezes MLP9 as the only primary reader; it does not search
MLP8/12 or any other layer.

Documents500:1000 and their final CE outcomes were used by rung499, but no MLP9 write under these finite actions was
captured or stored. Rung500's reader outcomes are therefore new. The scorer hash-checks but does not deserialize the
rung499 outcome-bearing result or bundle.

## Computation

Repeat rung499's exact two donors, two early backgrounds, and five late states with frozen rung459 RMS scales. A
forward hook records the1,152-dimensional write produced by MLP9 at every token; the complete remaining model still
runs so the intervention is physically identical.

For each action, task mask, and fixed quarter, let `W_absent`, `W_native`, and `W_hybrid` be MLP9 writes and define

`r_native = W_absent - W_native`,

`r_hybrid = W_absent - W_hybrid`.

Compare these vectors over selected tokens by cosine and the remaining RMS error after fitting one positive scale.
Also report their RMS relative to MLP9's native write. The primary copy mask is rung499's exact nearest-predecessor
copy task. Non-copy equality and all non-copy scored positions are fixed specificity controls.

## Predictions

### A — exact and live reader instrument

All frozen hashes and parent verdicts match; native/replay logits and MLP9 writes match exactly; factor reconstruction
relative-squared error is at most`1e-10`; every action edit is live; every task cell has support; MLP9 is captured
exactly once per forward; and the price is125 native plus2,375 analytical forwards. No rung499 result/bundle is
deserialized. Every native and hybrid copy-task response has RMS at least`1e-4` of MLP9's native write RMS.

### B — MLP9 reads the known score relation

For the L5H5 score hybrid, both quarters and both early backgrounds have copy-task response cosine at least`.75` and
positive-scale residual at most`.70`. These are rung459/rung498's existing named-response bars.

### C — the named reader rejects score and payload controls

In every quarter/background cell, the L5H5 score response beats both L7H3 score and L5H5 payload by at least`.30`
cosine or`.30` lower scaled residual. The same positive must win throughout; no cell-wise reader or threshold choice.
The margin is smaller than rung459's observed score-versus-payload cosine gap`.728` and score-versus-L7 gap`1.647`,
but materially excludes the weak separation seen in rung499's generic circuit battery.

### D — reader relation is stable under earlier-service removal

Within each quarter, the L5 score hybrid responses under early-present and early-absent backgrounds have cosine at
least`.75`. Their positive scales relative to the corresponding native response differ by no more than50%. Both
backgrounds must independently satisfy B.

### E — the reader is copy-task selective

For the L5 score hybrid in every quarter/background cell, copy-task cosine exceeds both the non-copy-equality cosine
and all-non-copy-position cosine by at least`.10`. This prevents a globally similar MLP9 perturbation from being
called a copy reader.

### F — interpretation

F requires A--E. A pass calibrates MLP9's write response as a named observation that separates the known shared score
from payload and wrong-score donors across finite actions. Only then may a separate four-score directed search use
MLP9 plus task CE as its frozen observation. A pass still does not prove that all of MLP9, or any internal product,
is the circuit; MLP9 is a downstream reader used to identify the upstream score relation.

## Nulls and price

A failure repairs only the instrument. B failure means the old MLP9 reader does not transfer to this corpus/action
table. C failure means MLP9 does not discriminate the computation here. D failure makes the relation background-
conditional. E failure means MLP9 sees a generic perturbation rather than a copy-selective one. None licenses another
reader search, rank, sparsity, SAE, quantization, or compression sweep.

The literal price is2,500 full-model forwards, zero fitted or deployed parameters, and sufficient statistics only;
raw tokens, logits, and MLP9 vectors are not stored.
