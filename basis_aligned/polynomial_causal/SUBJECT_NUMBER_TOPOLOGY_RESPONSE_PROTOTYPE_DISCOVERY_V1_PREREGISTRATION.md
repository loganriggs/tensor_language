# Subject-number topology-conditioned response prototype discovery V1

Registered after the complete-generator two-site null and its post-hoc open-slot
audit. This is opened-authority discovery, not fresh OOD evidence. It uses the
already-open 16-row/two-template singular-singular two-clause panel and reads no
answer logits or downstream intervention outcomes.

At each registered subject position (5 and 14), form an opposite-number role by
replacing only that one-token singular subject with its one-token plural mate.
Capture native and opposite MLP8 raw states and reconstruct the native L11H3 head
function. The exact site-local displacement is the opposite-minus-native MLP8
state. Changing only the subject token must replay the captured opposite head
within `5e-5`; failure makes the experiment invalid.

For each site, leave one of the two templates out. On the eight training rows,
retain the full centered displacement span (maximum rank seven), start from the
mean displacement, and ridge-fit its exact native-axis response residual using
the input gradient of the reconstructed head response. The ridge fraction is
`1.0`, matching the valid response-weighted V2 method. Compare on the eight held-out
rows with the site-conditioned mean, the frozen single-clause prototype, and eight
response-permutation controls. No coefficient law, head axis, template, row,
threshold, rank, or ridge may be selected after outcomes are seen.

Instrumentation requires exactly two physical forwards over 64 sequences, 32
input-gradient rows, native and opposite head replay at most `5e-5`, input closure
at most `5e-5`, four finite rank-seven fits, and every candidate norm at most twice
its corresponding mean norm.

Across both sites and held-out templates, the topology-conditioned candidate must:

1. predict exact response `s` with cosine at least `.90` and relative L2 error at
   most `.40`;
2. improve response error over the frozen single-clause prototype by at least `.30`;
3. predict the exact-response coefficient under the frozen `[1,z,s,zs] beta` law
   with relative L2 error at most `.40`;
4. predict the independently effective fixed two-site coefficient with relative
   L2 error at most `.50` and sign agreement at least `.90`; and
5. beat the median of eight response-permutation controls on fixed-coefficient
   error by at least `.05`.

Passing licenses an all-opened-row topology-conditioned prototype freeze before
authoring a new two-clause corpus. Failure of item 1 localizes the problem to the
prototype family; passing item 1 but failing items 3 or 4 localizes it to transport
of the scalar law. Neither outcome may be promoted to behavioral OOD evidence.
