## New evidence and confusing results

### 1. Exact map-rank simplification is real, but does not solve fidelity

If a context-free token table has rank $r$ plus its mean, the learned
embedding-to-row map factors through that table and therefore has

$$
\operatorname{rank}(W_s)\leq r+1.
$$

Charging a rank-64 map to a rank-1 table was an algebraic overcount. The corrected
rank-1 program stores 0.485M values rather than 5.628M, an exact cost correction with
no extra approximation. This is a genuine certified simplification of that program's
implementation.

The completed CE run shows why it is not yet a useful reverse engineering result:

| Table rank | Stored values | CE skip7000 | CE skip11000 | CE skip1200 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.485M | 7.51892 | 7.51560 | 7.53754 |
| 2 | 0.805M | 6.96082 | 6.99527 | 6.95063 |
| 4 | 1.444M | 6.62422 | 6.63689 | 6.59044 |
| 8 | 2.722M | 6.47177 | 6.47693 | 6.43090 |
| 64 | 20.531M | 6.17330 | 6.15261 | 6.14463 |

Rank 1 retains 77--79% of rank-64 top-1 agreement yet loses 1.34562, 1.36299, and
1.39291 CE nats. CE improves monotonically with rank. The preregistered prediction
that CE-per-real would select rank at most 4 failed; under the run's definition rank
64 wins on all three roles. Top-1, CE, storage, and causal fidelity are measuring
different things, and none may stand in for another.

### 2. The first three MLPs remain the dominant compositional gap

Held-out MLP0--2 Shapley allocation remains 0.7277 of 0.8727 global ship nats and
1.0776 of 1.176 nats on novel-rare tokens. Interactions account for 43--64% of measured
token-cell effects. MLP2 can compensate for upstream changes, so independently good
MLP0, MLP1, and MLP2 approximations need not compose.

### 3. A live suffix can turn a local substitution into an explosion

The context-free compiler produced live-consumer output norms 74--153 times their
native-input values at attention L5/L6. This was not repaired by norm matching. The
polynomial program may avoid this because it retains context, but that is a hypothesis,
not an exemption. Per-layer consumer norm ratios are therefore mandatory integrity
reductions in the new final capability.

### 4. The current missing interface is typed capability, not data

The suffix lifecycle, exactly-once final load, semantic envelope, replay checks, and
student poison pass 224 CPU tests. FineWeb, checkpoint, caches, `rspd`, and hardware
are present. The current adapter is validation-phase locked and has no typed final
intervention/gauge operation. Giving a callback raw rows/model access would defeat the
source closure; leaving the callback to return arbitrary booleans would not constitute
observed evidence.
