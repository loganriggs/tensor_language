# Bracket embedded-pending compact behavior V1

The donor-free rank-two L13H8 source generator transferred prospectively on a fifth construction. Separately, an immutable six-scalar ordered-closer-pair law, averaged from two earlier construction tables, transferred on the third construction. This test combines those frozen objects on a sixth construction without changing either.

`BRACKET_EMBEDDED_PENDING_OOD_V1_ROWS.json` was built before model access. It contains 36 answer-changing inner-opener rows and 36 answer-preserving middle-opener controls over twelve new lexical groups and a new embedded surface, balanced to twelve endpoints in each of six directed closer-pair cells.

Load the exact rank-two payload, key1, and key2 tables serialized by the fifth-construction result. For each sixth-construction recipient, rotate the donor-type keys at the live opener position, contract them with the live recipient queries, multiply the two score factors, and apply the rank-two payload difference to obtain a donor-free L13H8 source term. No donor activation enters this arm. Compare its native-suffix effect to exact `p_d*u_d` only as a ceiling.

Independently dispatch the immutable six-scalar table by ordered answer pair. Compare that scalar prediction both to the exact donor-term causal effect and to the donor-free program's causal effect. The scalar path uses neither donor state nor the native suffix; it is the proposed complete compact behavioral prediction. No row, program coordinate, scalar, gain, rank, threshold, or fit may change.

Price is four forwards over 144 endpoints: native, exact factor replay, exact joint ceiling, and donor-free program. This is 576 sequence evaluations, zero fits/backwards/updates/quantization.

Frozen bars:

- **A — instrument and capability:** replay error at most `1e-5`, all three program ranks equal two, exact 4/576 accounting, and every native target/control cell has accuracy at least `0.75` with positive mean margin.
- **B — exact parent:** exact donor-term effects are positive on at least `0.90` of every ordered-pair cell.
- **C — donor-free source program:** program versus exact effects has overall cosine at least `0.90`, relative L2 error at most `0.40`, sign agreement at least `0.90`, norm ratio in `[0.60,1.40]`, and each pair has cosine at least `0.75`, relative L2 at most `0.50`, and sign agreement at least `0.85`.
- **D — six-scalar exact-effect prediction:** scalar versus exact has cosine at least `0.80`, relative L2 at most `0.60`, sign agreement at least `0.90`, norm ratio in `[0.50,1.50]`; each pair has cosine at least `0.90`, relative L2 at most `0.65`, and sign agreement at least `0.90`.
- **E — six-scalar program-effect prediction:** the same bars hold against the donor-free program effect.
- **F — controls:** donor-free control RMS is at most `0.50` of exact target RMS.

All predicates passing yields `compact_source_and_suffix_behavior_transfer`. If A–C and F pass but either scalar predicate fails, the terminal is `fixed_suffix_scalar_null`: preserve the donor-free source generator and require a live source-amplitude interaction in the suffix law. Any other capable scientific failure is `compact_behavior_null`; failed replay/rank/accounting is invalid and failed capability is `sixth_construction_capability_null`.
