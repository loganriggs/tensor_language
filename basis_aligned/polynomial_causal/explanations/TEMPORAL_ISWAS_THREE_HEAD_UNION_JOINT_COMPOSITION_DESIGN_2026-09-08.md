# Three-head union joint composition

The full head/module factorial is an honest singleton-identification null: no one parent module
reaches 0.50 recovery. Its separately preregistered distributivity clause passes strongly. The
fixed L9H1+L11H3+L15H5 union recovers about 0.71/0.75 of temporal and 0.52/0.49 of is-was effects,
with union-minus-singleton-sum errors 0.024–0.057 and negligible cross-role collateral.

This successor treats that union as one distributed program. On every licensed native cell it runs
temporal-only, is-was-only, and simultaneous swaps at the two distinct answer-query positions.
The simultaneous arm uses the same independent counterfactual donors as the two single arms, so
the exact four-arm Möbius interaction measures cross-command composition rather than changing the
intervention definition. Canonical margins are scored at both answers on every arm.

Passing requires material single-command recovery, strict cross-role selectivity, interaction ratio
at most 0.10, additive cosine at least 0.99 in every phase/template, and no more than 0.05 loss when
each command is installed simultaneously. This can identify a shared physical interface carrying
task-typed states. It cannot repair or replace the parent's failed singleton/module claim.
