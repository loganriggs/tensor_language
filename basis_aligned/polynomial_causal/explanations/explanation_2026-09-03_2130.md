## Short answer

Yes. The useful unit is not five agents independently scoring five circuits; it is a staged builder–critic loop in
which each completed circuit investigation produces code, counterfactual patterns, active controls, and failure tests
that the next agents inherit. I started with two subagents as requested while doing one deeper integration track myself.

The first cycle has already improved the process materially:

- one agent found, before model execution, that a proposed R585 comparison divided by a quantity that is exactly zero
  on every relevant answer-preserving example;
- independent tests of R584 found missing-row acceptance, non-finite JSON, incomplete row-level evidence, and weak
  provenance before its GPU run;
- the clean R586 capability replication is now independently audited rather than accepted from its own summary; and
- every later prompt now inherits machine-readable family names, exact cell censuses, frozen factor caching, semantic
  token positions, strict result types, and active controls.

That is the bootstrap: failures become executable tests and prompt requirements, not merely warnings in a report.

## The newly audited positive result

R586 repeated the native induction capability test over 3,024 unique prompts. The model sees two possible source
matches and two possible payload assignments, giving four conditions:

$$
(s,p)\in\{(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)\}.
$$

Here (s) says which source matches the final query and (p) says which payload is attached to that source. Correct
answer rates were 77.8%, 88.9%, 94.4%, and 86.1% on FIT, and 80.6%, 91.7%, 91.7%, and 88.9% on SELECT. The average
selector-by-payload interaction was positive by at least 3.05 logits on FIT and 3.06 logits on SELECT at the lower end
of the 95% bootstrap interval. On selected-source match breaks, the expected answer-margin damage was positive in
97.9% and 98.6% of rows.

R587 independently rebuilt 3,240 saved rows, 108 factorial groups, 432 condition effects, and 86 bootstrap cells with
2,000 group resamples per cell. It reproduced every scientific decision, verified the exact 95-forward execution,
FIT/SELECT-only opening, zero gradient computations and weight updates, and the byte-level result/receipt binding.
The audit verdict is `held_independent_audit`. This establishes a sound behavioral target for R585; it does not yet
identify the internal attention factors.

## What the downstream MLP experiment found

R584 starts from the exact broad numeric signal found earlier and asks how a later bilinear MLP uses it. If (x^-)
is the normalized MLP input without that signal, (x^+=x^-+\delta) is the input with it, and

$$
M(x)=D[(Lx)\odot(Rx)],
$$

then the exact output difference is

$$
M(x^+)-M(x^-)
=\underbrace{D[(L\delta)\odot(Rx^-)+(Lx^-)\odot(R\delta)]}_{C:\ \text{signal combined with the rest of the state}}
+\underbrace{D[(L\delta)\odot(R\delta)]}_{Q:\ \text{signal combined with itself}}.
$$

The experiment separately removed (C), (Q), and (C+Q) at MLPs 8, 10, 12, and 14. This is an exact decomposition
of the local MLP response, not a low-rank approximation.

No component passed the full FIT gate, so SELECT remained closed. The strongest candidate, (C+Q) at MLP8, damaged
the intended successor output in 10 of 12 cells and showed a successor-versus-copy gap in 9 of 12. But it preserved
copy behavior in 0 of 12 cells and was stable across the two source roles in only 3 of 6 cells. All 12 active-control
cells passed, so this is not a dead-intervention null: these coarse terms matter, but none is a selective successor
computation.

## My deeper cross-MLP check

R589 uses the saved R584 FIT rows to compare parts of different MLPs by their downstream causal response rather than
by their native coordinates. For a normal successor, copy, or relation-break row, the response is

$$
y=\operatorname{margin}_{\text{native}}-\operatorname{margin}_{\text{removed}}.
$$

For a (+2) conflict row, (y) uses the model's arithmetic-(+2) logit minus its structural-(+1) logit instead.
I aligned all 576 rows across every pair of arms from different MLP sites and computed centered correlations overall,
within each representation-by-source cell, with each representation held out, and with each source role held out.

The best pair was MLP12's complete response (C+Q) with MLP8's (C) response: 78% overall correlation. Its weakest
representation-by-source cell was 63%; the worst leave-one-representation-out result was 73%, and the worst
leave-one-source-out result was 64%. No pair met every deliberately conservative discovery filter. Since those filters
were chosen after R584 FIT outcomes existed, this is only a screen. It licenses exactly one negative statement: no
pair passed the recorded post-outcome filter. It is not evidence that stable coarse grouping is absent, and the 78%
pair remains a possible hypothesis. Correlation also cannot show that the two removals compose as one executable
intervention.

This changes the priority, not the truth of the grouping hypothesis. We should not promote clusters of the three
algebraic pieces from this FIT screen. The higher-information next route is to split the upstream attention computation
more finely into a score (which source is selected) and a projected value (what payload that source contributes), then
test their crossed combinations directly. If that route fails, the MLP8/MLP12 pair is a concrete candidate for a
prospectively frozen joint-intervention test.

## The next exact computation

At an attention site (h), let (e_h(r)) be the attention score assigned to semantic source role (r), and let
(u_h(r)) be that source's value vector after the head's output matrix. The role-summed equality contribution is

$$
T_h=\sum_{r\in\{A,C\}}e_h(r)u_h(r).
$$

R585 will cache recipient and donor factors before any intervention and test four conditions at four fixed attention
sites:

$$
e_r u_r,\qquad e_d u_r,\qquad e_r u_d,\qquad e_d u_d.
$$

These mean replay, score-only swap, value-only swap, and joint swap. Selector-only edits and payload-only edits make
opposing predictions, while answer-preserving edits test whether the term is merely a broad contextual write. The
complete model-free manifest now fixes 20 target cells, 32 control cells, 24 arm/direction/condition coverage keys,
124 bootstrap statistics per split, and a maximum of 459 FIT plus 231 SELECT forwards. A hash-closed dependency lock
binds the audited R586/R587 evidence. No R585 model run exists yet.

## When to expand beyond two agents

Two agents are enough until one builder–critic cycle completes end to end. After R585's implementation and independent
review, the reusable packet will include:

- counterfactual edits that actually isolate selector, payload, and joint changes;
- code mapping semantic source/query positions even when prompts have different lengths;
- the smallest exact attention term that can be swapped or removed;
- active controls that distinguish selectivity from a zero intervention; and
- a categorized failure: behavior, site, factor capacity, factorization, selective removal, OOD, or bookkeeping.

At that point scaling to five circuits makes sense. Each agent can start from the same tested packet and a distinct
canonical dossier, while the parent retains one deep circuit and compares failure classes across the batch. Until then,
five parallel runs would mostly reproduce the same counterfactual and serialization mistakes faster.
