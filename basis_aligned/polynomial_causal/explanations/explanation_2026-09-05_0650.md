## The computation

Layer 13, attention head 8 contributes a vector at the final prediction position. For source token $s$, write its
projected value as $u(s)$ and the recipient's attention score as $a(s)$. The head contribution is

$$
H = \sum_s a(s)u(s).
$$

We kept the recipient's attention scores fixed and replaced $u(s)$ with donor values only in selected source regions:

- `PREFIX`: tokens before the relevant opening delimiter;
- `OPEN`: the opening bracket or quote itself; and
- `POST`: tokens after that opening delimiter.

This is below the native head boundary: it asks which source-token contributions inside one head perform different
roles. It is not a rank approximation.

For answer-changing rows, recovery is the swap's change in the correct answer-versus-alternative logit difference,
divided by the change produced by swapping the whole head. A value of 1 means the partial swap causes the same answer
change as the whole-head swap; 0 means no effect; a negative value pushes the answer the other way.

## Basic screen: the across-construction average failed for a useful reason

The first run tested all eight combinations of the three regions on 24 fresh rows. Native behavior was correct in every
family and direction, exact replay error was zero, the whole-head positive control worked, and same-state controls were
small. `OPEN+POST` passed its positive target bar.

The registered generic claim nevertheless failed because `PREFIX` was not uniformly irrelevant. Its average absolute
recovery was 0.409, above the 0.25 bar. Looking at the two constructions separately revealed a clean difference:

| Construction | PREFIX, base→donor | PREFIX, donor→base | OPEN+POST, base→donor | OPEN+POST, donor→base |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Direct pending opener | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.965 | 0.996 |
| Completed, then reopened | -0.793 | -0.843 | 1.630 | 1.616 |

Thus the null was not “the head has no structure.” It showed that pooling the two constructions erased a stable
within-head interaction. When an earlier bracket has already been completed, prefix values oppose the pending-opener
answer, while the opening-delimiter and later-token values more than compensate.

## Fresh SELECT confirmation

Before opening SELECT outcomes, we froze the family-specific prediction on 24 new rows with zero endpoint-text overlap
with the first screen or the older R545 dataset. Only `PREFIX` and `OPEN+POST` were evaluated, so the confirmation cost
10 forwards rather than repeating the eight-corner discovery sweep.

Every frozen bar passed in both directions:

| Measurement | Base→donor | Donor→base | Required |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Direct PREFIX absolute recovery | 0.000 | 0.000 | at most 0.10 |
| Completed/reopened PREFIX recovery | -0.699 | -0.673 | at most -0.50 |
| PREFIX gap between constructions | 0.699 | 0.673 | at least 0.50 |
| Direct OPEN+POST median recovery | 1.000 | 0.988 | at least 0.75 |
| Completed/reopened OPEN+POST median recovery | 1.380 | 1.347 | at least 1.25 |
| OPEN+POST gap between constructions | 0.379 | 0.359 | at least 0.25 |

Native correct-answer rate was 100% in every family/direction, replay error was zero, the whole-head ceiling worked on
every target row, and same-state control movement remained below its limits. This is therefore **held screen-level
evidence** for a construction-dependent source-region split within layer-13/head-8. It is not yet a TEST/OOD result or a
complete extracted bracket program.

## Current continuation

The exact eight-corner BASIC_SCREEN evidence showed that `OPEN` alone accounted for almost all `OPEN+POST` recovery,
while `POST` and their interaction were near zero. A third fresh authority therefore froze only that narrow prediction.
It passed: `OPEN` median recovery was 0.956/1.023 in direct prompts and 1.391/1.371 after completion/reopening;
`POST` mean absolute recovery was 0.013–0.033; and the OPEN-by-POST interaction was 0.00038–0.00099. All instrument and
same-state-control checks passed again.

The next experiment exactly removed only the semantic opener's projected value on 24 further fresh held-out rows. It
was necessary in every target cell: median damage was 0.978–1.102 of the whole-head removal effect, and every row moved
in the predicted direction. But it was not selective. Same-state controls lost 0.972 of the whole-head effect on
average, although their correct answers remained preferred.

This is a useful distinction between **state support** and **state identity**. The full opener contribution appears to
contain a shared component meaning roughly “some closer is pending,” plus a delimiter-specific component distinguishing
parenthesis, square bracket, and quote. Natural swaps change the type-specific part while retaining shared support;
zero removal deletes both. The active mathematical step is therefore to define the shared mean and centered type
contrasts across the three opener contributions, then test those pieces separately. The centering is tied to the
semantic delimiter states and causal interventions, not chosen by rank or activation reconstruction.
