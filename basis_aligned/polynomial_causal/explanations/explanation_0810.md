## What the six independent routes taught us

The six one-hour directions were useful precisely because most did not become adoption routes.

- **Direct bilinear MLP compression:** paired Left/Right maps do share contextual input subspaces. At MLP0,
  context-weighted reduced-rank regression produced fully gated ranks512 and640. Generic output-PCA and ordinary
  weight SVD were weaker, especially at late depth. This route contributed a real structural law, though moving
  MLP0 rank below the current certificate frontier does not improve the final scalar point.
- **Joint input/output vocabulary factorization:** the untied embedding and unembedding share a functionally useful
  frequency-weighted code and beat equal-price independent SVD. But rare-token error is distributed: exact sparse
  row repair failed, and enough residual rank to approach acceptable loss consumed nearly all saving. This remains
  a representation result, not a compiler route.
- **Causal-response coordinates:** direct suffix-gradient bases lost to ordinary activation PCA at equal price and
  were split-unstable. The useful descendant was instead a 62-dimensional *damage coordinate*: circuit-member
  damage vectors across rank programs lie almost on one ray.
- **Predictive causal states/Hankel structure:** quote parity was decodable and transferable, but explained little
  continuation-response variance; parenthesis state was weaker and shuffled controls dissolved the stronger
  representation claim. Generic Hankel expansion is parked until a behavior supplies a natural finite state.
- **Executable error contracts:** omitted local energy predicts end-to-end damage well enough to rank candidates,
  but its transferred interval was 3.4x wide and could not certify the strongest basis. Its successful successor
  is the family-specific induced-metric tail law, which predicts physical CE damage to roughly 7–8% leave-one-rank
  error.
- **Exact embedding-folded MLP0 structure:** all 50,304 one-token inputs can be folded exactly into the bilinear
  layer. Planted blocks are recoverable, but the real contraction algebra is irreducible under the tested nulls.
  Neither token-identity nor live-context four-state MoE routing beats one shared subspace at equal price. A DAG is
  not orientable from a single commutative bilinear layer without an asymmetric intervention or temporal relation.

This gives a coherent negative lesson: MLP0 is predominantly one shared spectral object, not a generic block,
hierarchy, DAG, or four-way geometric partition. A future finite router must use a behavior-named state fixed
before fitting. Generic input-dependent top-k remains a combinatorial compute policy, not a small tensor network.

## The mathematical compiler we obtained

For a linear map `W` receiving inputs with covariance `C`, the relevant rank error is the omitted singular energy
of `W C^(1/2)`, not ordinary Frobenius weight error. For each component family `f`, physical damage follows

`D_f(r) = a_f T_f(r)^(b_f)`,

where `T_f(r)` is summed omitted induced-metric energy. Q/K and MLP0 each fit at log-space R2 about `.995`, but
their gains differ by 3.30x and their exponents are about 1.69 and1.08. Rank must therefore be allocated by
family-weighted marginal tail reduction rather than uniformly.

For certificates, normalize each circuit member's compiled damage by half its native ablation magnitude. The
62-vector is approximately

`v_i(program) = s(program) k_i`,

with one nearly universal shape `k`. This ray fit has R2 `.99945` on the Q/K ladder, predicts leave-one-rank
certificate counts exactly, and transfers to held-out Q/K+MLP0 and value programs at cosine above `.997`. A
prospective intensity law plus the tail model predicts held-out composition counts with zero mean error in the
available four-program test.

These laws converted rank search into a calculator. Exact enumeration of 42 calibrated Q/K×MLP0 choices found no
program below 512,561,462 scalars with a conservative lower bound of 43 certificates. That discrete grid is now
closed; further scalar progress must come from a different family or representation.

The remaining bottleneck is semantic scalar count, not bytes. The ranked plan is:

1. **All-layer contextual bilinear screen, now running.** Independently test paired-Left/Right context ranks512

   shuffled controls and an equal-price shared-subspace comparator.
3. **Vector-valued downstream objective.** The scalar suffix-gradient and leverage reweightings failed rare-tail
   bars. A legitimate successor must preserve a suffix Jacobian subspace or several named interventions, not

   and fused BF16/FP16 execution separately. Do not infer speed or activation-memory improvements from byte bills.
