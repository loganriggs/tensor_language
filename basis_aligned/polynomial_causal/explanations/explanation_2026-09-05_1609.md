# Circuit-program update — 2026-09-05 16:09 UTC

## Current goal

The controlling goal is to build a reusable codebase that can produce hundreds of high-quality, nonduplicated causal circuits, then use those circuits to find the model's natural computational decomposition. A circuit should eventually specify what information is read, what operation is performed, what is written, and which later computations use it. It should predict held-out and out-of-distribution behavior and support selective removal, interchange, or editing without damaging unrelated behavior.

This work does not use low rank, activation variance, quantization, or activation reconstruction as evidence of a circuit.

## Where the grammatical-number circuit has been localized

The current subject–verb agreement examples require the model to choose the correct singular or plural verb after a subject and intervening material. The strongest established path is now:

$$
\text{subject state}
\longrightarrow \text{upstream MLP writes}
\longrightarrow \text{MLP8}
\longrightarrow \text{attention layer 11, head 3 current-value branch}
\longrightarrow \text{verb-number prediction}.
$$

Here “current-value branch” means the part of attention head 11.3's value vector computed from the current residual-stream state at the subject position. It is distinct from the cached value information previously written at that position.

An exact per-layer intervention compared two effects for every MLP from 4 through 10:

1. **Standalone effect:** change only that MLP's propagated write from the recipient example to the opposite-number donor example.
2. **Conditional effect:** begin with every MLP4–10 write changed, then return only that MLP to the recipient value.

The first asks whether the MLP write works alone. The second asks whether it remains useful in the presence of the other changed writes. CE loss and the correct-versus-incorrect answer-logit margin were measured in every case.

MLP8 was the only layer that passed both tests in every registered data cell:

- standalone recovery: about 26.6–28.3% of the complete MLP4–10 CE effect and 27.1–27.7% of its answer-margin effect;
- conditional recovery: about 28.0–31.2% of CE and 28.6–32.6% of the answer margin.

Same-number, different-noun interventions were small. This makes MLP8 a stable, number-specific causal writer at the layer boundary. It does **not** mean the whole MLP is one semantic unit.

The result is published canonically as `grammatical_subject_number.v16` with 20 focused publication tests passing.

## The new exact split inside MLP8

MLP8 is bilinear. For an input vector $x$, its product activations are

$$
z(x)=(Lx)\odot(Rx),
$$

where $L$ and $R$ are fixed linear maps and $\odot$ means elementwise multiplication. A final fixed map `Down` turns these product activations into the 1152-dimensional vector written to the residual stream.

Let $x_r$ be MLP8's normalized input at the recipient subject position, let $x_s$ be the opposite-number donor input, and define

$$
\Delta x=x_s-x_r.
$$

Then the exact product change is

$$
z(x_s)-z(x_r)=t_{\mathrm{cross}}+t_{\mathrm{quadratic}},
$$

with

$$
t_{\mathrm{cross}}
=(L\Delta x)\odot(Rx_r)+(Lx_r)\odot(R\Delta x),
$$

and

$$
t_{\mathrm{quadratic}}
=(L\Delta x)\odot(R\Delta x).
$$

The cross term asks how the number change interacts with the recipient's existing state. The quadratic term asks what is computed by multiplying the change by itself.

This split avoids an important ambiguity. Each product can swap its $L$ and $R$ factors or reciprocally rescale them without changing MLP8. A raw “left branch” versus “right branch” explanation would therefore depend on an arbitrary parameterization. The combined cross term and the quadratic term are unchanged by those transformations.

The experiment inserted four exact MLP8 product states at subject position 8:

$$
z_r,\qquad z_r+t_{\mathrm{cross}},\qquad
z_r+t_{\mathrm{quadratic}},\qquad z_s.
$$

Each state passed through MLP8's real `Down` map, the native residual scaling through blocks 9–11, the real attention-11 value computation, and the native model suffix. The experiment ran both with all other MLP4–10 writes kept at recipient values and with the other writes changed to donor values. Same-number different-noun controls were also run.

## Precision-invalid first run and repaired result

The first managed run was invalidated before interpretation. Regrouping wide float32 sums produced absolute differences of roughly $2\times10^{-4}$ to $3\times10^{-4}$, above the frozen $5\times10^{-5}$ exactness limit. Its causal arms were recorded as non-evidence.

The repair did not change the rows, interventions, task measurements, thresholds, predictions, or model endpoints. It evaluated the same local polynomial in float64, anchored the natural recipient and donor endpoints to their captured native float32 values, assigned the remaining numerical error to the cross bookkeeping term, cast once back to the model's native datatype, and reproduced the native sequential residual scaling.

The repaired run passed every exactness check. The largest gauge error was $1.14\times10^{-13}$; the natural recipient and donor propagated MLP8 writes matched exactly.

## Result: MLP8 uses direction-dependent cancellation

No single component was uniformly sufficient. Instead, the two number-change directions use opposite signed combinations.

For plural-to-singular changes:

- the cross term produced about 262–293% of the complete MLP8 effect;
- the quadratic term produced about -147% to -155%;
- their opposing effects largely cancelled to give the actual full response.

For singular-to-plural changes:

- the cross term produced about -64% to -33%;
- the quadratic term produced about 133–165%;
- again, the opposing effects combined to give the full response.

These percentages are ratios of causal effects, not probabilities. More than 100% means that a component pushes farther than the complete MLP8 response. A negative value means it pushes the answer in the opposite direction. Cancellation is therefore part of the computation rather than measurement noise.

The pattern was stable in both upstream-writer backgrounds. Number specificity passed and same-number noun collateral remained null. The registered “cross always dominates,” “quadratic always dominates,” and “both are always independently helpful” hypotheses were all rejected.

The registered downstream-interaction hypothesis was also null because it required a large interaction in every cell. Some plural-to-singular cells nevertheless had appreciable nonlinear interaction after the pieces were propagated through the later model, so the null should not be misread as saying every later computation is exactly additive.

## What this changes

This is a genuine within-module causal split. It shows why treating MLP8 as the final basis would conceal the computation: the same MLP contains two gauge-invariant response components with different signed roles, and their roles reverse with the direction of the grammatical-number change.

The result is still a screen rather than a complete circuit. It does not yet show that the signed pattern generalizes to new syntax, that either component is selectively necessary, which semantic variables inside $x_r$ and $\Delta x$ control the split, or which later weights read the two responses.

The result is now published canonically as `grammatical_subject_number.v17`, with 25 focused tests covering revisions v13–v17.

The next-action audit ranked the two candidate follow-ups as follows:

1. testing the direction-dependent signed pattern on genuinely fresh or out-of-distribution syntax; and
2. selectively removing each component to test whether the observed overshoot-and-cancellation account predicts behavioral damage.

No unopened and already licensed Task14 dataset remains at subject position 8. The selected follow-up therefore uses 16 balanced fronted, two-attractor prompts whose text and whole-head outcomes are already open, while keeping the new MLP8 intervention prospective. It is labeled `OOD_TEXT_REUSE_NEW_MLP8_INTERVENTION` rather than described as pristine held-out data. A new scoped native-capability license and the OOD runner are now being implemented. The same four product corners provide component-removal contrasts at no additional model-forward cost, although those removal contrasts are algebraically dependent on the interchange measurements rather than independent evidence.
