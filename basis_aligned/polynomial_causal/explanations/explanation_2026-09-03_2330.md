## The iterative workflow

The unit passed between agents is not a verbal summary. It contains:

1. the behavioral variable and at least two meaningfully different counterfactual constructions;
2. answer-preserving controls on which the intervention is nonzero;
3. exact source, query, and answer-token positions;
4. the smallest tensor term exchanged or removed, with an independent reconstruction;
5. row-level logits, cross-entropy, causal effects, split labels, and provenance;
6. frozen pass and null conditions;
7. tests that plant misleading but internally consistent evidence; and
8. a deterministic model-free dry run.

After each circuit, I extract five reusable objects: a dataset pattern, semantic-position code, an intervention
primitive, an active-control pattern, and a precise failure class. Later agents inherit those objects and negative
examples, but not outcomes from unopened held-out or out-of-distribution data.

The scaling rule is deliberately conservative. We stay at two subagents until the interface survives a complete
build–attack–repair–execution–audit cycle. Five parallel agents would currently reproduce the same hidden assumption
five times. Once the shared interface is stable, the two agents can cover distinct circuits, and then we can expand.

## What R585 computes

At an attention head, the contribution from source role $r$ is written as

$$
T_h=\sum_r e_h(r)u_h(r),
$$

where $e_h(r)$ is the attention score restricted to the registered equality edge and $u_h(r)$ is that source's value
vector after the head's output matrix. For recipient prompt $x$ and donor prompt $y$, R585 runs four replacements:

$$
\begin{aligned}
t_h^{xx}&=\sum_r e_h^x(r)u_h^x(r) &&\text{replay the recipient},\\
t_h^{yx}&=\sum_r e_h^y(r)u_h^x(r) &&\text{change selection only},\\
t_h^{xy}&=\sum_r e_h^x(r)u_h^y(r) &&\text{change copied content only},\\
t_h^{yy}&=\sum_r e_h^y(r)u_h^y(r) &&\text{change both}.
\end{aligned}
$$

This tests a semantic decomposition across four attention sites; it does not treat a whole head as the true basis and
does not optimize matrix rank.

## What happened in the managed attempts

Iteration 6 passed independent exact-byte review. Its first managed call then failed because the preregistered schedule
uses batches of 32 with several padded prompt lengths, while the model facade was asked to enforce its unrelated fixed
test shape `(4, 256)`. No result, receipt, or evidence directory was created.

The prospective repair changed the three real forward calls to relax only that shape assertion. Checkpoint identity,
model structure, token validity, intervention equations, thresholds, and the exact budget remained unchanged. A fresh
review found exactly those three calls and approved their hashes. The owner and adapter suites passed 72 tests, the
new review passed 6 more, and the managed retry was authorized.

The retry passed the shape boundary and then correctly stopped at

```text
replay/native full-logit comparison failed before publishable evidence
```

The replay run replaces the registered attention term with the same term reconstructed from saved $e$ and $u$. The
native comparator does not replace it. R585 requires their complete 50,257-token output-logit vectors to agree within
$10^{-5}$. They did not. Because those large vectors are intentionally not retained, the run hard-aborted rather than
publishing an unauditable `invalid instrument` result. The immediate next diagnostic separates three possibilities:

- local numerical error in reconstructing $\sum_r e_h(r)u_h(r)$;
- amplification of that small local error by downstream layers; or
- numerical differences caused by changing batch membership or padded length between replay and native calls.

The frozen $10^{-5}$ threshold will not be loosened after seeing this failure.

## What the numbered-list track taught us

The separate R584 artifact still passes its narrow later audit: the expected 576 FIT rows, 12 intervention arms, 432
bootstrap summaries, 379 forwards, and reported split decision reconstruct mechanically. A new adversarial audit found
that its producer contract was nevertheless too weak:

- detailed reports could be erased while an invented terminal decision was accepted;
- a failed replay/exactness check could be serialized as a scientific null; and
- result publication was not an atomic, mutually hash-bound result/receipt/evidence package.

Therefore R584 is not promoted as high-quality circuit evidence. R590 is a prospective new-namespace replication that
keeps the frozen numbered-list science and repairs only these evidence boundaries. Its builder cannot approve its own
repair; a different agent must review it before any run.

## The concrete bootstrap improvement

The shared handoff now has 22 accumulated lessons. The newest one requires every builder to enumerate all actual
model-forward call sites and prove that the registered batch and padding schedule is accepted by the explicit interface
mode before enqueue. A planted batch-32 versus fixed-`(4, 256)` mismatch must fail in preflight. This is stored in the
version-4 machine-readable handoff and its tests, so pending-opener and later circuit agents inherit the fix.
