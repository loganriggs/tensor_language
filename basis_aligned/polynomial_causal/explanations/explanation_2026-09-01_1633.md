# Research update — 2026-09-01 16:33 UTC

## Goal

The project goal is a smaller, transparent tensor program that continues to predict fresh text, composes when
several replacements are installed, preserves named causal interventions, and has a literal storage/compute price.
The current local question is whether our exact interpretation of MLP0 can improve the already adopted rank-448
shared-input program—or tell us rigorously which alternatives are not worth building.

Cross-entropy, abbreviated CE, is the average negative log-probability assigned to the correct next token. All
numbers below are **CE added above the native model, in nats; lower is better**.

## What the exact branch interpretation says

The complete MLP0 output was separated exactly into:

- `T`: the centered token-only main effect;
- `C`: the centered context-only main effect;
- `I`: the token-by-context bilinear interaction;
- `S`: the correction caused by the example-specific normalization gain.

On the frozen SELECT documents, Shapley attribution of the native branch benefit is:

| branch | benefit | share |
|---|---:|---:|
| token×context `I` | 1.5375 nat | 43.7% |
| token `T` | 1.4983 nat | 42.6% |
| context `C` | 0.4177 nat | 11.9% |
| normalization `S` | 0.0675 nat | 1.9% |

Thus `T` and `I` are co-primary, `C` is secondary, and `S` is the first branch to deprioritize. The exact head assay
further showed that attention head 3 is the largest carrier of `I`, but several other heads provide stable redundant
support; the interaction is not a two-head sparse program.

## Why more documents changed the compression conclusion

The initial 96-document rank-448 error audit looked strongly interaction-led. We therefore froze the program and
repeated all 32 branch-error combinations on 384 distinct source documents, or 73,728 scored positions.

Pooled error was still `I > T`, but `I` led only two of four 96-document waves; `T` led the other two. Pooled Shapley
damage was `I=.00479`, `T=.00334`, `C=.00067`, `S=-.00167`, with total damage `.00711 nat`. The registered
interaction-only claim failed. The stable statement is **token-dependent grammar `T+I`**, not `I` alone.

## Three global rank-448 alternatives all failed against covariance

Every candidate below retained the same legal 448-coordinate shared code and the same 9,954,432-value price.

| basis-selection rule | pooled damage | result |
|---|---:|---|
| ordinary input covariance | **0.007107** | baseline |
| token/interaction derivative basis | 0.007357 joint | cleaner branch MSE, worse CE |
| downstream CE-Fisher basis | 0.008513 | stable geometry, worse in every wave |
| shuffled Fisher directions | 0.184898 | negative control fails catastrophically |

“Fisher” here means the matrix `F=average(g g^T)`, where `g` is the real downstream CE gradient with respect to the
1,152-dimensional MLP0 input. Its two independent fitting halves had subspace overlap `.775`, so it is reproducible;
shuffling its directions was catastrophic, so it is aligned to the model. Nevertheless, preserving its largest
directions worsened both prediction and exact `T/I` reconstruction. Reproducible sensitivity is not the same as a
good finite-error compressor.

These receipts close the tested fixed first-order/quadratic rank-448 metric family without threshold or rank tuning.
They do not prove that every possible 448-dimensional subspace is bad.

## Price-aware router ceiling

A mixture-of-experts router stores several programs and chooses one state. Before trying to learn its state, we gave
it impossible future information and measured its best possible outcome.

| MLP0 object | stored values | measured/oracle damage |
|---|---:|---:|
| one covariance p448 | 9,954,432 | 0.007107 |
| one covariance p640 | 11,945,088 | 0.002709 |
| one covariance p768 | **13,272,192** | **0.001173** |
| two p448 experts, sharing only Down/bias | at least 14,599,296 | best document oracle 0.004817 |
| native MLP0 | 15,926,400 | 0 |

The **document oracle** runs each complete document with both experts, examines the future target losses, and keeps
the better expert. The best pair was interaction-active plus Fisher-active. It improved p448 by 32%, selected the two
experts in a balanced 58/42 split, and helped all four waves. Yet it remained four times worse than the cheaper single
p768 program. Even an oracle over all five p448 experts reached only `.002888`, still worse than p768. Therefore a
document-level two-state p448 router is price-dominated before router parameters or operations are counted.

The **position oracle** separately takes the lower loss at every token from two complete, separately executed models.
It reaches `-.0211 nat` for the best pair and `-.0393` across all five—better than native—but it is not one coherent
forward computation and uses the correct future token. It proves per-position complementarity, not a causal router.

## What is closed, and what remains open

Closed by frozen nulls:

- interaction-only rank-448 optimization;
- the tested token/interaction and downstream-Fisher input metrics;
- a document-level router among the five fixed p448 experts;
- any claim that head labels themselves provide the desired compression basis.

Still distinct:

1. **Token-state feasibility.** The preregistered router decision explicitly allows one feasibility study because the
   position oracle has large headroom. A state must use prefix-observable information, select experts on held-out
   documents, be evaluated in a coherent physical forward pass, and beat the cheaper p768 after its literal price.
   The oracle says it must recover roughly 21% of the best-pair position advantage merely to reach p768.
2. **Direct nonlinear CE fitting.** Optimize the legal rank-448 program against finite downstream CE rather than a
   local quadratic metric, initialized from covariance and confirmed on untouched documents.
3. **Output-side or later-layer surrogates.** Change the represented object, following the successful 14,984-value
   layer-16 quadratic precedent, instead of continuing input-subspace reweighting.

The next cheap discriminator is the held-out token-state feasibility screen. If simple prefix-observable states
cannot harvest enough of the saved position-oracle advantage to beat p768, routing closes completely and direct
nonlinear/output-side work becomes the next active object.
