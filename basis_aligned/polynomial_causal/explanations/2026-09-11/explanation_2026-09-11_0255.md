# An executable local edit program, including attention normalization

**The routing components now have an exact local intervention program that uses
only information from the unedited state.** It predicts how a specified source
edit changes normalized QK1×QK2 routing and the scalar value contribution read by
the frozen MLP component. It handles finite edits and their order of composition.

This is conditional prediction with explicit native background, not an isolated
semantic circuit or a simpler replacement model. No natural text entered the
calculation. The useful advance is that the required normalization information
is now included in a concrete executable interface, rather than merely listed
as an unresolved caveat.

## Why the normalizers matter

The [previous update](explanation_2026-09-11_0243.md) found 17-dimensional source
spaces that participate in 59.6% of the joint routing numerator's coefficient
energy. Most of this energy involves crossing interactions with outside inputs.
The actual routing also divides by query/key RMS factors.

For an original source $s$ and edited source $s'$, write normalized routing as
$\alpha=N/D$ and $\alpha'=N'/D'$. The exact change is

$$
\alpha-\alpha'
=\frac{N-N'}{D}+N'\left(\frac1D-\frac1{D'}\right).
$$

The first term changes the numerator while retaining the original denominator.
The second accounts for recomputing the normalizers. On 64 fixed Gaussian formal
inputs, across nine heads and two source distances, omitting the second term
gives **10.8–33.3% relative error** in the routing-change vector. The registered
10% error requirement therefore failed. Including both terms agrees with direct
normalized head execution to numerical precision.

The key normalizers also retain substantial outside-source structure: roughly
44.6–69.0% of their quadratic coefficient energy lies entirely outside the selected
spaces in these cases. Those are weight-coefficient measurements, not natural
activation or loss fractions. The formal probes are a numerical stress test;
they do not establish FineWeb or OOD behavioral performance.
[Normalizer/source-edit audit](../../NORMALIZED_QK_SOURCE_EDIT_V1_RESULT.json).

## The compiled calculation

Let $E\in\mathbb R^{1152\times17}$ contain the fixed orthonormal source readers.
Allow an arbitrary edit matrix $C\in\mathbb R^{17\times17}$:

$$
z=E^\top s,\qquad \delta=Cz,\qquad s'=s-E\delta.
$$

$C=I$ removes the whole selected space; a diagonal mask removes chosen directions;
negative diagonal entries amplify them. General matrices allow mixing inside
the space. This edit acts after the source's input normalization. The head key
RMS is recomputed; query and base-value source states remain fixed.

For QK branch $j\in\{1,2\}$, let

$$
b_j=q^\top A_j s,\qquad
\mu_j=q^\top G_{qj}q+\epsilon,\qquad
\kappa_j=s^\top G_{kj}s+\epsilon.
$$

$A_j$ includes the actual query/key weights, head-width scaling and position
rotation. $G_{qj}$ and $G_{kj}$ are the respective head norm matrices. Then

$$
\alpha=\frac{b_1b_2}{\sqrt{\mu_1\mu_2\kappa_1\kappa_2}}.
$$

Prepare these additional quantities from the unedited state and weights:

$$
p_j=E^\top A_j^\top q,\qquad
t_j=E^\top G_{kj}s,\qquad
H_j=E^\top G_{kj}E.
$$

The edited quantities follow without constructing an edited source vector:

$$
b_j'=b_j-p_j^\top\delta,\qquad
\kappa_j'=\kappa_j-2t_j^\top\delta+\delta^\top H_j\delta.
$$

Use the original $\mu_j$ and these new $b_j',\kappa_j'$ in the routing formula.
The original epsilon remains in each $\kappa_j$; it is not dropped or refitted.

The frozen MLP component's shared gate reads a scalar value contribution
$v=g^\top s+v_{\rm base}$ through the head's native OV map. Its edited value is

$$
v'=v-(E^\top g)^\top\delta,
\qquad\text{edited routed contribution}=\alpha'v'.
$$

The predictor receives the baseline quantities above, the small matrices $H_j$,
the vector $E^\top g$, and $C$. It receives no edited endpoint, raw source state,
or native weight matrices. Preparing its baseline quantities still requires the
native computation or equivalent explicitly priced maps.

## Composition, checks and price

For edits $C_1$ followed by $C_2$ in the same source frame, the combined edit is

$$
C_{12}=C_1+C_2-C_2C_1.
$$

This preserves order: noncommuting edits can have different results when reversed.
Changing the internal basis $E\to ER$ with orthogonal $R$ and transforming
$C\to R^\top CR$ preserves the physical edit. The corresponding ports and
small matrices transform with it.

The native-weight audit covered zero edit, full removal, removal of eight
directions, amplification, and both orders of a noncommuting edit pair. It used
all nine heads, both fixed source distances and 64 formal inputs. The largest
relative replay error was $6.42\times10^{-15}$; source-state composition error
was $1.48\times10^{-16}$. Basis-change checks also passed. These are local
algebraic checks, not evidence that two semantic circuits compose in the model.
[Native result](../../COMPILED_QK_SOURCE_EDIT_V1_RESULT.json).

Per head and fixed position pair, the current implementation uses:

- **92 dynamic numbers:** five 17-dimensional projections and seven baseline
  scalar scores, norms and value quantities.
- **595 small-core numbers:** two dense 17-by-17 norm matrices and one value vector.
- **97,920 projection-map numbers** if all five 1152-by-17 maps are stored.

Native score/norm/value computation, position rotation and upstream background
remain required. This is an additional intervention interface, not a demonstrated
storage or compute saving. It answers the normalizer omission with an exact
conditional program, not by claiming the omitted dependencies were unimportant.

## Next structural consequence already implemented

The present source spaces were selected separately at two distances. A reusable
component should preferably use a shared frame across positions. I derived an
analytic source-influence matrix in common key coordinates, whose average over
position maps can be computed before choosing eigenvectors. This avoids forming
a large source tensor separately at every position.

Its CPU checks agree with the earlier automatic-differentiation calculation and
with explicit averaging to $2.9\times10^{-16}$. The native all-position selection
has not run yet. That is the next structural test; it will distinguish reusable
source features from reliance on a separate position-specific frame.
