# A stable compact shared-input component — 11 September, 02:26 UTC

**The joint weight-only factorization converged from all four starts to the same
quadratic function.** It finds one input signal reused by 16 multiplication
branches. Its fit improves modestly over simplifying the previous reader after
selection. I then folded the frozen component through attention's output and
value maps, making its source readers explicit. No text data entered discovery.

This is a reproducible local component, not yet a semantic circuit. It captures
0.6506% of the centered coefficient tensor, not 0.6506% of behavior. Extraction,
selective removal, OOD prediction and behavioral composition remain untested.
The exact background and normalization interfaces are still needed.

## What was optimized

The [previous update](explanation_2026-09-11_0206.md) found a shared reader $a$:
the scalar $a^\top x$ multiplies a residual-output partner $M_ax$. The unrestricted
partner has an exact interpretation as the effect of removing that input direction:

$$
f(x)-f\bigl((I-aa^\top)x\bigr)=(a^\top x)M_ax,
\qquad \|a\|_2=1.
$$

Here $f(x)=D[(Lx)\odot(Rx)]$ is the quadratic residual write of MLP17, and $x$
is its already normalized input. The edited input is not normalized again.
The previous reader was chosen with no restriction on $M_a$, so it need not be
best when the partner must be simple.

The new representation jointly chooses $a$ and a partner with at most 16 linear
branches:

$$
\widehat f(x)=(a^\top x)W(Bx),
\quad W\in\mathbb R^{1152\times16},\quad
B\in\mathbb R^{16\times1152}.
$$

This means 17 input projections, 16 products, and 38,016 stored coefficients,
before charging the retained native background and unembedding. The input signal
is reused across branches; each branch can affect many tokens through $UW$.
It assumes this shared-input pattern, rather than sparse or exclusive token
clusters. It does not claim those 16 branches are independently identifiable.

For each proposed $a$, the best rank-16 partner is solved exactly by a singular
value decomposition. Let $U_c$ be the unembedding with its vocabulary mean
subtracted, and let $CC^\top=U_c^\top U_c$. Define

$$
J_a=I+(\sqrt{2}-1)aa^\top,
\qquad
F_{16}(a)=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{16}\sigma_i(C^\top M_aJ_a)^2}{2E_c},
$$

where $E_c$ is the total centered quadratic coefficient energy. The factor
$J_a$ accounts for the geometry of symmetric quadratic products; a plain
low-rank approximation of $M_a$ would optimize a different norm.

The nonlinear solver changes only $a$, differentiating this exact conditional
score. It uses conjugate-gradient directions tangent to the unit sphere and a
backtracking line search requiring improvement. Convergence requires tangent
gradient norm at most $10^{-8}$, rather than a flat-looking loss curve. This is
variable projection with constrained optimization; it gives a conditional exact
solution and a local stationary reader, not a global tensor-factorization theorem.

## Results and the missed prediction

The starts were the previous full-output reader, previous centered reader, and
two Gaussian random readers. All converged in 16–25 reported iterations and
35–56 seconds each; the complete managed run took 181 seconds. The score needs
a 1,152-by-1,152 singular value decomposition at each evaluation, which is the
main new expense compared with the unrestricted reader's cheap quadratic score.

| Quantity | Result |
|---|---:|

| Relative improvement | 3.83% |
| Minimum whole-function cosine across four starts | greater than 0.999999999999 |
| Maximum independent coefficient/replay discrepancy | $4.6\times10^{-15}$ |
| Relative gap between the 16th and 17th partner singular values | 4.24% |

Numerical accuracy, convergence and whole-function stability passed. The
preregistered **5% improvement** prediction failed. Its narrow meaning is that
joint selection did not improve this same-price baseline by the chosen amount.
It does not mean there is no shared structure. The executed four-start check
and nonzero singular-value gap argue against an unfinished fit or a cutoff tie
explaining this particular miss; a better, unvisited local optimum is not ruled
out. The original prediction remains failed.

Whole-function comparison includes every output and input interaction, so it
does not confuse an internal change of SVD basis with a different component.
The returned compact partner retains 66.72% of the exact removal component for
its selected reader. Its omitted partner remainder is required to recover the
full input-removal identity above. In full unembedding geometry, 52.59% of this
compact component's energy is vocabulary-common. The MLP17 dossier already
contains calibration structure; this result is not assigned a new semantic name.

## Folding this component further backward

Stack the shared reader and partner readers into $F=[a^\top;B]$. Attention17's
pre-MLP residual can be written as

$$
y=b+\sum_{h,p}\alpha_{hp}O_hV_h\xi_p,
\qquad x=y/\rho(y).
$$

Here $b$ is the incoming residual, $O_h$ is head $h$'s output map,
$\alpha_{hp}$ is its signed joint QK1×QK2 routing weight to source $p$, and
$\xi_p$ contains the current and base normalized value-source states. $V_h$
includes the model's actual signed mixing coefficient, which is $-0.0888672$;
it is not constrained to be a convex mixture. $\rho$ is the native input RMS
divisor, with its epsilon explicit.

The folded source readers are simply

$$
G_h=FO_hV_h,
\qquad z=Fb+\sum_{h,p}\alpha_{hp}G_h\xi_p,
\qquad \widehat f(x)=\frac{z_0Wz_{1:16}}{\rho(y)^2}.
$$

The CPU audit replayed this substitution with signed routing and multiple
sources to relative error $6.5\times10^{-15}$. It exposes 17 source readers per
head and retains the original routing/background. It neither decomposes the
QK product nor proves that computing these 153 projected value readers saves
work in the full model.

I also compared the source-input geometry across heads. For this particular
component, the largest absolute cosine between the nine shared-gate source
readers is 0.315. The largest principal cosine between any pair of 16-dimensional
partner-reader spaces is 0.438. A principal cosine measures the best alignment
between directions in two spaces, independent of their internal bases. These
are descriptive weight measurements, not a test of the two previously discussed
behaviors or a rejection of partial task-specific QK sharing.
