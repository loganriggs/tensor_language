## What was folded

For a fixed query, head $h$ has routing weights$a_{hp}$ to source positions$p$.
They are the model's actual two-QK products, with norms, rotary positions and
masking still required. Write$x_p$ for the shared tuple containing current-layer
and block0 normalized source states. The value/output weights give

$$
W_h=[(1-\lambda)V_{17,h},\ \lambda V_{0,h}],\qquad M_h=O_hW_h,
$$

$$
Z_{hi}=\sum_p a_{hp}x_{pi},\qquad y=\sum_h M_h Z_h.
$$

The native mixing parameter is $\lambda=-0.0888671875$: the current value is
multiplied by1.0888671875 and the base value is subtracted. This is a signed
linear combination, not a convex average.

A token's folded bilinear function has a symmetric matrix$Q_v$. Its attention-only
quadratic numerator is$y^\top Q_vy$. The residual-only and residual–attention
cross terms, MLP bias, input RMS, final RMS and tanh remain required. These
calculations do not supply an independent token-to-logit circuit.

## Why one source differs from multiple sources

With one source, $Z=ax^\top$ has rank1, so its2×2 determinants vanish. A specific
part of the lifted quadratic coefficient tensor consists of these determinants.
It is antisymmetric separately in head indices and source-feature indices,
while symmetric under exchange of the complete input slots. This is what
“double-antisymmetric component” means here.

For several sources,

$$
Z_{hi}Z_{kj}-Z_{hj}Z_{ki}
=\sum_{p<q}(a_{hp}a_{kq}-a_{hq}a_{kp})
(x_{pi}x_{qj}-x_{pj}x_{qi}).
$$

The component combines **routing contrasts** with **value contrasts**. It must
not be deleted from full attention. A two-source controlled example gives a
nonzero determinant even though either source alone gives zero.

Adding the common output might change the optimal modes, so the centered result
cannot simply be reused as an all-U optimum. We instead derived a valid bound.
For any rank $r$ routing projector$P$,

$$
\operatorname{tr}[P(G_c+G_m)]
\leq\sum_{i=1}^r\lambda_i(G_c)+\operatorname{tr}(G_m).
$$

Here $G_c$ and $G_m$ are the positive-semidefinite centered/common routing covariances.
Even granting all common energy to four modes, they can retain at most24.59% of
the full antisymmetric tensor. At least30 modes are necessary for90%. These are
bounds on this linear routing-mode representation, not on arbitrary arithmetic
circuits or the usefulness of small, lower-energy computations.

## What begins next

A large output-side contrast is only useful when the heads actually route
differently across sources. If two routing rows are proportional, their routing
determinant vanishes. The next implemented tool compares the **joint** QK1×QK2
numerator along with both squared query/key normalization polynomials. It does
not assign one task to QK1 and another to QK2.

At a specified positional relation, the numerator is

$$
N_h(q,k)=(q^\top A_hk)(q^\top B_hk).
$$

The squared query denominator is a product of quadratic forms, including the
native epsilon terms; the key denominator has the analogous form. Numerator
agreement alone is not enough to merge normalized routers. Exact coefficient
inner products for these objects are implemented and match dense synthetic
tensors to below1e-15. A trained-weight QK comparison has not run yet.
