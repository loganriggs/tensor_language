## Where 0331 left off
We had a shared, portable equality MATCHER (an early head's match signal that substitutes three layers forward)
and one mystery: the effect obeys a strict context law — strongest at long range and unique predecessors — but
neither any single reader (458) nor reader amplitude (461) carried that law.

## Five experiments later, the circuit has a parts list
**462**: no single later write mediates the law (best single site recovers .056 of .10 required) — and most
single-site patches actively HURT, a clue that the later writes only make sense together.
**463**: the decisive factorial. The direct equality signal is nearly context-FLAT; the complete set of later
writes, patched as a whole, carries the context law. My first interpretation ("the suffix corrects the signal")
had a circular falsifier, which I withdrew — the honest computation showed the suffix doesn't correct a varying
signal, it IS the context modulation (its profile matches the native law at r .9996).
**464**: the modulation is source-INDEPENDENT: swap the matcher (native late head vs transplanted early head) and
the correction stays the same at cosine .995; cross-wired circuits recover .87–1.08 of the effect. Two separable
parts, proven by interchange.
**465**: the correction's strongest site (MLP17) turned out NOT to carry the context pattern — it is a broad,
context-flat suppressor. "Most necessary" and "context-carrying" are different properties.
**466**: the full anatomy. Three MLPs (8, 9, 12) carry the context law (alignment .992 with the full correction,
identical under both sources); attention14+MLP17 form the separate broad suppressor; the two groups interact
positively; five named sites reproduce the correction's direction at cosine .93 while its magnitude stays partly
distributed (~70% captured).

## What this buys
A compositional, swap-tested circuit spanning ten modules across twelve layers, with each part's role established
by removal, transplant, and interchange — not by similarity or rank. The candidate compressed form is now
concrete: a flat matcher plus a small scalar gate in (distance, predecessor-count), to be priced only after the
within-MLP split says how fine the task structure goes. Three consecutive zero-abort hours; every receipt scored
as registered, failures preserved.
