## Our goal

We are trying to compile the 546M-parameter bilin18 transformer into a smaller tensor program that remains:

- **predictive:** low added cross-entropy on fresh and shifted text;
- **composable:** independently useful replacements still work when combined;
- **manipulable:** named interventions preserve their signed causal effects;
- **literally simpler:** every factor, index, state, metadata item, and storage dtype is counted.

This is stronger than finding a low reconstruction error or an attractive visualization.  A representation only
enters the frontier after a physical model build survives held-out prediction, circuit certificates, OOD tests,
and causal gates.

## What is already real

The current fully gated fidelity dial is:

| program | stored bytes | added CE (lower is better) | circuit certificates |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| source-aware BF16 high-fidelity endpoint | 1.0918 GB | +0.000009 | 62 / 62 |
| QK72 + MLP0/4 at p768 | 1.0325 GB | +0.009301 | 50 / 62 |
| QK64 + MLP0/4 at p768 | 1.0235 GB | +0.012329 | 43 / 62 |

The smallest point stores 511,758,646 scalars and preserves the original model's signed intervention direction
with cosine 0.9865.  Its choice of two mild, distributed MLP cuts is also the exact solution of the local
certificate-constrained allocation problem.  More Q/K rank changes, a third local MLP cut, value-map ranks,
shared encoders, finite routers, and raw block/tree/DAG recovery have all been measured rather than merely
suggested, and none currently beats that local solution.

## The new structural result

The embedding-folded MLP0 idea was useful, but its answer was asymmetric: MLP0 has a stable input subspace, while
its product mode is too diffuse for a small Tucker core.  The important surprise is that this is **not** a law of
all bilinear MLPs.

Exact invariant mode spectra across all 18 layers show that the cheap tensor direction moves with depth.  Early
layers are mainly compressible on their inputs.  At layers 16 and 17, the input, product, and output modes are all
concentrated.  For layer 16, a core with ranks `(input=512, product=576, output=512)` costs exactly 2,065,536
scalars, versus 15,926,400 for the native layer.

The latest function-space screen then measured the missing conversion.  On three disjoint live-text populations,
the simple importance-truncation core reconstructs about 82% of layer 16's function (`R²=0.8145` and `0.8306` on
two untouched sets).  A joint Adam fit greatly reduces training error but raises held-out R² only to 0.8309, a
gain of 0.016 rather than the preregistered 0.05.  So optimization is not the lever, and the earlier ≥0.90 hope is
honestly rejected.  The stable ~0.82 function level is nevertheless achieved at only 13% of the native layer's
scalar price.

## What runs next

The next adoption-track rung physically substitutes that frozen layer-16 core into the full model and measures:

1. census CE added above the original native model;
2. the 62 circuit-certificate damage vector and its agreement with the already validated certificate ray;
3. fresh and shifted-corpus damage;
4. exact program identity and literal scalar price.

This is a calibration, not an adoption shortcut.  A good result licenses one independently preregistered causal
gate and composition test.  A bad result closes the 2.07M-scalar corner even though its local R² looks respectable.
The decisive unknown is whether the layer's omitted 18% function energy lies in downstream-sensitive directions.

## Independent paths kept alive

If the physical Tucker calibration is poor, the plan steps back rather than tuning around the result:

1. test the same frozen construction at layer 17, whose product and output spectra are even more concentrated;
2. try vector-valued intervention-aware reduced-rank regression, preserving a basis of causal responses rather
   than another scalar loss weight;
3. model the vocabulary residual with a priced graph-smooth basis instead of global rank or exact rare rows;
4. use asymmetric conditioned derivative operators for any defensible hierarchy or DAG claim;
5. measure fused runtime and lower-bit physical storage after the semantic representation stabilizes.

The durable loop is active: after each landed result, score the frozen predictions and null, update the ledger and
board, then take an hourly strategic step back before choosing the next highest-information action.
