# Circuit-program update — 2026-09-05 09:59 UTC

## Goal

The controlling goal is to build a reusable codebase and evidence base containing hundreds of high-quality causal circuits, then use repeated structure across those circuits to discover the model's natural computational decomposition.

The evidence target is task behavior: answer-logit margins, CE loss, meaningful interchange, and selective removal. Rank reduction, quantization, activation variance, and activation reconstruction do not establish the properties we need and were not used in this work.

This update continues from `explanation_2026-09-05_0909.md`. It follows one subject–verb agreement circuit below the layer-11 attention-head boundary, first across source-token values and then across attention scores.

## The exact attention computation being split

At the final prediction position $i$, layer-11 attention head 3 writes

$$
h_i=\sum_k p_{ik}u_k.
$$

Here $k$ is a source-token position. The projected value

$$
u_k=W_Ov_k\in\mathbb{R}^{1152}
$$

is the vector that source $k$ writes into the 1152-dimensional residual stream after this head's output matrix. The scalar attention score is

$$
p_{ik}
=\left(\frac{q_i^\top k_k}{128}\right)
 \left(\frac{{q'_i}^\top k'_k}{128}\right).
$$

This model has two query–key dot products per head and multiplies them. There is no softmax in this score formula. Replacing one exact term $p_{ik}u_k$ and running the real remaining model lets us ask what that source term does causally to the answer.

## Ordinary syntax: the value circuit is distributed over grammatical positions

The all-source TEST-reuse atlas used recipient and donor sentences with the same noun and syntax but opposite subject number. They differed only at the subject token. For every source position $k$, it replaced the recipient value with the donor value while retaining the recipient score:

$$
h_i^{(k)}
=h_i-p_{ik}^{R}u_k^{R}+p_{ik}^{R}u_k^{D}.
$$

It also replaced all values jointly:

$$
h_i^{\mathrm{all\ values}}
=\sum_k p_{ik}^{R}u_k^{D},
$$

and compared that with the complete donor head

$$
h_i^{D}=\sum_k p_{ik}^{D}u_k^{D}.
$$

Joint donor values recovered 89.39%–105.01% of the complete donor-head margin effect in all four ordinary PP/relative-clause cells. Score changes were therefore secondary for these same-syntax interventions.

The subject value was causal, but it was not the whole circuit. The first token after the subject—`in` for the PP construction or `that` for the relative clause—recovered 21.42%–47.49% of the complete-head effect depending on the cell. Other grammatical bridge tokens also mattered. The distracting noun itself was slightly anti-causal.

Because the prompts differ only at the earlier subject, a later value difference means that earlier model computation has transformed or relayed subject-number information to that later position. It does not mean layer-11 head 3 created the information there; this head is reading it there.

## Exact group interactions: subject plus grammatical bridge, not attractor phrase

Singleton effects did not add linearly after the nonlinear downstream model, so the next experiment evaluated every one of the $2^4=16$ combinations of four source-value groups:

- $S$: the grammatical subject;
- $I$: the first token immediately after the subject;
- $B$: the remaining grammatical bridge tokens;
- $A$: the attractor determiner and noun.

Every selected group used donor values and every unselected group used recipient values, while all attention scores remained those of the recipient.

In the PP-plural cell, $S+I$ recovered only 60.20% of the joint-value effect. Adding $B$ raised recovery to 102.26%, moved all 16 examples toward the donor answer, and added 0.965 nat of donor-answer CE improvement. Across all four cells, $S+I+B$ recovered 98.61%–102.26%.

Adding the attractor phrase changed recovery by only -2.26% to +1.39%. Within this intervention family, the value circuit is therefore the subject plus a syntax-dependent relay through grammatical bridge positions, not storage on the distracting noun phrase.

The subject-by-immediate-token interaction was not one universal signed effect. It was positive for plural recipients and negative for singular recipients in both syntaxes, with the sign repeating in both fixed lexical halves. That direction-dependent pattern is a hypothesis for later independent data, not a held-out confirmation.

## OOD constructions show that the route changes with token order

The next atlas reused two previously opened OOD text families but applied the new below-head intervention, so it is best described as a new intervention on reused OOD text rather than pristine OOD confirmation.

The two constructions were deliberately different:

1. A fronted phrase followed by the grammatical subject at the final input position. The prompt changes only that final subject token.
2. A two-attractor relative clause with the grammatical subject near the beginning, leaving many later positions that can relay its number.

In the long relative clause, joint donor values recovered 101.73% and 94.84% of the complete-head effect in the two number directions. The relative marker alone recovered 13.15% and 26.24%; the relative verb and following preposition were also causal in one direction. The distributed number relay therefore recurs outside the ordinary TEST constructions.

The fronted construction behaved differently. Since only the final subject token changes, causal masking guarantees that every earlier projected value is identical between recipient and donor. The final-subject value is therefore exactly the joint-value intervention. It recovered:

- 127.71% from a plural recipient to a singular donor;
- 53.85% from a singular recipient to a plural donor.

This was a valid null for uniform value sufficiency. It also localized the missing computation: because no earlier value can have changed, the difference between the final-subject value intervention and the complete donor head must be caused by attention-score changes or their interaction with the value.

## The score correction is the subject's self term

For the fronted construction, the next exact factorial held the donor subject value fixed and divided score changes into three groups:

- $E$: all positions in the earlier fronted phrase;
- $D$: the determiner immediately before the subject;
- $S$: the final subject's score to itself.

It evaluated all eight score-group combinations. The empty combination reproduced the value-only intervention, and the full combination reproduced the complete donor head. Therefore the game exactly decomposed the conditional score correction

$$
\Delta_{\mathrm{score}\mid\mathrm{donor\ value}}
=m(\mathrm{donor\ scores},\mathrm{donor\ value})
-m(\mathrm{recipient\ scores},\mathrm{donor\ value}),
$$

where $m$ is the donor-directed `is`/`are` answer margin.

In the weak singular-recipient to plural-donor direction:

- the complete conditional score correction was $+0.5406$ logits;
- changing only the final self score contributed $+0.5764$ logits, or 106.62%;
- all 16 examples moved in the correct direction;
- the self-score change added 0.5185 nat of CE improvement;
- changing the earlier phrase and determiner scores contributed $-0.0327$ logits, or -6.05%.

The reverse direction used the same self mechanism with the opposite sign:

- complete conditional score correction: $-0.1600$ logits;
- final self-score contribution: $-0.1482$ logits;
- Shapley contribution assigned to the self-score group: $-0.1478$ logits.

The exact Mobius interaction terms between the three source-score groups were tiny. This is not broad redistribution of attention over the earlier nouns. Nearly the entire directional correction is in the final subject's self term

$$
p_{ii}u_i.
$$

## Why the self score is strongly value-dependent

The whole-score-vector by final-value $2\times2$ factorial separates a score main effect, a value main effect, and their interaction. With recipient/donor score state $a\in\{0,1\}$ and value state $b\in\{0,1\}$, the interaction is

$$
I=m_{11}-m_{10}-m_{01}+m_{00}.
$$

For singular recipients, the score main effect was $-0.1250$ logits but the score-by-value interaction was $+0.6656$, giving the observed conditional correction of $+0.5406$. For plural recipients, the score main effect was $+0.1883$ but the interaction was $-0.3484$, giving $-0.1600$.

So the score is not a generic additive number signal. Its effect depends strongly on which subject value it multiplies. This is exactly what the architectural term $p_{ii}u_i$ suggests: changing the scalar multiplier and changing the written vector are causally coupled.

## Current experiment: split the self score into its two QK branches

The active claimed experiment goes one level lower. At the self position,

$$
p_{ii}
=\left(\frac{q_i^\top k_i}{128}\right)
 \left(\frac{{q'_i}^\top k'_i}{128}\right).
$$

With donor $u_i$ fixed and all non-self source terms kept native, it will evaluate all 16 recipient/donor combinations of $q_i$, $k_i$, $q'_i$, and $k'_i$. It tests whether the first complete QK branch is sufficient, whether the second is independently sufficient, and whether the named interaction between the two branches is material.

There is an important interpretation limit. Naturally, all four vectors come from the same residual state at the same token. Mixed corners such as donor $q_i$ with recipient $k_i$ break that tie. They are useful algebraic interventions into the exact weight computation, but they do not prove that query and key are four naturally independent circuit variables. Individual query-versus-key results will remain descriptive until a tied-residual or natural-counterfactual experiment validates them.

The scalar dot products are invariant under paired query/key gauge changes. The names of the two multiplicative QK branches can still be exchanged, so branch numbering itself has no semantic meaning.

## Result of the active QK split

The QK experiment completed after this document was started. All exact checks passed. Neither complete QK branch was sufficient across both number directions:

- first QK branch: 76.40% recovery for plural-to-singular and 57.39% for singular-to-plural;
- second QK branch: 39.02% and 24.84%.

The named interaction between the two complete branches contributed 15.43% and 17.77% of the total self-score margin effect. Its sign was positive in both directions and in both deterministic lexical halves. Thus the final-subject self score is a genuine composition of both multiplicative QK branches. The first branch is larger, but neither branch is a complete causal unit by itself.
