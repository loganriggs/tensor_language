## The new response-space SAE result

A sparse autoencoder (SAE) writes an input vector approximately as a sum of a few learned vectors called atoms. In
rung444, one input was not a private query or key vector. It was the exact downstream-response vector contributed by
one realized attention edge:

`edge_response = sum_over_heads(score_branch_1 * score_branch_2 * U16_payload)`.

`U16` is the16-dimensional attention0 output subspace that earlier experiments found relevant to downstream
readers. The loss weighted errors by the downstream-response metric from rung424, so two errors counted as similar
only when the six measured downstream consumers treated them similarly.

Three equal-size models used32 atoms and at most4 active atoms per edge:

- `U32`: an ordinary unconstrained dictionary;
- `A32`: every atom is a nonnegative weighted average of real FIT edge responses, with weights summing to one;
- `P32`: the same convex construction after source payloads were permuted, destroying real source binding while
  preserving the marginal distribution.

All instrument checks pass in the authoritative managed run. On unseen SELECT edges, response-metric relative
squared error was`.07571` for U32,`.09780` for A32, and`.09432` for P32. Lower is better. The permuted convex control
is slightly better than the real convex hull, so real edge geometry gives no specific advantage.

Restart stability tells the same story. After optimally matching atoms between two runs, median downstream-metric
cosine is`.6476` for A32 and`.7042` for U32. Cosine1 means identical directions and0 means orthogonal directions.
The convex restriction made the atoms less, not more, reproducible under the registered median statistic.

Inserted back into the model, A32 retains routed-U16 `R²=.8302`, all six downstream-reader `R²` values are
`.9034–.9353`, and suffix cross-entropy increases only`.00189` nat. Here `R²=1` is exact reproduction and `R²=0`
is no better than predicting the mean. These are respectable approximations, but A32 misses the frozen routed
`.90` bar and is worse than U32 (`.8898`). The strong null fires independently because A32 does not beat the
permuted control and does not improve stability.

Conclusion: continuous response geometry is useful, but this32-atom/top-4 convex atomization is not its privileged
discrete vocabulary. We retain the continuous rung424 quotient and close this exact atom family rather than tuning
after seeing SELECT.
