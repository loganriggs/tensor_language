## A correction made before outcomes

Rung443 learned a small structural score that predicts **reconstruction recovery** from MLP0 to MLP1. It did not
learn to predict selective removal or composition. Using that score as if it were already a causal-simplicity rule
would change the meaning of its target after seeing a positive result.

The corrected design therefore uses rung443 only as a pre-frozen baseline. The actual removal and composition rules
will be fit on the35 teaching candidates, with regularization chosen by leaving out one entire teaching family at a
time. Their coefficients and feature transformations are then frozen before the10 attention0 outcomes are opened.
The decisive question is whether the frozen rule orders candidates in a program family it never saw.

## What removal and composition mean computationally

For every candidate program `P`, the planned removal test applies the same distant native intervention: remove the
mean output of attention layer16. If `Delta_native` is the resulting vector of behavior changes in the native model
and `Delta_P` is the vector with `P` installed, the main measurements are their cosine, normalized difference, and
non-target collateral. This asks whether simplifying one part of the model preserves a separate circuit's signed
effect.

For composition, install `P` together with the already physical14,984-value MLP16 quadratic program `Q`. If
`Delta_P`, `Delta_Q`, and `Delta_(P,Q)` are changes from native, the non-additive interaction is

`Delta_(P,Q) - Delta_P - Delta_Q`.

A composable program makes that joint effect predictable; merely adding the two solo cross-entropy numbers is not
assumed correct.
