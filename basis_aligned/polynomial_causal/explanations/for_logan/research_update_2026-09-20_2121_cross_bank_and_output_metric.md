# Cross-bank stability is conditional, and output geometry matters

2026-09-20 21:21 UTC

The registered cross-bank replication changed the six-product bank and fit
four root initializations. Prediction consistency passed, but the all-feature
and output-direction thresholds failed. The first three canonical features
remained strong. The fourth fell to 0.829 in one start, whose root objective
was substantially worse; other fourth-feature cosines were 0.959–0.974.

A separately registered bounded warm-start refinement of that one start
improved root error from 8.02% to 5.20% and fourth-feature cosine to 0.977. All
follow-up bars passed. This supports a finite-budget optimization explanation
for that case; the original fixed-budget replication remains failed. The
selected deployed program never changed. Cross-moments were computed exactly
in a common Gaussian input frame, not by comparing unrelated coordinates.

The vocabulary audit found another important qualification. Canonical output
mode cosines with the uniform vocabulary direction are 0.403, 0.767, 0.150 and
-0.278. The registered common-shift bar fails. Extra GPT2 vocabulary rows
account for less than 0.86% of any mode's energy and pass that control.
Across mode variances, 22.75% of centered Gaussian output energy is a common
logit shift; 77.25% is relative-token contrast. A diagnostic contrast-only
interpretation is exported and replays to 4e-15. It is not a model change.

A common shift before the final softcap is not automatically probability
invariant. Nor do token rankings or spectral stability establish semantic
circuits. The next registered diagnostic measures full-vocabulary contrast
errors of the frozen programs alongside their ordinary errors, without refits.
It will show whether their accuracy comparisons survive this output geometry.
