# The hour everything landed (10:59–11:31)
*Written at 11:31 UTC.*

## Six verdicts in thirty minutes
After a two-and-a-half-hour computation, the big attention experiment
landed — and behind it, five queued experiments drained in four minutes.
The scoreboard:

1. **The attention interface has real, downstream-chosen directions** —
   two of its three latent modes are fixed by what later computation does
   with them, stable across independent refits and immune to the
   "interpolation trap" that fooled earlier fits. But what those
   directions MEAN per circuit doesn't survive changing the data view —
   so the experiment's own strict rules called it a null. The geometry is
   banked; the semantics are not.
2. **MLP0's four exact input pathways are used diffusely** — removing
   them hurts unrelated positions as much as circuit positions. The
   62-circuit lens is the wrong magnification for layer 0.
3. **The reproducibility mystery closed as "contained, cause unknown"**:
   six separate processes now agree bit-for-bit; the one discrete shift
   (between 05:57 and 08:02) touched nothing we can find on disk, in the
   environment, or in the GPU's caches, and library-workspace pinning
   changes nothing. Every future experiment carries its baseline in-run;
   a bit-level tripwire now watches for any second shift.
4. **The third try landed the claim**: under a new, geometry-defined way
   of removing the equality signal (project out only its direction), the
   three cooperating MLPs' joint effect on code text is finally
   CONSISTENT across the two matcher sources (.94 agreement) — where the
   two older removal styles flatly disagreed (+.98 vs −.88). The first
   two attempts died on instrument technicalities (one environmental,
   one my own too-tight tolerance — both preserved as failures, and the
   fix never rescued the originals).
5. **The claim survived its stress test immediately**: split the
   documents in half and the agreement holds in both halves
   (.97 / .87). It also reproduced bit-exactly. This is the program's
   first composition statement that is stable across frames, sources,
   and halves — scope limited, honestly, to code text.
6. **A bonus insight from comparing all three removal styles**: on
   natural text, the joint effect nearly vanishes under the new style
   but was sizable under the old ones — meaning natural-text
   cooperation flows through exactly the part of the signal the new
   style leaves untouched. Code and natural text don't just differ in
   stability; they use geometrically different parts of the same wire.
   That gives the dormant natural-text investigation a sharp opening
   question for when it wakes.

## Where this leaves the map
The compiled-program picture now has: one continuous attention interface
with banked (unnamed) directions; three MLPs that share behavior but not
representation, now with one guarded rule for how they compose on code;
a layer-0 input stage whose influence is diffuse at circuit grain; and
instruments that survived a genuine crisis morning with every published
verdict intact.
