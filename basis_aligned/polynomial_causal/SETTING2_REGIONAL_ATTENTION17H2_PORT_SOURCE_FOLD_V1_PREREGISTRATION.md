# Head17.2 edited-port source fold

The fresh-confirmed head17.2 program keeps QK1 native and moves QK2 plus value
to the state induced by the fixed late-touching head9.8 QK1 edit. This opened-panel
fold asks whether those two edited factor ports can be generated from a sparse
set of explicit downstream module edges.

For attention and MLP writes at layers 9 through 16, define the exact propagated
residual contribution

$$d_i=\left(\prod_{j=\ell(i)+1}^{17}\lambda_{j,0}\right)
       (w_i^{edited}-w_i^{native}).$$

Their 16-term sum must reconstruct the complete layer-17 residual-input change.
For every unit-gain support of zero through three terms, add its contributions
to the native layer-17 residual, recompute native head17.2 QK2 and current value,
keep head17.2 QK1 native, and execute the resulting corner write. Exhaustive
selection minimizes aggregate paired UK-minus-US response error, breaking ties
by smaller support and lexicographic order. No gains are fit.

Frozen predictions:

1. `pred_a_exact_instrument`: exactly 12 full executions; carry and manual
   attention9 errors, 16-term residual reconstruction, zero/full support factor
   endpoints, and head17.2 projection endpoints are each at most `2e-6`; the
   inherited first-layer value state is unchanged; all outputs are finite.
2. `pred_b_sparse_port_response`: the selected support has at most three terms,
   aggregate response relative L2 at most `.25`, cosine at least `.90`, and
   each-family relative L2 at most `.35` against the complete edited-QK2/value
   corner.
3. `pred_c_causal_installation`: selected versus complete corner installation
   into the native layer-17 background has relative L2 at most `.35`, cosine at
   least `.90`, and sign agreement at least `.80` in each family.
4. `pred_d_causal_removal`: the same bars hold for removal from the fully edited
   layer-17 background.
5. `pred_e_control_nonworsening`: for installation and removal, each of five
   unrelated-reader RMS effects is no more than `1.25×` the complete corner plus
   `1e-6` in each family.
6. `pred_f_support_specificity`: the selected aggregate response error plus `.05`
   is no greater than the median error of sixteen deterministic equal-cardinality
   support nulls (or all alternatives if fewer exist).
