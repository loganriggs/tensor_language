# Numeric sequence nonadjacent OOD capability v1

## Question

Does the model natively perform `+1` continuation on fresh digit and number-word sequences whose paired states are separated by five, before any exact-factor or interaction intervention is opened?

## Frozen authority

The model-free builder creates 32 pairs and 64 endpoints. Two fresh prompt constructions (`fresh_series`, `fresh_instruction`) are crossed with digit and number-word representations. Each of the four cells contains 16 endpoints. Every endpoint is a three-value `+1` sequence; paired starting values differ by five, and neither expected answer is the partner's final visible value. Rows, vocabulary, answers, order, and manifest are frozen by the binding receipt.

## Predictions

- A: all hashes, manifest, four-cell balance, single-token answers, checkpoint, and the one-forward/64-sequence price are exact.
- B: native digit accuracy is at least 0.85 and mean correct-versus-partner-answer margin is positive in each digit construction.
- C: the same bars hold independently in each number-word construction.
- D: there are no interventions, fits, parameter updates, row filters, ranks, or quantization.

A pass licenses a separately preregistered exact-factor screen that compares the shared L8H3+H7 cached-value payload with complete value and score/value interaction arms. A failed cell closes without threshold or row repair.
