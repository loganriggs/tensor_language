# Numeric successor spaced OOD capability v1

## Question

Can a fresh authority support the numbered-list successor and both copy-control behaviors before any causal transfer is opened? The positive endpoints use final visible values separated by five. This removes the prior adjacent-pair overlap in which the lower endpoint's expected successor was the higher endpoint's visible source label.

## Frozen authority

The model-free builder creates 48 pairs and 96 endpoints. There are two new construction cells (`fresh_plain`, `fresh_headered`) crossed with three roles: step-two numbered lists, repeated digit copy, and repeated number-word copy. Each cell has 16 endpoints. Vocabulary, prompts, row order, answers, and the row manifest are frozen by the binding receipt.

## Predictions and bars

- A: hashes, manifest, six-cell balance, answer tokenization, checkpoint, and one-forward/96-sequence price are exact.
- B: native accuracy is at least 0.85 and mean correct-versus-partner-answer margin is positive in both numbered-list cells.
- C: the same bar holds independently in all four digit/word copy-control cells.
- D: no intervention, fitting, parameter update, rank selection, quantization, or row filtering occurs.

A capability pass licenses a separate preregistered bidirectional cached-value transfer test. Any failed cell closes this authority without threshold or row repair.
