# ODD_ATTENTION8H2_DESTINATION_RESPONSE_QUOTIENT_V1 preregistration

Deduplicate the frozen four-family destination-role panel to16 paired contexts;
each forward scores all six spelling endpoints and four unrelated controls.
Families0--1 define the discovery response direction, while families2--3 remain
held out. For every framing destination, intervene on only that destination's
inherited-city head8.2 edge and propagate its state change through head9.8 O and
the native suffix. Also run native and full-destination arms. No semantic labels,
position partitions, or model outcomes enter the split.

- **A — instrument:** hashes, one-token pairs, source/write/full-effect anchors,
  outside-destination zeros, and finite/live single-destination effects pass the
  existing `1e-5` native tolerance.
- **B — discovery quotient:** after centering only by the frozen native baseline,
  one right singular response direction explains at least95% of the discovery
  single-destination target-effect matrix's squared energy.
- **C — held-out identification:** project held-out single-destination target
  effects onto the discovery direction without refitting. Aggregate cosine is at
  least.9 and relative reconstruction error at most35% in each held-out family.
- **D — composition:** the sum of separately measured destination target effects
  predicts the jointly executed full-destination effect within10% per family.
- **E — selectivity:** each single-destination and summed unrelated-control RMS is
  at most.5 of the corresponding target-effect RMS per family.

Passing identifies destinations by operational equivalence under downstream
responses: one shared six-endpoint response direction plus a scalar gain per
destination. It does not make description/instruction or early/late labels valid,
and it is not adopted as compression unless the held-out and composition gates
pass. Native state generators and suffix remain external.
