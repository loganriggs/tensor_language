# Hourly strategic review — 2026-09-01 21:36 UTC

## Goal and current frontier

The goal remains a compiled, predictive, manipulable account of the bilin18 model: small enough to expose reusable
structure, accurate on held-out and changed distributions, able to predict named circuit behavior, and editable or
removable without moving unrelated circuits. A decomposition is not adopted because it reconstructs activations or
uses few parameters; those are candidate simplicity measures whose value must be earned by the four consequences.

This hour produced two durable changes.

1. **Simplicity itself is now a learnable, falsifiable object.** Solved whole-circuit families teach separate small
   predictors for OOD transport, extraction, selective editing, and composition from complete price, graph/interface,
   gauge-safe dimension, locality, reuse, conditioning, and causal distortion. Different development families choose
   the rule and search. Sealed circuit families, intervention kinds, and composition partners judge a program built
   under the frozen rule. Primary evidence is prospective pairwise consequence ordering at matched causal distortion,
   not the surrogate value optimized by search.
2. **The attention0 normalizer route has been causally confirmed and algebraically narrowed.** Rung433b shows that
   replacing only 36 token-dependent Q/K RMS factors by constants causes +.32122 nat CE and product error6.3985.
   Rung435 then shows that the 36 exact quadratic matrices do not have useful common-congruence structure: their union
   is full rank1152, 99% of union energy needs1106 dimensions, real whitened commutators are 1.018 times the most
   favorable destroyed-alignment control, and the best diagonalization residual is 1.006 times control. The strong
   null fires. Do not fit a common R256 positive-square model.

## Assumptions and confounds

- Rung435 rejects a *global common congruence* of the complete weight matrices. It does not reject approximation only
  on the 50,257 token states, nor a block-specific basis frozen from independent head/map evidence.
- Coordinate-permutation controls preserve spectra, ranks, and entries but not every trained weight statistic. The
  result is nevertheless not a marginal miss: both registered real/control ratios are slightly worse than one.
- The exact denominator table's 1,809,252-scalar price excludes all Q/K numerators, rotary handling, decoders, and
  reciprocal-square-root computation. It is not a 66% complete Q/K compression despite being smaller than the native
  5,308,416 Q/K maps.
- The historical experiment ledger is adaptive. A chronological split can reject weak simplicity rules, but cannot
  confirm one; confirmation needs circuit-family splits chosen before labels are assembled.

## Independent alternative directions

1. **Private normalizers plus shared score computation.** Treat denominator functions as head/map-private while
   retaining the near-lossless continuous six/six score quotient. Ask whether the private part can be tabled or
   block-factored at complete price without destabilizing the shared downstream interface.
2. **Token-manifold-only quadratic approximation.** The weight-space union is full, but the finite token states may
   admit a smaller evaluation-equivalent quadratic program. This must be priced as an evaluator and compared against
   the exact table, with unseen contextual states as the decisive OOD test.
3. **Archetypal score-profile identification.** Convex-hull anchors may stabilize observable score-profile atoms even
   though arbitrary signed Q/K atoms are gauge-unstable. Judge by restart identity, held-out scores, interventions,
   and composition—not geometric anchoring alone.
4. **Learned simplicity objective.** Build the cheap chronological audit, but reserve untouched circuit families now.
   Use uncertainty-aware search and adversarial fake-reuse/hidden-native/inert-sparsity/collateral-moving candidates.
5. **MLP0 exact token/context decomposition.** Continue the independent MLP0 route rather than letting the attention
   result monopolize effort: token-only is exact identity plus common/linear/quadratic pieces; the live open problem is
   finding context and token×context programs that predict downstream effects and survive selective interventions.

## What would falsify the live approaches

- Private/table normalizers fail if their complete physical program does not beat the existing sparse Q/K or native
  maps at matched CE and downstream consequences.
- Token-manifold compression fails if it does not transfer from exact token states to held-out contextual residual
  states, even when token IDs repeat.
- Archetypal constraints fail as an identifiability tool if restart matching improves but OOD score predictions,
  signed removals, and composition do not.
- A learned simplicity rule fails if it cannot prospectively order consequences on sealed whole-circuit families, or
  if optimizer-found programs leave its calibrated feature support or exploit hidden cost/collateral.
