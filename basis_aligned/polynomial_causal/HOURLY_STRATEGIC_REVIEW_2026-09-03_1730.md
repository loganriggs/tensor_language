## Goal and evidence standard

The program goal is a compiled description of bilin18 whose pieces are both predictive and manipulable. A claimed
circuit should:

1. generalize to held-out and genuinely shifted examples without refitting;
2. be extractable as a concrete computation, not just a correlated activation or architectural component;
3. be removable or interchangeable while preserving unrelated computations;
4. compose and recur across examples or tasks in ways the description predicts;
5. translate toward executable weight-level objects.

Fewer dimensions, lower reconstruction error, smaller storage, quantization, or lower matrix rank do not establish any
of these properties by themselves. They may be engineering measurements after a circuit is identified, but they are
not circuit evidence and must not choose the research direction.

## Mathematical step-back

The model is a tensor program. Its natural units need not be layers, heads, or low-dimensional Euclidean subspaces.
At a fixed attention source, the exact L13H8 write is

$$
c_{qk}=
\left(\frac{\langle q_q,k_k\rangle}{128}
      \frac{\langle q'_q,k'_k\rangle}{128}\right)
W^O_{13,8}v_k.
$$

This gives an exact score factor, an exact projected-value factor, and an exact product. R560 asks which factor carries
the counterfactual variable while all other computation stays live. The score×payload interaction

$$
I=\Delta_{sp}-\Delta_s-\Delta_p
$$

tests whether the causal effect is additive or genuinely depends on their composition. For several candidate factors,
the analogous full subset table can be Möbius-inverted on the Boolean lattice to measure unique and interaction effects
without pretending architectural boundaries are independent causes.

This suggests a general circuit representation: semantic counterfactual variable, exact tensor factors that transport
it, downstream response equivalence, and causal interaction coefficients. Gauge freedom is handled by grouping
representations that downstream computation cannot distinguish, rather than privileging a raw coordinate basis.
