# Rung445 preregistration: prospective consequence-bank freezer

Status: registered before the freezer output exists. CPU-only structural audit. This rung loads no candidate
consequence file, model checkpoint, row cache, or evaluation role and authorizes no GPU queue entry.

## Decision this rung resolves

Rung441 produced45 structurally priced candidate programs but too few removal and composition outcomes to learn a
consequence-specific simplicity rule. Before generating those expensive labels, determine whether the exact45
candidates form a rebuildable, whole-family-separated teaching/confirmation bank.

Rung443's fixed structural score predicts candidate reconstruction recovery between MLP0 and MLP1. It is retained as
a fixed comparison but is not renamed a removal or composition predictor. The causal consequence predictor must be
fit on teaching-family removal/composition labels and frozen before confirmation-family labels are opened.

## Rebuild certificate

Every candidate row must be copied without outcome fields and extended only with:

- its frozen role;
- a hash-pinned producer source;
- rebuild mode: deterministic refit from the producer source, or retained bundle plus deterministic arm transform;
- hash-pinned required structural artifacts that already exist;
- the source receipt hashes already present in rung441.

The freezer verifies source syntax with Python byte-code compilation. It does not execute producer code. Existence
and syntax establish that a consequence experiment can be built; they do not claim that all45 programs have been
executed under the new common harness.

## Frozen future consequence contract

This rung generates no outcomes, but freezes the common questions so candidates are not selected around answers:

1. **Signed removal preservation:** with candidate program `P` installed, apply one distant original-native
   attention16 mean knockout. Compare its signed target effect and non-target collateral with the same knockout in
   the native model.
2. **Composition:** install `P` with the physically factored MLP16 rank2 program. Measure the joint change and the
   non-additive interaction
   `Delta_(P,Q) - Delta_P - Delta_Q` on fresh rows.

Later preregistrations must freeze the row roles, exact target/collateral vectors, prices, and numerical bars before
executing either consequence. Teaching outcomes open first. A consequence-specific predictor is then fit and frozen.
Only afterward may the attention0 confirmation outcomes open.

  family counts appear; structural rows contain no CE, loss, damage, error, fidelity, accuracy, cosine, correlation,
  certificate, outcome, predicate, or null key; the consequence file is never loaded.

- **C — teaching support:** at least30 buildable teaching candidates span exactly three whole families, with at

- **D — sealed support:** at least8 buildable sealed candidates span exactly the one unseen attention0 family,

buildable teaching candidates, fewer than8 buildable sealed candidates, or any candidate lacking a source/artifact
hash. Under the null, do not generate consequence labels; repair the bank from a separately registered source set.

learned-simplicity claim, opening confirmation outcomes, score-guided search, compression, or adoption.

Literal price: zero deployed model values, zero native calls, and CPU filesystem/syntax work only. Claim level:
