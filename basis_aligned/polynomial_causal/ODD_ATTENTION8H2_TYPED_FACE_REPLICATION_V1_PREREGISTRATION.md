# Typed-face inherited-city graph replication V1

The first prospective sparse-graph test showed that unconstrained atom-wise
selection can splice terms from different physical subgraphs: it selected the
inherited-value singleton together with current-value terms. Its aggregate OOD
prediction passed, but the British-direction composition cell narrowly failed.

Before scoring this replication panel, freeze a typed alternative. Head8.2
routing multiplies each value source, while current and inherited values add.
Therefore compare exactly two complete multiplicative faces on the 48 authored
discovery rows:

1. routing × current value: masks `(1,2,3)`;
2. routing × inherited value: masks `(1,4,5)`.

`sparse_interaction_graph.face_masks` constructs each face and
`select_graph_family` chooses the lower unscaled discovery error. It may not
drop a main effect or interaction within a face. The chosen graph is then
frozen before evaluating 96 newly harvested natural FineWeb rows whose eight
source documents do not overlap the first natural panel. All eight corners are
measured for evaluation, costing 1,152 body forwards over discovery plus
replication; there are no fits, gradients, or model updates.

- **A — instrument:** hashes, pairing, source disjointness, native source replay,
  routing/value identities, full-corner construction, Möbius closure, and exact
  typed-selector replay pass within `1e-5`; all corners are live and finite,
  destinations are exact, and 1,152 forwards execute.
- **B — frozen OOD prediction:** replication relative L2 is at most `.35`,
  cosine is at least `.9`, and there are no sign reversals where the full effect
  magnitude is at least `1e-5`.
- **C — composition:** selected atoms sum to the full-corner effect within `.35`
  separately for British and American rows and for every endpoint.
- **D — reusable sparse graph:** discovery error is at most `.30`, the selected
  complete face requires at most four of eight corners after freezing, and all
  three selected atoms are live on replication.
- **E — selectivity:** each unrelated predicted readout RMS is at most `.5` of
  target RMS on replication.

This is a prospective replication of a method designed after the V1 miss, not a
reinterpretation of V1. It still does not certify four traits: a standalone
typed-face package and equal-norm same-site removal null remain required.
