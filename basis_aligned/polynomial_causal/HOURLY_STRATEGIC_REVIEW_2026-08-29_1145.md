## What was actually completed this review

### 1. Outcome-sealed selection batch primitive

For each four-document batch, the new owner runs one canonical native trajectory and
the eight frozen physical candidates. Every candidate uses a live sequential
counterfactual: a later changed head reads the residual state produced by earlier
changes. Raw logits remain inside the call and are immediately reduced to
document-by-cell sufficient statistics.

For candidate (a), document (d), and cell (c), the retained objects are only:
the number of positions, native/candidate summed negative log likelihood, native and
candidate correct counts, native-to-candidate KL sum, and an exact support digest.
The causal estimator is

$$
\widehat\tau_{a,c}=
\frac{\sum_d\sum_{p\in c_d}
  [\ell_{a,dp}-\ell_{\mathrm{native},dp}]}
 {\sum_d |c_d|}.
$$

Production merging now requires exact document order, exact candidate head plans,
exact native-versus-adapter calls at every layer, retained closures, nonnegative finite
recomposition errors below 0.003 relative error, and bit-identical native baseline
statistics across all eight candidates. Recursive logit-nonescape and injected partial
forward/reducer failures are tested. The focused current suite passes 25 tests.
