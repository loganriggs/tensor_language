# Content-API tensor compiler specification

## Purpose

The current 36/36 ship is a replacement-scope success but remains about `+0.93`
cross-entropy above the frozen clean anchor. The residual evidence points to content,
not another isolated selector: novel/rare targets carry `0.473` of ship damage, and
MLP0-2 account for `0.499` of the separately measured frequent-target damage.

The compiler target is therefore not an unchanged 1152-output MLP. The weights-only
audit gives those full maps numerical output-flattening rank `1152/1152` at registered
relative tolerances through `1e-4`, evidence against near-exact coefficient
degeneracy at that scale. It is not a symbolic rank proof or a natural-activation
compression result, and the practical coefficient-space knee remains unmeasured.
The target is a narrower causal content API whose factors can be priced and installed
as a correction inside the running ship.

## Typed object

At a registered boundary immediately after component `l`, define

```
z_l       : post-RMSNorm MLP input in R^1152
y_l       : MLP output in R^1152
c_l       : U_l^T (y_l - mean_l) in R^m
Q_l(z_l)  : sum_i c_i (a_i.z_l)(b_i.z_l), i=1..k
```

`U_l` is fitted on discovery rows only and frozen before compiler fitting. Every
candidate records whether `U_l` is:

- the established content-coordinate basis;
- a local transported version of that basis;
- or a ship-residual basis discovered without behavior labels.

Those variants are separate grammars and may not share headline numbers. RMSNorm is
outside `Q_l`; its live/frozen semantics are explicit in every intervention.

## What existing evidence licenses

- Content reading is compact: the top-k sparse skeleton in a 64-dimensional content
  coordinate system carries most causal read-side value.
- Content construction is not a fixed linear lookup: the construction budget is
  approximately 53% transport, 17% attention injection, and 30% created in flight.
- That last 30% is not recovered by quadratic products of the 64 content coordinates
  (`R^2` only `0.705 -> 0.711`, with a rotated-coordinate null at `0.707`), nor by
  widening a *linear* input read through 512 raw dimensions. The paired factors must
  therefore read the real `z_l` and earn their narrower output API; a polynomial only
  over the already-compressed content state is a registered failed route.
- Consequently, the compiler must still execute nonlinear products. It is trying to
  share and prune those products at a causal boundary, not tabulate the computation.
- Scalar slices can simplify dramatically: the rank-2 indefinite question slice has
  a one-product exact form. However, its matched-cost causal test favored neither
  exact geometry nor scalar reconstruction as a behavioral criterion: the best
  square had 35.4% held-out scalar error but only 0.39% of zero-slice question KL.
  Full vector maps do not show the same algebraic collapse. Both facts push the
  compiler toward a causally scored joint interface.

## First executable ladder

1. **Local interface control.** Project the real MLP output onto frozen `U_l`. Verify
   identity replacement, null replacement, and basis-gauge invariance.
2. **Matched product frontiers.** At `k in {16,32,64,128,256,512}`, compare:
   original-unit selection, unconstrained learned paired factors, a linear map, and
   a random-subspace control. Every product feature family gets its optimal
   discovery-fitted linear decoder; retaining native output weights is not the
   unit-selection baseline. Price projections, products, decoder, and precision.
3. **Ship-residual correction.** Fit factors on inputs generated by the running ship,
   targeting `U_l^T(y_clean-y_ship)`, then install the correction without making any
   other plank real.
4. **Causal transport.** Hold out intervention families and predict their downstream
   content-coordinate and output-logit effects. Test pairwise installation with a
   second correction before extending the ladder.
5. **Whole-model admission.** Admit a factor library only if it improves the current
   composite on fresh rows and retains its advantage after amortized pricing.

## Registered first-stage gates

The first MLP0-2 content correction is a win only if all of the following hold:

1. on fresh rows, it removes at least `0.05` nats of the current ship's global gap;
2. novel/rare CE improvement is at least 10% of that cell's ship excess, without a
   frequent/copy cell worsening by more than `0.01` nats;
3. at matched product count and encoded bits, learned paired factors beat both
   original-unit selection and a linear correction;
4. normalized error on held-out intervention responses is at most `0.25`;
5. the gain retains at least 75% when composed with the best independently fitted
   second correction;
6. registered input/output gauge changes alter product count by zero and encoded
   price by at most 1%.

Failing (1) or (2) demotes the early correction and moves the same ladder to the
deep content boundary. Failing (3) rejects tensor-specific compilation at that
interface. Failing (4) keeps the candidate as compression only, not reverse
engineering. Failing (5) rejects admission to the whole-model program.

## First-stage result (2026-08-27)

At `k=32`, learned paired factors beat native-product selection and seeded random
products on untouched rows at all of MLP0-2, but lose to the parameter-matched linear
map by `0.097`, `0.135`, and `0.170` whitened `R2`. The best paired program reaches
only `0.542`, below the registered `0.60` gate, while the MLP0 linear arm reaches
`0.639`. Validation generalization and reciprocal factor-gauge tests pass.

Thus gate 3 fails and tensor-specific compilation is rejected at the early local
interface. No paired factors are installed in the ship. The linear arm is not yet a
ship win: it predicts clean local output rather than `y_clean-y_ship`. Its next test
is conditional on the factorial residual cross-tab in `RESIDUAL_FACTORIAL_SPEC.md`.

The selected-native arm retains 69% of MLP0 linear `R2` for 1/35 the decoder
parameters, but this is only a conditional amortized point. Until its two projection
factors are independently admitted and paid for as a reused library, its primary
standalone price is `75840` parameters and it too is dominated by the `73792`-parameter
linear map. Original-model provenance is not a free decoder.

## Leakage and accounting rules

- Discovery rows choose `U_l`, compiler parameters, product count, and quantization.
  Evaluation rows choose none of them.
- Output classes used to discover a basis are disjoint from classes used to score
  circuit localization or removal.
- The real orthogonal complement may remain live during interface experiments, but
  the result must be labeled a slice correction rather than a module replacement.
- A 36/36 scope count, local reconstruction score, or sum of marginal improvements
  is never substituted for current-composite delta-CE.
- Tensor decompositions are compiler upper bounds unless a lower bound meets them.
