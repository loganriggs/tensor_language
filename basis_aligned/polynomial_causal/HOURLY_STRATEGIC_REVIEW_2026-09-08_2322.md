## Circuit interpretation targets and full goal

A useful circuit decomposition must eventually provide all seven targets: (1) a computational
specification of what is read, computed, written, and consumed downstream; (2) cross-boundary
grouping and within-module splitting according to operational equivalence rather than native module
names; (3) held-out and OOD prediction; (4) extraction or sufficiency of an executable interface;
(5) selective removal, swap, and edit tests that account for interactions and redundancy; (6)
composition and reuse of shared subcomputations with task-specific branches; and (7) stable
identification across corpora, gauges, and fitting restarts. The full goal remains a transparent
tensor program that is jointly predictive, composable, manipulable, and literally simpler in
storage, compute, edges, and state. Rank, reconstruction, variance, and CE are controls or prices,
not substitutes for those circuit properties.

## Confounds, alternatives, and direction

- Baselines and nonlinearity: v23 effects are paired native differences. The factorial's exact joint
  residual-plus-M11 restoration is the instrument positive control; RMSNorm forbids treating raw
  static weight composition as exact state transport.
- Frame/padding and token difficulty: v23 has equal semantic endpoints and exact self closures. v24
  freezes construction cells before results and scores every target row in its causal stage rather
  than selecting jointly correct examples.
- Leakage and post-selection: P/C, direction, construction, and reporter halves remain separate. No
  v23 spectral ladder may choose the shared-core rank tested on v24.
- Gauge and precision: private head coordinates are not compared literally. The common residual
  projector is invariant under independent private rotations, has an exact optimum certificate, and
  all finite/shape/replay contracts are tested. A spectral gap proves projector uniqueness only, not
  semantic uniqueness.
- Fitting norm: heterogeneous private widths can dominate raw concatenated Frobenius energy. The norm
  is now explicit; alternatives must be preregistered. Writer and reader tensors should initially be
  decomposed separately because mixing their units in one norm would be another hidden weighting.
- Dataset decomposition: PCA/SAE/hierarchical SAE can reveal reachable or discrete activation regimes,
  but cannot enumerate all operations permitted by the restricted checkpoint. It remains an occupancy
  overlay after the weight-defined interface is frozen.

The current route is still highest-information. Direct causal branch and reader localization can
falsify the circuit interpretation immediately; weight decomposition then tests cross-boundary reuse
on a causally live object. CP/Tucker/HT or SAE before that boundary would mainly optimize rank or
reconstruction. A direct residual-to-final RMSNorm/unembedding model remains the genuinely different
fallback if no downstream module passes reciprocal tests.
