# Rung 566: development-only increment format exploration

R564 showed that the first natural prose template elicited copy/repeat but not `+1`. R566 is an exploratory template
screen, not confirmatory circuit evidence. It compares six prompt formats under three rules (`+1`, copy, and `+2`) on
a development-only pool. Its rows and outcomes are permanently excluded from later FIT, SELECT, FINAL_TEST, and OOD.

Registered expectations are: the known two-line digit numbered-list anchor reaches at least 75% numeric-candidate
accuracy on `+1`; at least one other digit format reaches 75%; and at least one number-word format reaches 50%.
Regardless of the outcome, any selected construction must be rebuilt with unseen lexical and numeric groups and
frozen before component localization.
