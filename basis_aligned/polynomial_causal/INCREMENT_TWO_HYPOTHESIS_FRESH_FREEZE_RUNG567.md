# Rung 567 plan: fresh evidence for two numeric hypotheses

R566 was development-only and showed that “increment” should not be treated as one behavior. The next fresh authority
will separate:

1. **Numbered-list index successor.** Two- and three-line numbered lists strongly predict the next index under `+1`
   but do not continue a `+2` arithmetic sequence. The candidate computation is “advance the list label,” not general
   addition.
2. **Numeric-sequence continuation.** Comma-separated digits and number words support `+1` and copy, while `+2` is
   weaker. The candidate computation may contain representation-shared sequence state plus rule-specific output work.

R567 will use new semantic groups and disjoint lexical pools. No exact R566 prompt, noun triple, or oriented pair may
be reused. Each group will include answer-changing state shifts, answer-preserving surface changes, a broken-relation
necessity row, copy controls, and `+2` specificity rows. Digit/word cross-format rows will be attached only to the
comma-sequence hypothesis. The split unit is the full semantic group across every derived family.

The dataset builder is CPU-only and opens no model output. A later native-capability gate must score the two hypotheses
separately; failure of one cannot be hidden by averaging it with the other. Only a held native gate may reopen component
localization, and the old L8H7/L8H3/MLP8--14 result is initially a hypothesis only for numbered-list successor.
