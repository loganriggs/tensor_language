# Hourly strategic review — 2026-09-03 14:30 UTC

## Goal and success criteria

The goal is a simpler, predictive, manipulable tensor program for bilin18. A proposed
circuit is useful only when it supports:

1. held-out and genuinely OOD prediction;
2. extraction/sufficiency of the claimed computation;
3. selective removal or interchange without damaging unrelated circuits;
4. compositional reuse, including measured interactions with other circuit variables;
5. a physical weight-level realization whose behavior matches the activation claim.

Low rank, parameter count, quantization, or reconstruction error alone satisfies none of
these. Rank is a budget inside a causally validated hypothesis, not the research target.
