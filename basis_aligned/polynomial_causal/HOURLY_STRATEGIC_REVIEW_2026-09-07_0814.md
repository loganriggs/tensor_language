## DAS regularization and confound audit

held-out error from about `.4485` to `.2445`, near difference-in-means `.2385`; tangent noise alone
stays near `.4486`, and complete-family transfer still fails. Thus noise is not magic regularization,
and complement inertness cannot identify the intended subspace because it is also the criterion the
optimizer can exploit. A serious combined optimizer must select on complete held-out families and keep
KL, noise, cross-fit, and weight execution as independent tests.

reuse beyond the two temporal tasks, and lack of a reverse necessity/removal execution. The `.80`
behavior boundary is close, so further same-population pruning would amplify post-selection risk.
