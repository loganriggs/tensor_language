# Hourly strategic review — 2026-09-07 04:14 UTC

## Circuit target and controlling goal

A completed circuit must identify what is read, computed, written, and consumed; group or split
native modules by causal function; predict held-out and OOD effects; support extraction, removal,
swaps, and edits; compose shared and task-specific branches; and remain stable across documents,
constructions, controls, gauges, and starts. The controlling goal remains a smaller transparent
tensor program that is jointly predictive, composable, manipulable, and cheaper in literal
storage, compute, state, edges, and intervention price. Activation replay, a high-ranking weight
path, or a fitted subspace alone is not a completion certificate.

## What changed since 03:14

The five-MLP response program transferred on the fresh temporal-v13 construction. Its five MLPs
were then expanded through their literal bilinear weight tensors. The first run exposed an invalid

That boundary was attacked with a complete upstream intervention atlas: all 162 attention heads in

The user's constrained-DAS memorization diagnosis was directly red-teamed. Starting from the
difference-in-means axis, aligned KL regularization reduced held-out full-vocabulary error from
`.5292` to `.0632`, close to the DIM floor `.05923`; tangent noise alone remained `.5187`. Under a
hard target-feasibility constraint, 16 trained checkpoints improved the poor DIM-start secondary
loss, but no update beat the frozen pooled transferable axis. The selected pooled axis achieved
sealed-family mean/worst loss `1.045/1.363`, versus `2.473/2.725` for the original DIM axis; noisy
and deterministic optimization were effectively identical. Thus regularization can prevent a bad
task-specific solution, but the current optimizer has not discovered a better transferable axis
than pooled difference-in-means. Further KL/noise sweeps are demoted unless the target family or
objective changes.

The independent seven-task circuit line clarified routing. Exact head sets plus the shared MLP7--10
band recover roughly `.92--1.03` of donor margin and are subadditive. Direction-only patches route

registered claim that direction routing should systematically improve on exact routing failed.
This supports a reusable head-write / middle-MLP-read motif without making the fitted direction a
superior causal object.

## Confounds and route decision

The highest-information next experiment is the preregistered tensor-ranked greedy union over the
top 20 upstream sites. It directly asks whether the strong incidence screen composes into at most
eight jointly necessary sites, preserves MLP1 plus a known task writer, reproduces both behavior and
physical modes, and remains inert on same-answer controls. Its first leave-one-out panel is frozen;
failed sites will not be adaptively deleted and retested.

Confounds explicitly controlled or retained:

- **Generic donor transport:** MLP1 dominates every downstream target; same-answer C controls and
  the full-pool-versus-small-program comparison distinguish selectivity from broad activation copy.
- **Post-selection:** the top-20 pool comes from the sealed atlas and the greedy/LOO rules were
  registered before outcomes. No simultaneous backward-pruning pass will rewrite a failed set.
- **Tensor score versus causal use:** selection evaluates native interventions against behavior and
  both physical reader modes; tensor rank only defines the frozen candidate pool.
- **Nonlinear composition:** individual left/right/interaction closure does not imply that multiple
  native modules compose additively. The greedy experiment runs every tested union through the
  transformer.
- **Scale and precision:** absolute errors are no longer interpreted without a target-relative
  denominator; both prior scale failures remain preserved as invalid evidence.
- **DAS task leakage:** complement KL/noise can regularize memorization, but model selection must use
  sealed families and hard target sufficiency. The pooled axis is the present frontier, not proof of
  uniqueness or mechanistic identity.
