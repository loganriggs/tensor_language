# Preregistration: direct non-head attention0 score generators

Date: 2026-09-01 20:51 UTC

Status: frozen before implementation, GPU execution, or result inspection. Discovery/feasibility screen only; no
adoption, semantic-unit, or whole-program simplicity claim is licensed by this rung.

## Question

Rungs 424/425 found a document-stable rank-6 basis across the nine first-branch scores and another rank-6 basis
across the nine second-branch scores. Their complete product with a rank-32 output payload preserved the measured
attention0 write almost losslessly, but the screen first computed all native per-head Q/K values. This rung asks
whether the two six-coordinate score objects are themselves cheaper to generate.

The changed object is a directly executable bilinear map from the exact layer-0 normalized token state. It never
calls the four native layer-0 Q/K projections. The primary matched comparison is:

- **MODE96:** six non-head score coordinates per branch, each generated by one width-96 query/key bilinear pair,
  then decoded through the frozen rank-6-to-nine rung-424 score basis;
- **HEAD64:** nine architectural-head score coordinates per branch, each generated by one width-64 query/key
  bilinear pair.

Their input-map price is identical:

`2 branches × 2 sides × 6 modes × 1,152 × 96 = 2,654,208 values`,

`2 branches × 2 sides × 9 heads × 1,152 × 64 = 2,654,208 values`.

All biases, score decoders, rotary-frequency metadata, and constants are included in the complete bill. The small
decoder difference is reported rather than hidden. The native four-map price is `4 × 1,152² = 5,308,416 values`.

## Exact computation

For token `t`, use the exact state supplied to attention0,

`x(t) = RMSNorm((lambda_0 + lambda_1) × RMSNorm(embedding(t)))`.

For branch `b`, mode `j`, query token `u`, source token `v`, and offset `delta`, MODE96 computes

`q_bj(u) = x(u) A_bj`, `k_bj(v) = x(v) B_bj`,

`z_bj(u,v,delta) = q_bj(u)^T R_delta k_bj(v) / 96`.

`R_delta` uses 48 evenly spaced frequency pairs from the native 64-pair rotary grid. HEAD64 uses the analogous
formula with 32 evenly spaced native frequency pairs. Neither arm applies a hidden native headwise RMS operation;
the maps must learn the realized normalized score functions they claim to replace. MODE96 reconstructs nine scores
as `mean_b + z_b basis_b^T`; HEAD64 emits the nine scores directly.

The rung-424 rank-6 bases and means are refit deterministically from its frozen FIT rows and must bridge the stored
SELECT result before either new arm is interpreted. Training uses only FIT natural edges. The objective is the mean
of normalized branch-score error and the same individual-plus-head-summed downstream-metric complete-product error
used by rung 424. Rank, frequencies, objective weights, steps, seeds, and roles are frozen here; SELECT is not used
for model or hyperparameter selection.

## Roles and controls

- FIT/SELECT documents and query positions are exactly rung 424's bound role tensors and positions.
- A token-population holdout uses token IDs modulo five: maps are fitted only from natural edges whose query and
  source tokens are both FIT token types; SELECT reports both all natural edges and the subset containing a held-out
  token type.
- **MODE96-DERANGED** keeps every learned value and price but cyclically pairs second-branch mode `j` with decoder
  column `j+2`; this tests contraction identity.
- **QUERY-SOURCE-PERMUTED** is trained with source-token identities permuted within FIT while preserving query,
  offset, and marginal token frequencies; it tests whether generic offset/frequency structure suffices.
- An untrained zero-score prediction and the frozen native/rung-424 projected scores are measurement anchors.
- Natural SELECT documents measure score error, the two-score product, full attention0 write with the exact native
  value/output side, the six immediate consumers, and exact suffix CE in two document waves.
- During candidate evaluation, all four native layer-0 Q/K weights are poisoned. Candidate outputs must be bitwise
  unchanged and literal native-QK calls must be zero.

## Frozen predictions

### A. Instrument and independence

All of the following must hold: exact role hashes and zero document overlap; native score replay error at most
`1e-10`; refit rung-424 SELECT summed-edge relative MSE within `0.005` and routed-U16 R² within `0.005` of the stored
`0.0097180294` and `0.9852544171`; finite training; declared rotary frequencies; complete price accounting; poisoned
native-QK candidate invariance exactly zero; and zero literal native layer-0 Q/K calls.

### B. A direct composite generator is feasible

On SELECT, MODE96 must have mean branch-score relative squared error at most `0.25`, complete two-score-product
relative squared error at most `0.20`, full attention0-write relative squared error at most `0.20`, routed-U16 R² at
least `0.85`, mean six-consumer R² at least `0.85`, and CE damage at most `+0.010 nat` in each wave.

### C. The non-head basis beats the matched head basis

MODE96 and HEAD64 are compared using their complete bills. MODE96 must have no more than `0.80 ×` HEAD64's
full-write error, no more than `0.80 ×` its two-score-product error, and CE damage no worse by more than `0.002 nat`.
The same error ordering must hold on the held-out-token edge subset. This is the key basis comparison.

### D. The learned pairing is specific

MODE96-DERANGED must have at least `1.25 ×` MODE96's product and full-write errors and at least `+0.010 nat` more CE
damage. QUERY-SOURCE-PERMUTED must have at least `1.25 ×` MODE96's mean branch-score error on SELECT and must not
beat MODE96 on any complete-product metric.

## Strong null and routing

The strong null fires if A fails; or MODE96 product error exceeds `0.50`; or its full-write error exceeds `0.50`;
or its CE damage exceeds `+0.030 nat`; or MODE96 fails to improve either product or full-write error over HEAD64.

- If A/B/C/D hold and the null is false, proceed to a fresh-corpus/offset guard and then a full value/output plus
  first-value-bus generator. This rung alone licenses only “the non-head score basis is cheaper to generate at this
  matched price.”
- If B holds but C fails, direct bilinear generation works but architectural heads remain as good a generator basis;
  retain rung 424 only as a downstream quotient.
- If the strong null fires, stop this direct linear-bilinear family. The likely missing operation is the token-specific
  headwise RMS denominator; test an explicitly shared nonlinear norm generator rather than increasing rank blindly.

## Relation to simplicity consequences

Fewer values and better matched-fidelity execution are only the resource side of the project contract. Even a full
pass does not establish interpretability. A promoted candidate must later predict shifted/OOD effects, extract a
named circuit above matched controls, permit selective removal with bounded unrelated-circuit collateral, and show
predictable composition/reuse. Those consequence tests are deliberately downstream of this feasibility screen.
