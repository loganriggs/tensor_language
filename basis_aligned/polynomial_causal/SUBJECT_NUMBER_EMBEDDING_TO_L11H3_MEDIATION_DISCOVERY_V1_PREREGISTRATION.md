# Subject-number embedding-to-L11H3 mediation discovery V1

Registered after opening the embedding-coordinate removal result. This is an
opened-panel path-discovery assay, not a fresh OOD confirmation. No L11 head
response or rescue outcome from these rows has been inspected.

Replay the frozen same-norm embedding-coordinate removal on its 64-row authority.
At layer 11, capture head 3's 128-dimensional pre-output-projection vector at the
subject token. Let `d_h` be the exact change needed to restore that head vector
to its native value and let `d_o` be `d_h` passed through the frozen head-3 slice
of the attention output matrix.

Run four kinds of downstream arms from the removed embedding state:

1. no rescue;
2. add the full `d_o` at L11 attention output as a mediation ceiling;
3. add only the projection of `d_o` onto the already-frozen rank-one L11H3 writer
   axis; and
4. 16 random-axis additions matched row-by-row to the rank-one rescue norm.

No answer label, number label, behavioral outcome, or fitted coefficient enters
the rescue. The native head vector is an explicit donor port in this discovery
assay and must be counted as such.

Instrumentation requires exact checkpoint/artifact hashes, parent native margins
and removal damages replayed within `1e-5`, repeated removed head captures within
`1e-5`, finite tensors, and exactly 20 forwards over 1,280 sequences.

Predictions fixed before execution:

1. Full-head rescue is live: its effect has cosine at least `.50` with the
   embedding-removal damage, least-squares recovery at least `.10`, and positive
   sign on at least `.75` of rows.
2. Rank-one rescue retains the full-head mediation: cosine with the full-head
   rescue at least `.80`, relative L2 error at most `.50`, norm ratio in
   `[.50, 1.50]`, and least-squares recovery of the original damage at least `.10`.
3. Rank-one rescue has lower relative error to the removal damage than all 16
   matched random-axis rescues and effect RMS at least twice their median.

Failure of the full-head ceiling localizes the main embedding-number path outside
L11H3. A live ceiling but failed rank-one arm means the frozen writer is a useful
external intervention but not the natural mediator of this input coordinate.
Passing licenses a new-vocabulary confirmation; it does not itself establish OOD
mediation because the authority is already open.
