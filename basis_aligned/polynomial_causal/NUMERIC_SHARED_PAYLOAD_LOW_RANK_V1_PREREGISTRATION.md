# Shared numeric H3+H7 cached-payload low-rank V1

Existing exact experiments establish that the final numeric source's L8H3+H7 cached term transfers numbered-list successor, nonadjacent numeric +1, and digit/word copy. They do not establish the dimensionality of that 256D two-head interaction. This experiment freezes a cross-authority compression test before inspecting any projected intervention.

FIT uses all 16 capable spaced numbered-list pairs in both directions. For each directed pair, form the exact cached-only two-head delta by subtracting the recipient H3+H7 complete output from the exact cached replacement, flattening the two 128D heads to 256D. Stack the 32 deltas without centering and freeze right-singular-vector bases at ranks 1, 2, 4, and 8. No numeric-sequence or copy delta, logit, or label enters basis fitting.

Evaluate projected cached deltas on FIT and choose the smallest rank that recovers at least .80 of the exact cached intervention's mean donor-margin effect and donor CE gain in every construction × direction cell, with donorward fraction at least .75. If no rank passes, stop.

TEST freezes the selected basis and evaluates it without refitting on all capable nonadjacent +1 pairs and all spaced digit/word-copy pairs, both directions. Report construction × representation × direction cells. A cell passes at .80 recovery for margin and CE and .75 donorward fraction. `pred_c_cross_authority_shared_payload` requires all +1 and copy cells; `pred_d_plus_one_only` and `pred_e_copy_only` register asymmetric transfer; `pred_f_cross_authority_null` records that neither family passes. Rank-one and rank-two outcomes are separately registered.

Native replay, head-source sums, cached decomposition, and installed tensor error retain the exact parent bars (`1e-10`, `1e-10`, `1e-10`, and `1e-5`). Passing supports a low-rank shared numeric value payload, not a +1 relation/router or downstream action consumer. Maximum price: six forwards, 736 sequences, zero backwards, zero updates, one FIT SVD.
