## Circuit interpretation targets

A useful circuit must specify what is read, computed, and written; group semantic variables across
native modules while splitting modules where required; predict held-out and OOD causal effects;
provide an executable sufficiency interface; support selective removal, swapping, and editing;
compose and reuse shared subcomputations; and remain stable across splits, gauges, and restarts.
The program goal remains a predictive, composable, selectively manipulable tensor program that is
also simpler under literal storage, compute, edge, state, and program price. Compression alone is
not identification.

## Confounds and path decision

- Absolute rather than additive clamping, aligned rows, early and full parent replay, factor closure,
  and exact price control baseline subtraction and frame mixing.
- A hard target feasibility gate prevents the complement-only dead-knob solution.
- A2/C were opened after selection, so their failure is not post-selection leakage.
- Gaussian noise and a local Jacobian penalty are only local smoothness constraints; they add no
  construction environments and cannot by themselves identify an invariant causal variable.
- Fold stability rules out simple restart instability, but it does not imply construction stability.
- The one-direction P failure shows that averages can hide asymmetric collateral.

The highest-information next route is to add complete construction environments, not widen rank,
step, or regularization grids. A new cue bank must first pass a capability-only gate; if it does,
A1 and A2 become observed environments and the new construction remains sealed for prediction.
Only after a construction-stable response subspace exists should its coordinates be translated
through OV and downstream Q/K/V/MLP weight tensors as a candidate shared writer/reader variable.

Alternatives are ranked:

1. **New construction capability plus multi-environment response fitting.** Changes held-out/OOD
   prediction and stable identification. Kill it if no text-disjoint cue construction has adequate
   native is/was capability, or if a projector trained across old complete constructions still fails
   the new one.
2. **Input-conditional/nonlinear response operator.** Changes within-module splitting, composition,
   and selective manipulation. Promote it if fixed projectors remain construction-specific after
   multiple observed environments; kill a proposed operator if its routing cannot itself transfer.
3. **Weight-tensor writer/reader translation.** Changes computational specification and extraction.
   Use now only as a diagnostic; promote it when a causally selective subspace survives a sealed
   construction.
4. **Further coefficient/rank sweeps.** Demoted because they change target fit or capacity without
   adding identifying observations.
