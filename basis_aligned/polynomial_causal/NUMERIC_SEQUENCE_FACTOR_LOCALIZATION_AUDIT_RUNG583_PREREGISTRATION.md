## Null preservation and knowledge packet

An R577 scientific null remains a scientific null even when some target effects are large. R583 reports, for every
site, how many of the six target-direction cells and two relation-direction cells transferred, and names every active
control cell and threshold that failed. This becomes a reusable circuit-agent packet: broad final-query state swaps
that transfer sequence behavior but also move surface/copy/`+2` behavior should not be repeated as candidate
decompositions. The next experiment must split or condition the transported state, or test its downstream readers,
rather than rerun a larger undifferentiated state swap.
