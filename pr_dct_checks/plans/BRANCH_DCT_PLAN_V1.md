# Replication of AJ's branch-specific ("bilinear quadratic") DCT, with its closed form

23 September 2026, 02:15 UTC. Claude (Fable). Registered before running. Logan asked (23 Sep) to replicate the new variant in `sparse-asymmetric/bilinear_quadratic_experiment.py` (commit 9d3a787 → 6a62171), which AJ reports fixes the factor collapse: instead of adding l + r to the residual, add l to the input of the source MLP's Left projection and r to the input of its Right projection, take the ordered cross-derivative ∂²(u·Δ)/∂α∂β, and fit (u, l, r) with the same fixed-point loop and no PR penalty. His results file (`bilinear_quadratic_results.json`, softmax-attention bilinear model, blocks 8 → 12, 32 train / 64 held-out AdvBench prompts, 64 WikiText-2 OOD paragraphs, 8 factors, 30 iterations, seeds 0 1 2) reports: held-out energy ≈ train, WikiText retention 0.82, swapped/direct energy 0.01–0.04, joint alignment of (l, r) with one source unit's (Left row, Right row) 0.73–0.99, top-1 source-hidden energy fraction 0.71–0.998, and cross-seed matches above 0.8: 0, 2, 2 of 8.

**What the variant computes (derivation to be checked in-run).** With m(α, β) = D[(L(n+αl)) ⊙ (R(n+βr))] + bias the source MLP output and G the downstream map, ∂²(u·G∘m)/∂α∂β at 0 = u·J_G D[(Ll) ⊙ (Rr)] + (∂m/∂α)ᵀ H_{G,u} (∂m/∂β). The first term is Σ_h w_h (a_h·l)(b_h·r) with w = Dᵀ J_Gᵀ u ∈ ℝ⁴⁶⁰⁸ (a_h, b_h the unit's Left/Right rows): a bilinear form in (l, r) with matrix M_u = Aᵀ diag(w) B, generally asymmetric. If the second (downstream-curvature) term is small, the fixed-point fit for a given u converges to the top singular pair of M_u, and when w has a dominant entry that pair is (a_h, b_h) of one unit — the neuron alignment AJ observes. The method would then be a Jacobian-weighted ranking of source-block neurons, and its "circuit" content the pair (u, h).

**Runs.** (1) Faithful replication on AJ's model and settings (softmax bilinear, 8 → 12, AdvBench, WikiText-2 OOD, plus FineWeb OOD), with the fit ported into `circuit_checks` (branch perturbation added to the adapter). (2) The same on bilin18 (squared attention), Logan's model. Both report AJ's metrics and the closed-form analysis: per factor, w̄ (mean of Dᵀ J_cᵀ u over the 32 training contexts), the rank of the fitted unit in the ordering |w̄_h|·‖a_h‖·‖b_h‖, the cosines of the fitted (l, r) with the top singular pair of M_u, the singular-value participation ratio of M_u, and the share of the score carried by the curvature term (score − term 1) per context.

**Controls.** (a) With θ = 0 the branch span reproduces the plain span exactly. (b) With every downstream MLP and attention block frozen, G is linear and the ordered score equals u·G(D[(Ll)⊙(Rr)]) computed by one jvp, to ≤ 1e-4 relative, for random (l, r). (c) AJ's random-direction swap check (direct vs swapped response differ, relative difference ~1.4). (d) CPU smoke test on a tiny random model of each architecture.
